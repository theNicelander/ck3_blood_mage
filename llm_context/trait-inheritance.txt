# Blood Mage Trait Inheritance Reference

This document outlines the trait inheritance architecture of the Blood Mages mod. The mod bypasses CK3's vanilla recessive/tiered genetic trait system in favor of explicit, deterministic trait inheritance chances, game-rule-driven birth prevalence filters, and supernatural lineage manipulation.

## Core Inheritance Mechanics

### Direct Lifestyle Trait Inheritance (`lifestyle_blood_mage`)

The Blood Mage trait is a lifestyle trait configured with explicit engine inheritance probabilities rather than CK3's recessive genetic system (`genetic = yes`):

- **Single Parent Inheritance**: When one parent has `lifestyle_blood_mage`, offspring roll against `inherit_chance` (25%).
- **Dual Parent Inheritance**: When both parents possess `lifestyle_blood_mage`, offspring inheritance is guaranteed via `both_parent_has_trait_inherit_chance` (100%).
- **Spontaneous Birth Manifestation**: Children born to non-mage parents have a baseline random chance (`birth = 0.25`, roughly 1 in 400) to spontaneously awaken the trait at birth.
- **Random Creation**: Eligible randomly generated characters have a baseline chance (`random_creation = 0.25`) to appear with the trait.
- **Potential Gate**: The trait definition gates potential behind `NOT = { has_game_rule = bm_prevalence_player_only }` for AI characters, preventing AI from generating with the trait when Player Only mode is active.

### Non-Hereditary Personal Traits (`lifestyle_crimson_empowerment`)

The secondary progression trait, `lifestyle_crimson_empowerment`, is strictly non-hereditary (`inherit_chance = 0`). It represents power accumulated through active ritual casting and can never be inherited by offspring.

## Birth Prevalence and AI Retention Auditing

Inheritance at birth triggers the on-action pipeline to evaluate active campaign game rules:

1. **On-Action Hook**: `on_birth_child` fires `bm_apply_blood_mage_prevalence_at_birth`.
2. **AI Retention Filtering**: Any AI child born with `lifestyle_blood_mage` (whether inherited or spontaneously manifested) is evaluated by `bm_apply_blood_mage_prevalence_retention_effect`:
   - **Player Only** (`bm_prevalence_player_only`): AI characters immediately lose the trait (`remove_trait = lifestyle_blood_mage`).
   - **Extremely Rare / Rare / Less Frequent**: AI characters roll against retention thresholds. Characters failing the check have the trait stripped immediately.
   - **Default**: AI retains the trait without additional restriction.
   - **Retention Initialization**: Characters who successfully retain the trait receive the `bm_blood_mage_prevalence_reviewed` character flag and initialize their story cycle via `bm_ensure_blood_mage_story_effect`.
3. **More Frequent Game Rule Acceleration**:
   - If `bm_prevalence_more_frequent` is enabled and an AI child does not inherit naturally:
     - If either parent is a Blood Mage (`scope:mother` or `scope:real_father`), the child has an additional 35% chance to receive `bm_become_blood_mage_effect = yes`.
     - If neither parent is a Blood Mage, the child has a 1% chance to receive `bm_become_blood_mage_effect = yes`.

## Lineage and Genetic Trait Manipulation

Blood mages possess supernatural abilities that actively enhance the inheritance of other positive traits across their lineage:

### Crimson Empowerment (`legacy` Track)
Advancing the `legacy` track of Crimson Empowerment grants escalating personal character modifiers that enhance reproductive inheritance in descendants:
- Increases `positive_random_genetic_chance` (+0.05 per tier up to tier 100).
- Increases `positive_inactive_inheritance_chance` (+0.05 per tier up to tier 100).

### Dynasty Lineage Modifiers (`bm_crimson_legacy_modifier`)
Through the dynasty channel interaction, a blood mage can bestow the Crimson Legacy house modifier onto their house:
- Grants `positive_inactive_inheritance_chance = 0.1` (+10% chance to inherit positive inactive traits).
- Grants `negative_inactive_inheritance_chance = -0.1` (-10% chance to inherit negative inactive traits).
- Augments health (+0.5) and fertility (+0.1).

### Trait Theft as Alternative to Inheritance
Rather than relying on selective breeding or generational inheritance, blood mages can bypass reproduction entirely:
- **Trait Drain Interaction** (`trait_drain_prisoner_event_interaction`): A blood mage challenges a prisoner in a supernatural duel to violently siphon positive congenital traits (intellect, physique, beauty) directly into themselves, stripping the trait from the prisoner.
