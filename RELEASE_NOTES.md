# Blood Mages — Major Update Release Notes (v1.20)

Comprehensive release notes detailing all net additions, overhauls, and gameplay features introduced since the 2025 baseline release (v1.15).

---

## ⚔️ The Twin Devotion Paths: Blood Mage & Blood Knight

- **Twin Endgame Lifestyle Classes**: Blood magic is now split into two permanent, mutually exclusive lifestyle archetypes, each with distinct playstyles and 5 progression tracks (2 unique specialization tracks and 3 shared Devotion tracks):
  - **Blood Mage** (*Shepherd of the Faithful*): The occult scholar and dynasty architect. Focuses on realm stability, domain development, dread manipulation, and dynasty expansion.
  - **Blood Knight** (*Fire and Sword*): A dedicated martial warlord and battlefield champion. Focuses on personal prowess, combat advantage, army movement speed, and knight empowerment.
- **Mutual Exclusivity & Awakening**: Characters choose their path upon awakening to blood magic—via hereditary bloodlines, occult mentorship, witch conversion, or by venturing north to duel the 100-year-old hermit **Egill Skallagrímsson** in Iceland or Tsushima.
- **Shared Devotion Progression (Ancient, Enlightenment, Benediction)**:
  - Both classes advance through 3 shared spiritual tracks scaling up to 100 XP.
  - **50 XP Milestones**: Dynamic scaling bonuses based on your Level of Devotion, including bonus knights, cultural acceptance, and diplomacy.
  - **100 XP Apex Capstones**: Unlock a massive **+50% Devotion Impact** multiplier (amplifying all Level of Devotion effects across your realm), universal immunity to aging prowess loss (**Age Prowess Lock**), and +2.0 skill points per Devotion level.
- **Dedicated Blood Knight Tracks**:
  - **Vanguard**: Boosts army movement speed, combat advantage, supply duration, and direct martial prowess.
  - **Slaughter**: Drastically increases personal prowess, knight limit, knight effectiveness, and bonus prowess scaling with Devotion.
- **Dedicated Blood Mage Tracks**:
  - **Bloodline**: Increases domain limit, county tax, development growth, and building construction speed.
  - **Hematurgy**: Unlocks hostile scheme power, scheme defense, dread gain, and natural tyranny decay.

## 🧬 Blood Empowerment & Somatic Ascendancy

- **Secondary Mastery Trait**: Blood Empowerment is granted alongside Blood Mage or Blood Knight, representing the physical and metaphysical refinement of the caster's body and mind.
- **Five Independent Specialization Tracks**:
  - **Dynasty (Sanguine Eugenics)**: Enhances fertility, reduces inbreeding risk, and boosts dynasty renown. Reaching 50 XP cuts inbreeding chance in half; reaching the 100 XP capstone grants **total inbreeding immunity (-100%)**, allowing the creation of pure bloodlines without genetic defects.
  - **Mastery (World-Walker)**: Accelerates lifestyle XP gain, adds personal scheme slots (+1 at 50 XP, +2 at 100 XP), increases cultural acceptance, and ignores negative culture opinion penalties at 100 XP.
  - **Presence (Mesmeric Allure)**: Boosts general opinion, stress loss, and prestige gain. Grants extra Diplomacy and Martial per Fame level, raises your maximum Fame level cap, and completely eliminates different-faith opinion penalties at 100 XP.
  - **Prosperity (War Chest & Logistics)**: Expands domain limit, boosts monthly gold per Devotion level, and increases Men-at-Arms regiment size (+1 at 50 XP, +2 at 100 XP) and regiment slots (+1 slot at 100 XP).
  - **Shadows (Occult Defense & Intrigue)**: Drastically reduces enemy hostile scheme success chance (-15% at 50 XP, -30% at 100 XP), unlocks an extra hostile scheme slot at 100 XP, and grants scaling Intrigue and Prowess.
- **Full Devotion Synergy**: Mastering all 5 empowerment tracks provides an additional cumulative **+25% Devotion Impact** (+5% per track capstone).
- **Inline Option-List Widget**: Channeling Blood Empowerment now features a dedicated in-game widget displaying each track's emblem, active bonuses, and next milestone.

## 🩸 The Three-Tier Lifeforce Economy

- **Three Potencies of Lifeforce**:
  - **Minor Lifeforce**: Common vital essence harvested from prisoners or minor rituals; provides passive health and disease resistance.
  - **Major Lifeforce**: Concentrated lifeblood used for powerful body modifications and high rituals; significantly extends lifespan.
  - **Superior Lifeforce**: The apex manifestation of life essence, required for creating immortal Blood Golems and carving legendary Superior Capstone runes.
- **Interactive Manifest Lifeforce**: Cast a scaling ritual via an interactive popup with four investment tiers (from 250 to 1,500 Piety), letting you condense life essence on demand without arbitrary cooldowns.
- **Battlefield Lifeforce Harvesting**: Blood Knights leading armies now directly reap Lifeforce from victorious battles, dynamically scaling with the scale of the confrontation (Skirmish, Battle, or Epic Clash).
- **Single-Combat Harvest**: Slaying rivals or duelists in lethal single combat allows Blood Knights to harvest their victim's life essence on the spot.
- **Childbirth Blessing**: Blood Mage and Blood Knight mothers channel supernatural protection during childbirth, yielding Major Lifeforce upon safe delivery.

## 📜 Grimoires, Spellcasting & Decision Widgets

- **Consolidated Grimoires**: Replaced scattered individual spell decisions with organized Grimoire menus:
  - **Minor Blood Magic**: Fast utility rituals including Lifeforce Attunement, Minor Blood Runes, Vitality Cleansing, and Lifeforce Condensation.
  - **Major Blood Magic**: Powerful rituals including Blood Empowerment, Education Enhancement (Tiers 1★–4★), Major Blood Runes, Congenital Trait Manifestation, and House Blessings.
  - **Superior Blood Magic**: Legendary rites including Blood Golem Creation, 5★ Master Educations, Secondary Educations, and Superior Capstone Runes.
- **Inline Option-List Widgets**: Integrated CK3's modern option-list interface for key rituals—letting you preview bonuses, track icons, costs, and requirements in a clean window without multi-step event spam:
  - *Attune Lifeforce*: Select your yearly lifestyle XP bonus.
  - *Carve Galdrastafir*: Choose and preview your flesh-carved magical stave.
  - *Channel Blood Empowerment*: Choose and invest in your somatic tracks.
- **37-Condition Healing & Situation Alerts**:
  - Cure up to 37 physical and mental ailments on yourself, your courtiers, family, or prisoners—ranging from illnesses and wounds to blindness, severed limbs, and melancholy.
  - A persistent **"You Can Heal Someone"** notification appears in the Current Situation dropdown whenever an eligible courtier or family member falls sick or injured.
- **Ex-Nihilo Congenital Manifestation**: Manifest or improve genetic beauty, intellect, and physique traits through ritual blood channeling, supporting progression up to Rank 5.
- **Personality Reshaping**: Purge negative or conflicting personality traits from courtiers and imprint occult virtues through ritual blood manipulation.
- **Master & Secondary Educations**: Elevate education traits to mythical 5★ rank, or study under an ancient blood mentor to acquire an entirely new secondary education trait.

## ᚱ Icelandic Galdrastafir Flesh-Carved Blood Runes

- **Five Historical Galdrastafir Staves**: Carve authentic Icelandic magical staves directly into your character's flesh, each granting a passive ambient Lifeforce pulse and unique thematic bonuses:
  - **Vegvísir** (*The Wayfinder*): Travel safety, movement speed, supply capacity, and battlefield retreat defense.
  - **Ægishjálmur** (*Helm of Awe*): Terror, dread, knight effectiveness, and bonus Lifeforce from battlefield kills.
  - **Feingur** (*The Fertility Stave*): Fertility, pregnancy chance, health, and dynasty-wide blessing rites.
  - **Nábrókarstafur** (*Necropants / Wealth Stave*): Direct domain tax bonuses, building gold cost reductions, and monthly gold income.
  - **Hulinhjálmur** (*Helm of Disguise*): Hostile scheme success, scheme secrecy, and personal plot defense.
- **Superior Stave Capstones**: Reach the pinnacle of rune carving to unlock game-changing capstone powers (such as guaranteed double lifeforce pulses or complete scheme invisibility).
- **Rune Inscription on Followers**: Inscribe blood runes onto your sworn knights, champions, and family members to bolster their combat prowess and loyalty.
- **Custom DDS Icons**: Every Galdrastafir stave features authentic, hand-crafted 120×120 visual iconography across modifiers, decisions, and menus.
- **Blood Universities**: Construct Blood University holding improvements (Tiers 0–3) in your realm's duchy capital to train apprentices and accelerate rune research.

## 🛠️ Bugfixes & Technical Improvements

- **Fixed Devotion Capstone Scaling**: Replaced non-functional vanilla max piety level cap increases (which are hard-clamped to level 5 in vanilla CK3 governments) with clean, universally functioning **+50% Devotion Impact** and **+2.0 stat-per-devotion** scaling.
- **Fixed Event Portrait Staging**: Resolved CK3 1.20 rendering errors in Seek Power events by placing 3D portraits prior to event option evaluations, ensuring wandering mages and mentors render properly.
- **Fixed Character Naming & Wanderer Scopes**: Corrected portrait triggers and localized names for wilderness encounter characters.
- **Fixed Geyser Duel Scopes**: Corrected unhandled variables and scope evaluation errors during lethal initiation duels.
- **Eliminated Double Piety Charges**: Fixed character interactions that inadvertently deducted piety twice upon execution.
- **Restored Missing Cooldown Tooltips**: Added clear in-game cooldown timers and requirements across Education Enhancement, Blood Golems, Stave Carving, and Major Rites.
- **Vanilla 1.20 Ailment Support**: Expanded healing effects to support all ailments introduced in recent base-game updates (withering mind, clouded eyes, fragile bones, faltering heart, and lunatic stages).
- **Modernized Trait Inheritance**: Overhauled hereditary transmission to use CK3's modern non-genetic inheritance pipeline, preventing trait degradation and engine warnings.
- **Scope & Null-Safety Hardening**: Added existence guards across all duel triggers, courtier lists, and AI targeting logic to prevent silent script errors.
