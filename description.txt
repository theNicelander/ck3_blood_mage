[h1]Blood Mages (by Nicelander)[/h1]

Low-fantasy blood magic for Crusader Kings III (1.20.*).

Drain [b]Lifeforce[/b] to extend lifespan, fuel rituals, steal congenital traits, forge loyal golems, and empower sworn blood knights.

Modular architecture. [b]Zero vanilla file overwrites[/b]. Mid-save compatible. Full compatibility with total overhauls: [i]A Game of Thrones (AGOT)[/i], [i]Realms in Exile (LotR)[/i], [i]Elder Kings 2 (EK2)[/i], and [i]Princes of Darkness (PoD)[/i].

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Highlights[/h2]
[list]
    [*] [b]Long Life, Not True Immortality[/b]: Live centuries, but stay mortal. Wounds, disease, murder, and exhaustion still kill.
    [*] [b]Earned Genetic Power[/b]: Acquire or upgrade Genius, Beautiful, and Herculean through Lifeforce, rituals, and sacrifice.
    [*] [b]Deep Progression[/b]: 3 lifestyle traits across 13 mastery tracks (10–100 XP).
    [*] [b]AI Parity[/b]: AI uses all mechanics, balanced by game rules.
    [*] [b]Zero Vanilla Overwrites[/b]: Drops cleanly into any load order.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]The Lifeforce Resource[/h2]
Stored as character modifiers across three tiers:
[list]
    [*] [b]Minor Lifeforce[/b]: Minor heals, attribute channeling, knight empowerment.
    [*] [b]Major Lifeforce[/b]: Major rituals, education rank 1–4, bloodline empowerment, trait manifestation.
    [*] [b]Superior Lifeforce[/b]: Distilled essence for 5-star education, superior body runes, and blood golems.
[/list]

[h3]Sources[/h3]
[list]
    [*] [b]Prisoner & Courtier Drain[/b]: Siphon captives or courtiers individually.
    [*] [b]Mass Lifedrain[/b]: Drain entire dungeons in one action.
    [*] [b]Combat & Single Combat[/b]: Harvest essence from battlefield victories and fatal duels.
    [*] [b]Spiritual Manifestation[/b]: Meditate via Learning duels to manifest or distill tiers.
    [*] [b]Seek Power[/b]: Venture into the wilderness (landed or landless adventurer) for beasts and fonts.
    [*] [b]Inscribed Body Runes[/b]: Passive annual Lifeforce roll from flesh runes.
[/list]

[h3]Mortality & Magic Toll[/h3]
[b]Blood Mages are not immortal.[/b] Spells cost Lifeforce and cause temporary exhaustion backlash. Empty reserves drop health, leaving mages vulnerable to illness, schemes, and battlefield death.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Three Lifestyle Traits & 13 Mastery Tracks[/h2]
Tracks scale from 10 to 100 XP with milestones at level 50 and 100.

[h3]1. Blood Mage (lifestyle_blood_mage)[/h3]
Base trait. Grants +2 Learning per Piety level and unlocks the Blood Magic Situation panel.
[list]
    [*] [b]Ancient[/b]: Piety gain and old-age Prowess retention.
    [*] [b]Enlightenment[/b]: Lifestyle XP gain, Martial, Prowess.
    [*] [b]Bloodline[/b]: Monthly dynasty prestige, Stewardship, house blood modifiers.
    [*] [b]Benediction[/b]: Diplomacy, prestige, general and vassal opinion.
    [*] [b]Hematurgy[/b]: Intrigue, hostile scheme resistance, trait theft proficiency.
[/list]

[h3]2. Blood Empowerment (lifestyle_blood_empowerment)[/h3]
Unlocked via Major Blood Magic. Every track level grants +0.1 Health, +2 Life Expectancy, +1 Year Fertility, and +1 Epidemic Resistance:
[list]
    [*] [b]Dynasty[/b]: Congenital trait inheritance chance and fertility.
    [*] [b]Mastery[/b]: Lifestyle XP multiplier, language scheme speed and capacity.
    [*] [b]Presence[/b]: Stress loss multiplier, opinion, sway schemes.
    [*] [b]Prosperity[/b]: Domain tax, construction discounts, Men-at-Arms maintenance.
    [*] [b]Shadows[/b]: Scheme secrecy, hostile scheme resistance, enemy plot disruption.
[/list]

[h3]3. Blood Knight (lifestyle_blood_knight)[/h3]
Martial identity for champions and commanders. Leveled via battles, duels, tournaments, and minor magic. Can cast minor magic, seek power, and heal minor ailments without full Blood Mage initiation.
[list]
    [*] [b]Vanguard[/b]: Advantage, Martial, knight effectiveness.
    [*] [b]Slaughter[/b]: Prowess, dread gain, duel lethality.
    [*] [b]Resilience[/b]: Health recovery, wound healing, fertility, epidemic resistance.
[/list]

[h2]Spells & Ritual Hubs[/h2]

[h3]Minor Blood Magic[/h3]
[list]
    [*] [b]Channel Lifeforce[/b]: 5-year buff to any primary attribute or Prowess.
    [*] [b]Attunement[/b]: Specialize aura for annual bonuses (Ancient, Vanguard, Slaughter, Resilience).
    [*] [b]Minor Restoration[/b]: Cure wounds, scars, gout, and early illnesses on self or courtiers.
[/list]

[h3]Major Blood Magic[/h3]
[list]
    [*] [b]Blood Empowerment[/b]: Progress the 5 empowerment tracks.
    [*] [b]Enhance Education (1★ → 4★)[/b]: Upgrade education rank via mental trials.
    [*] [b]Inscribe Minor/Major Runes[/b]: Carve body runes for annual Lifeforce rolls.
    [*] [b]Empower Bloodline[/b]: Lasting house-wide dynasty modifiers.
    [*] [b]Manifest Perfection (Ranks 1–3)[/b]: Awaken Quick, Comely, or Hale without donors.
[/list]

[h3]Superior Blood Magic[/h3]
[list]
    [*] [b]Master Education (5★)[/b]: Attain 5-star education traits.
    [*] [b]Secondary Education[/b]: Acquire a complete second education trait.
    [*] [b]Inscribe Superior Rune[/b]: Top-tier body rune for annual Lifeforce rolls.
    [*] [b]Manifest Transcendent Perfection (Ranks 4–5)[/b]: Awaken Genius, Beautiful, or Herculean.
    [*] [b]Forge Blood Golem[/b]: Construct loyal golem champions.
[/list]

[h3]Trait Harvesting & Cleansing[/h3]
[list]
    [*] [b]Harvest Congenital Traits[/b]: Steal Genius, Beautiful, Herculean, Giant, and Fecund from prisoners.
    [*] [b]Purge Genetic Flaws[/b]: Cleanse Dull, Ugly, and Delicate by draining afflicted captives.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Blood Golems & Retinue[/h2]
[list]
    [*] [b]Blood Golems[/b]: Constructs forged from Superior Lifeforce and piety (bm_house_golem). Completely loyal, sterile, no title inheritance.
    [*] [b]Shaping Duels[/b]: Spar with golems to grant Berserker, Blademaster, Athletic, and education traits.
    [*] [b]Blood Knight Retinue[/b]: Swear courtiers or knights into the order as elite commanders.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Shrines & Universities[/h2]
[list]
    [*] [b]Domicile Blood Shrines (Tiers I–V)[/b]: Built in Adventurer Camps (camp) and Noble Estates (estate). Grants piety, health, lifespan, genetic inheritance bonuses, and courtier protection aura (bm_blood_shrine_aura).
    [*] [b]Blood Universities (Tiers 0–3)[/b]: Built in Duchy Capitals. Accelerates development and cultural fascination.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Blóðtrú Religion Family (rf_blodtru)[/h2]
Three regional faiths:
[list]
    [*] [b]Blóðtrú (blodtru_faith)[/b]: Europe/Atlantic focus. Centers on Iceland (Reykjavik, Tálknafjörður) and European power centers.
    [*] [b]Ketsudō (ketsudo_faith)[/b]: East Asia focus for Japonic, Korean, and Mongolic cultures (Mount Fuji, Yamashiro, Gyeongju).
    [*] [b]Xuédào (xuedao_faith)[/b]: China/Himalayas focus for Chinese, Qiangic, and Tibetan cultures (Chang'an, Luoyang, Taishan).
[/list]

[h3]Religion Mechanics[/h3]
[list]
    [*] [b]50 Holy Sites[/b]: Across Europe and Asia, with holder-specific buffs to prevent stat bloat.
    [*] [b]Identity Doctrine (bm_blodtru_identity_doctrine)[/b]: Built-in syncretism, high foreign tolerance, and opinion buffers. Peaceful coexistence without altering vanilla faiths.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Situation Panel[/h2]
Dedicated in-game UI hub:
[list]
    [*] Real-time tracker for disciplines, attunement, and body runes.
    [*] Stack counters for Minor, Major, and Superior Lifeforce.
    [*] Collapsible rosters: Dynasty Blood Mages, active Golems, sworn Blood Retinue.
    [*] Direct buttons for mass drain and situation decisions.
[/list]

[h2]Getting Started[/h2]
Become a Blood Mage or Blood Knight:
[list]
    [*] [b]Ruler Designer[/b]: Pick either trait directly.
    [*] [b]Egill Duel[/b]: Travel to Reykjavik or Tsushima. Fight Egill Skallagrímsson in a Learning duel (Blood Mage, costs Devotion) or Prowess duel (Blood Knight, costs Fame).
    [*] [b]Blood Cultist Decision[/b]: Adopt a Blóðtrú faith and take the transformation rite.
    [*] [b]Learn from Another[/b]: Request initiation from a Blood Mage friend, lover, or soulmate.
    [*] [b]Convert Witchcraft[/b]: Abandon coven to convert witchcraft into blood magic.
    [*] [b]Siphon Prisoner[/b]: Drain an imprisoned Blood Mage to take their power.
    [*] [b]Hereditary Birth[/b]: Inherited by blood mage offspring based on game rule frequency.
[/list]
