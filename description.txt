[h1]Overview[/h1]
Blood Mages drain [b]Lifeforce[/b] from others to extend their own lifespan and cast powerful spells. This power can be used to heal, enhance abilities, absorb traits, and more, at the cost of their own health.

[h3]Therefore, this mod is for you if:[/h3]
[list]
    [*] You love playing a [b]single character[/b]
    [*] You want your character to [b]live a long time[/b], without being completely immortal
    [*] You want your character to gain and level up [b]positive physical traits[/b] (intelligent, beautiful, etc) in a way that feels earned and part of the game
    [*] You want the AI to have the same abilities as you do
    [*] You like mods that don't overwrite any base game files, so are [b]maximum compatible[/b] with other mods, even overhaul mods like AGOT / EK2 / PoD / LOTR
[/list]

[h1]Lifeforce System[/h1]
[b]Lifeforce[/b] is the fundamental resource for Blood Mages, primarily gained through draining other characters, such as prisoners or courtiers. Other ways do exist, but you'll have to discover those.

Using magic consumes Lifeforce and grants temporary exhaustion.

This means [b]Blood Mages are NOT immortal[/b]. They can still be assassinated, killed in combat or by illness if their Lifeforce runs out.

Managing [b]Lifeforce[/b] therefore requires a careful balance between health and longevity versus short term power gains.

[h3]Lifeforce comes in three levels[/h3]
All tiers provide increased lifespan, fertility, disease resistance, health and prowess:
[list]
    [*] [b]Minor Lifeforce[/b]: Smaller bonuses, used for minor magic, attribute channeling, and knight empowerment
    [*] [b]Major Lifeforce[/b]: Significant bonuses, required for powerful rituals, education upgrades, and manifesting traits
    [*] [b]Superior Lifeforce[/b]: Distilled essence, required for 5-star master education, superior body runes, and blood golems
[/list]

[h3]Ways to gain Lifeforce[/h3]
[list]
    [*] Drain prisoners or courtiers individually, or mass-drain entire dungeons at once
    [*] Harvest essence from battlefield victories and fatal single combat duels
    [*] Meditate via Learning duels to manifest or distill higher tiers
    [*] Venture into the wilderness to seek fonts of power
    [*] Inscribe runes onto your body to passively generate Lifeforce over time
[/list]

[h1]Lifestyle Traits and Specialisations[/h1]
There are two main paths to walk, each with progressive tiers of power that take decades and thousands of piety to master:

[h3]Blood Mage[/h3]
The mystic and scholarly path. Features five distinct tracks that gain experience as you use related abilities:
[list]
    [*] [b]Ancient[/b]: Age slower, gain piety, and retain prowess in your old age
    [*] [b]Enlightenment[/b]: Gain XP by casting blood magic on yourself, boosting learning and lifestyle XP
    [*] [b]Bloodline[/b]: Enhance your dynasty, domain limit, and realm development
    [*] [b]Benediction[/b]: Gain XP by healing ailments and curing others, earning the love of your vassals
    [*] [b]Hematurgy[/b]: Intrigue, dread, hostile schemes, and harvesting traits from victims
[/list]

[h3]Blood Knight[/h3]
The martial path for commanders and battlefield champions. Shares the Ancient, Enlightenment, and Benediction tracks, plus two combat specialisations:
[list]
    [*] [b]Vanguard[/b]: Battlefield advantage, martial prowess, and knight effectiveness
    [*] [b]Slaughter[/b]: Single combat lethality, duel harvesting, and dread
[/list]
Blood Knights can cast Minor and Major blood magic, and can even attempt the dangerous ascension trial to become a full Blood Mage!

[h3]Blood Power[/h3]
Unlocked through Major Blood Magic as a secondary lifestyle trait. Every level grants flat monthly Lifestyle XP across six tracks:
[list]
    [*] [b]Dynasty[/b]: Better congenital trait inheritance, fertility, and flat active lifestyle XP
    [*] [b]Fury[/b]: Combat advantage, siege speed, knight capacity, and flat Martial lifestyle XP
    [*] [b]Mastery[/b]: Faster lifestyle XP gain, personal schemes, and flat Learning lifestyle XP
    [*] [b]Presence[/b]: General opinion, stress relief, and flat Diplomacy lifestyle XP
    [*] [b]Prosperity[/b]: Monthly gold income, economic growth, and flat Stewardship lifestyle XP
    [*] [b]Shadows[/b]: Scheme secrecy, warding against plots, and flat Intrigue lifestyle XP
[/list]

[h1]Blood Magic Abilities[/h1]
Blood Mages can use their Lifeforce to perform all sorts of rituals:
[list]
    [*] [b]Heal ailments[/b]: Cure wounds, scars, illnesses, and severe afflictions (blindness, infirmity) on yourself or others
    [*] [b]Steal congenital traits[/b]: Drain GOOD traits from prisoners (Genius, Beautiful, Herculean, Giant, Fecund)
    [*] [b]Purge flaws[/b]: Remove your own BAD congenital traits (Ugly, Slow, Delicate) by draining afflicted captives
    [*] [b]Manifest traits[/b]: Awaken congenital traits directly without donors, up to apex Genius and Herculean
    [*] [b]Improve education[/b]: Advance your education traits up to 5 stars, or master an entirely second education trait
    [*] [b]Inscribe flesh runes[/b]: Carve runes onto your body that passively generate Lifeforce
    [*] [b]Channel Lifeforce[/b]: Temporarily boost any primary attribute or prowess for 5 years
    [*] [b]Empower your bloodline[/b]: Grant permanent buffs to your entire dynasty
    [*] [b]Forge Blood Golems[/b]: Construct completely loyal champions from Superior Lifeforce, and spar with them to shape their combat traits
    [*] [b]Swear Blood Retinue[/b]: Induct elite courtiers and knights into your personal order
    [*] [b]Drain overhaul traits[/b]: Works with special traits from overhaul mods (such as Blood of Númenor from LotR)
[/list]

[h1]Blood Magic Situation Panel[/h1]
A dedicated in-game UI panel to manage your magical realm:
[list]
    [*] Track your Lifeforce stacks, flesh runes, and active attunements in real time
    [*] Collapsible rosters to easily view your Dynasty Blood Mages, active Golems, and sworn Blood Retinue
    [*] Keeps all blood magic decisions in one organized hub instead of cluttering your realm decisions menu
[/list]

[h1]The Blóðtrú Religion Family[/h1]
Blood Mages can follow unique blood faiths tailored to different regions of the world:
[list]
    [*] [b]Blóðtrú[/b]: Europe & Atlantic focus, centered around Iceland (Reykjavik)
    [*] [b]Ketsudō[/b]: East Asia focus for Japonic, Korean, and Mongolic cultures
    [*] [b]Xuédào[/b]: China & Himalayas focus
[/list]
[list]
    [*] [b]50 Holy Sites[/b]: Spread across Europe and Asia, granting small balanced bonuses
    [*] [b]Peaceful Coexistence[/b]: Built-in syncretism and tolerance means you can practice blood magic without being hated by your Christian, Muslim, or Eastern neighbours
    [*] [b]Blood Universities & Shrines[/b]: Build special duchy universities to accelerate magical study, or domicile blood shrines in adventurer camps and noble estates
[/list]

[h1]Getting Started[/h1]
There are various ways to gain the Blood Mage or Blood Knight trait:
[list]
    [*] Select the trait directly in the Ruler Designer
    [*] Travel to Reykjavik or Tsushima and duel the legendary Egill Skallagrímsson (Learning duel for Mage, Prowess duel for Knight)
    [*] Join a Blóðtrú faith and complete the transformation ritual
    [*] Ask a Blood Mage friend, lover, or soulmate to teach you
    [*] Convert from being a witch into a Blood Mage
    [*] Drain an imprisoned Blood Mage to take their power
    [*] Small chance characters are randomly born with it
[/list]

[h1]Game Rules[/h1]
The mod comes with flexible game rules to tailor the experience to your liking:
[list]
    [*] [b]Blood Mage & Knight Prevalence[/b]: Choose how common mages and knights are in the world (from Player Only to widespread)
    [*] [b]Starting Counties & Holders[/b]: Optionally give historical rulers or counties in Iceland and Tsushima blood faiths and traits from game start
    [*] [b]Physical Appearance[/b]: Choose your visual style (normal, blood-red eyes, or red eyes with white hair)
    [*] [b]Religion Settings[/b]: Control whether AI characters can adopt Blóðtrú faiths
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
The mod doesn't overwrite any base game files, so it is [b]maximum compatible[/b] with virtually all other mods. It's save game compatible, so you can safely add it to an existing campaign anytime.

Tested and working with major overhaul mods:
[list]
    [*] A Game of Thrones (AGOT)
    [*] Realms in Exile (LotR)
    [*] Elder Kings 2 (EK2)
    [*] Princes of Darkness (PoD)
[/list]
