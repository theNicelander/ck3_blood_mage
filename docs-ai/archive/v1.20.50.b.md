# v1.20.50.b: Consecrate Union Interaction for Spouses and Lovers

## Summary & Motivation
Introduces the **Consecrate Union** rite (`bm_consecrate_union_interaction`), enabling Blood Mages and Blood Knights to supernaturally consecrate their bond with themselves, spouses, consorts, lovers, and soulmates. By infusing Lifeforce into the union, the rite drastically improves fertility and reinforces the inheritance and manifestation of positive congenital traits across offspring.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Consecrated Union Modifier (`common/modifiers/bm_union_modifiers.txt`)**:
  - `bm_consecrated_union_modifier`:
    - `fertility = 0.50` (+50% Fertility)
    - `years_of_fertility = 10` (+10 Years of reproductive window)
    - `positive_random_genetic_chance = 0.30` (+30% Chance of spontaneous positive congenital traits)
    - `genetic_trait_strengthen_chance = 0.30` (+30% Chance to reinforce tiered congenital traits like Quick/Genius, Beautiful, Herculean)
- **Consecrate Union Interaction (`common/character_interactions/bm_union_interactions.txt`)**:
  - **Category**: `interaction_category_bm_blood_magic`.
  - **Actor**: `lifestyle_blood_mage` or `lifestyle_blood_knight`.
  - **Recipient**: Self, spouse, consort, lover, or soulmate lacking `bm_consecrated_union_modifier`.
  - **Cost**: `100` Piety.
  - **Modal Options (`send_option`)**:
    - `union_minor`: Consumes 1 Minor Lifeforce (`blood_magic_used_minor = yes`), applies `bm_consecrated_union_modifier` for 3 years, awards +2 `bloodline` XP (Mage) / +2 `vanguard` XP (Knight), and +20 opinion (`magic_opinion_positive`) if cast on partner.
    - `union_major`: Consumes 1 Major Lifeforce (`blood_magic_used_major = yes`), applies `bm_consecrated_union_modifier` for 10 years, awards +4 `bloodline` XP (Mage) / +4 `vanguard` XP (Knight), and +35 opinion (`magic_opinion_positive`) if cast on partner.
  - **Toasts**: All outcomes enclosed in `send_interface_toast` with `bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect = yes`.
  - **AI Logic**: Targeted (`ai_recipients = { self spouses scripted_relations }`), weighted toward spouses and soulmates, vetoed without Lifeforce.
- **Assets & Localization**:
  - Dedicated interaction icon: `gfx/interface/icons/character_interactions/bm_consecrate_union_interaction.dds`.
  - Concise English loc: `localization/english/bm_union_l_english.yml`.

## Key Decisions
- **Class Parity**: Available to both Blood Mages (advancing `bloodline`) and Blood Knights (advancing `vanguard`), giving martial commanders equal access to reproductive and genetic dynastic magic.
- **Self & Partner Versatility**: Can be cast directly on the practitioner or on any spouse, consort, or lover/soulmate, ensuring players in monogamous, polygamous, or concubinage marriages can bless their lineage effectively.
- **Tiered Duration & Impact**: Minor Lifeforce offers a 3-year burst suited for urgent conception windows, while Major Lifeforce secures a full decade of reproductive longevity and congenital strengthening.
