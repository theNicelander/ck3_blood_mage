# v1.20.50.d: Empower Blood Mage, Siphon Stored Lifeforce & Occult Mage Duels

## Summary & Motivation

Expands interactions between Blood Mages and fellow practitioners. Previously, Blood Knights had initiation and empowerment options while Blood Mages lacked symmetrical peer empowerment, and there were no ways to challenge fellow occultists to extract stored Lifeforce or contest mystical mastery. This update adds:
1. Symmetrical empowerment for Blood Mages (`empower_blood_mage_interaction`) mirroring Blood Knights.
2. Contested extraction of condensed Lifeforce reserves (`bm_siphon_lifeforce_reserves_interaction`).
3. High-stakes mystical duels between practitioners (`bm_occult_mage_duel_interaction`).
4. Toast wrapping with automatic Lifeforce recounting across initiation and empowerment interactions.

## Core Concepts & Changes

- **Empower Blood Mage (`empower_blood_mage_interaction`):**
  - Actor: `lifestyle_blood_mage`.
  - Recipient: Courtier, family, spouse, lover, friend, or dynasty member who has `lifestyle_blood_mage`.
  - Major Option: Consumes 1 Major Lifeforce (`blood_magic_used_major`), grants recipient +10 `enlightenment` and +10 `bloodline` XP (+30 opinion), actor gains +4 `benediction` XP.
  - Minor Option: Consumes 1 Minor Lifeforce (`blood_magic_used_minor`), grants recipient +5 `enlightenment` and +5 `bloodline` XP (+15 opinion), actor gains +2 `benediction` XP.
  - Toast: Wrapped inside `send_interface_toast` with `bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect`.
- **Siphon Stored Lifeforce (`bm_siphon_lifeforce_reserves_interaction`):**
  - Actor: Blood Mage or Blood Knight.
  - Recipient: Any living non-self character possessing `lifeforce_modifier_minor`, `lifeforce_modifier_major`, or `lifeforce_modifier_superior`.
  - Cost: 75 Piety. Cooldown: 1 year.
  - Duel: Auto-wins if recipient is imprisoned by actor. Otherwise, Blood Mage uses learning/intrigue duel, Blood Knight uses prowess duel.
  - Success: For each Lifeforce modifier tier present on recipient (`minor`, `major`, `superior`), removes 1 stack from recipient and adds 1 stack to actor. Awards +3 `hematurgy` XP (Mage) / +3 `slaughter` XP (Knight). Inflicts `lifedrained_me` (-50 opinion) unless actor possesses Superior Stave **Hulinhjálmur**.
  - Failure: Actor suffers 1-year `lifeforce_modifier_negative_minor` and +20 Stress; recipient gains `lifedrained_me` opinion (unless Hulinhjálmur).
- **Contest of the Red Art (`bm_occult_mage_duel_interaction`):**
  - Actor & Recipient: Living Blood Mages or Blood Knights (`NOT = { this = scope:actor }`). Recipient cannot be imprisoned.
  - Cost: 150 Piety + 1 Minor Lifeforce (`blood_magic_used_minor`). Cooldown: 3 years.
  - Duel: Contested duel using combined skills `learning` and `prowess`.
  - Critical Success: Actor condenses 1 Major Lifeforce and gains +5 `hematurgy`/`slaughter` XP. Recipient suffers `bm_blood_curse_modifier` (5 years) and `wounded_1`.
  - Success: Actor gains +3 `hematurgy`/`slaughter` XP. Recipient suffers 3-year `lifeforce_modifier_negative_major` and +35 Stress.
  - Defeat: Actor suffers 1-year `lifeforce_modifier_negative_minor`, +25 Stress, and gains +1 `enlightenment` XP.
- **Interface Toast Wrappers:**
  - `grant_blood_magic_interaction`, `make_blood_knight_interaction`, and `empower_blood_knight_interaction` now execute outcomes cleanly inside `send_interface_toast` with `bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect = yes`.

## Key Decisions

- **Symmetrical Peer Empowerment:** Added `empower_blood_mage_interaction` to mirror `empower_blood_knight_interaction`, ensuring Blood Mages can nurture apprentices, relatives, and court mages while gaining `benediction` experience.
- **Prisoner Auto-Win for Siphon:** An imprisoned practitioner cannot meaningfully resist a ritual extraction by their captor, bypassing duel rolls and guaranteeing the transfer of held Lifeforce reserves.
- **Duel Attribute Split:** Blood Mages siphon using Learning and Intrigue, while Blood Knights leverage raw Prowess, matching their class identity and track specializations.
