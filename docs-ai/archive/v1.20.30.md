# v1.20.30 - Blood Knight Stepping Stone, Exclusivity, Elevation & Minor Trait Cleansing

## Summary & Motivation
Positions Blood Knight as a stepping stone toward Blood Mage. Traits are mutually exclusive opposites. Blood Mages can bestow Blood Knight upon others but cannot become Blood Knights. Blood Knights can attempt a ritual decision to elevate themselves into Blood Mages, preserving accumulated `ancient` and `benediction` track progress. Minor magic and healing enable Blood Knights to heal themselves and others while purging bad congenital traits.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Mutual Exclusivity**: `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight` define each other in `opposites`. Characters cannot possess both traits simultaneously.
- **Grant & Elevation Hierarchy**:
  - `make_blood_knight_interaction`: Blood Mage bestows Blood Knight on a courtier/knight (cannot target self; Blood Mage cannot become Blood Knight).
  - `bm_elevate_to_blood_mage_decision`: Dedicated decision for Blood Knights to attempt ascension into Blood Mage via `bm_blood_knight_elevation.0001` trial (Learning or Prowess test).
  - Accrued track XP in `ancient` and `benediction` transfers loss-free via `bm_elevate_blood_knight_to_blood_mage_effect`.
- **Shared Trait Tracks**:
  - `lifestyle_blood_knight` gains identical `ancient` and `benediction` tracks alongside its martial tracks (`vanguard`, `slaughter`, `resilience`).
  - Yearly pulse grants +1 `ancient` XP and attunement rolls to Blood Knights.
- **Minor Healing & Bad Trait Cleansing**:
  - `heal_disease_minor`: Targeted interaction for Blood Mages and Blood Knights. Valid targets expanded to self, courtiers, liege, spouse, close family, and realm knights.
  - Cleanses minor wounds/illnesses AND removes negative bad traits: ugly (`beauty_bad`), opposites of genius (`intellect_bad`, `dull`), and opposites of herculean (`physique_bad`, `weak`).
  - Casting grants Blood Knights +2 `benediction` XP and +1 `resilience` XP.
  - `bm_cast_blood_magic_minor_decision` (`bm_cast_blood_magic_minor.001`): Added self-purge option to cleanse negative traits and minor ailments directly.
- **Geyser Duel Adjustment**:
  - Prowess duel option strictly blocked for existing Blood Mages.
  - Egill Skallagrímsson spawns with `lifestyle_blood_mage` only.

## Key Decisions
- **Stepping Stone Design**: Blood Knight serves as the martial gateway before transcendence to Blood Mage.
- **Mutual Exclusivity via Engine**: Used vanilla `opposites` blocks so engine strictly enforces one trait per character.
- **Lossless XP Transfer**: Script values read exact Knight track XP and apply it to Mage tracks upon elevation.
- **Minor Magic Scope**: Cleansing bad congenital traits is accessible via Minor magic rather than gated behind Major/Superior rites.
