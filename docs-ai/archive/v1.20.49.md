# v1.20.49: Go on a Blood Hunt Overhaul & Wilderness Expansion

## Summary & Motivation
Re-themes the wilderness expedition decision (`seek_power_decision`) and its event chain (`events/bm_seek_power_events.txt`) from a meditative stroll ("Seek Power in Nature's Domain") into a visceral, martial **Blood Hunt** ("Go on a Blood Hunt"), open to all **Blood Mages** and **Blood Knights**. Streamlines the encounter roll directly into the decision's execution with a 7-way Prowess + Learning contest, updates art and animations, differentiates the road encounter between classes, cleans up rewards, and adds 4 new thematic encounters.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Go on a Blood Hunt Decision (`common/decisions/get_lifeforce/bm_seek_power_decision.txt`)**:
  - Replaced religious art with hunting illustration `decision_activity.dds`.
  - Opened availability (`is_shown`) to both `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight`.
  - Shifted encounter roll directly into `effect = { duel = { skills = { prowess learning } value = 0 ... } }`, allowing players to inspect odds in the decision window and jump straight into the rolled encounter without an extra popup click.
  - Cooldown remains 2 years landed, 1 year unlanded/adventurer, halved by Superior **Vegvísir**.
- **Expanded 7-Encounter Pool (`events/bm_seek_power_events.txt`)**:
  - `seek_power.001`: Converted to hidden fallback dispatcher.
  - `seek_power.002` (**The Stag's Last Stand**): Run down and slaughter a royal stag for 1 Minor Lifeforce + 1 Slaughter (Knight) / 1 Hematurgy (Mage) XP.
  - `seek_power.008` (**The Beast's Lair**): Slay an apex predator in its den via Prowess duel for 1 Major Lifeforce, +25 Prestige, and +2 Slaughter/Hematurgy XP (or harvest carcasses for 1 Minor Lifeforce + 1 Ancient XP).
  - `seek_power.009` (**The Edge of the Woods**): Stalk an isolated homestead via Intrigue+Prowess duel for 1 Major Lifeforce and +2 Hematurgy/Slaughter XP (or extract an orderly blood tithe for 1 Minor Lifeforce + 1 Benediction XP).
  - `seek_power.003` (**Blades on the Lonely Road**): Road encounter with wandering warrior; sworn escort knight (`bm_escort_knight_character_template`) spawns exclusively for Blood Knights (Blood Mages encounter the traveler alone).
  - `seek_power.010` (**Blood in the Embers**): Ambush an outlaw/poacher camp via Prowess+Martial duel for 1 Major Lifeforce, +25 Gold, and +2 Slaughter/Hematurgy XP (or extort 1 Minor Lifeforce, +15 Gold, +5 Dread, +1 Vanguard/Bloodline XP).
  - `seek_power.011` (**The Crimson Rite**): Usurp a rival occultist's ritual via Prowess+Learning duel for 1 Major Lifeforce and +2 Slaughter/Hematurgy XP (or share the chalice for 1 Minor Lifeforce, +50 Piety, +2 Enlightenment XP).
  - `seek_power.004` (**The Cairn of Forgotten Carnage**): Ancient sacrificial cairn granting 1 Major Lifeforce + 2 Enlightenment XP + 1 Ancient XP for both classes without exhaustion penalties.
- **Visuals & Dynamic Stances**:
  - Replaced passive `idle` and `beg` portrait animations with hunting/martial poses (`hunting_knife_start`, `aggressive_sword`, `marshal`, `spymaster`).
- **Localization & Stave Integration**:
  - Rewrote `bm_seek_power_l_english.yml` with punchy, concise Blood Hunt text.
  - Updated **Vegvísir** descriptions in `bm_blood_runes_l_english.yml` and `bm_modifiers_l_english.yml` to reference "Blood Hunt".
  - Updated all architecture docs and master decision/event tables.

## Key Decisions
- **Unified Class Access**: Allowed both Blood Mages and Blood Knights to embark on Blood Hunts, giving Mages an active wilderness harvesting path while preserving class flavor via specialized options and XP gains.
- **Knight-Only Escort Champion**: Restricted the high-prowess sworn escort knight to Blood Knights, allowing them to engage in fatal Single Combat duels or prowess recruitments, while Blood Mages face the lone traveler to emphasize covert siphoning, trait theft, and learning persuasion.
- **Symmetric, Clean Rewards**: Removed the Minor Exhaustion debuff from the ancient cairn discovery and granted symmetric Lifeforce and XP gains to prevent unintuitive penalties during wilderness exploration.
- **Direct Decision Duel Resolution**: Embedded the 7-way duel into the decision effect so players immediately see encounter odds in the CK3 decision preview tooltip and bypass filler clicks.
