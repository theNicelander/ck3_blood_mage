# v1.20.27

## Summary & Motivation

Branch `v1.20.27` rebalances `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_empowerment` traits to prevent game-breaking modifier bloat while preserving high-fantasy power:
- Enforces a maximum combined `+30` General Opinion ceiling across traits, redistributing excess power into Vassal Opinion.
- Softens Blood Empowerment economic multipliers across the board to a maximum of `20%` (monthly income, Men-at-Arms maintenance, and domicile building costs).
- Tames Blood Empowerment `dynasty` congenital and inactive inheritance compounding from `50%` down to `25%`.
- Confirms and preserves the universal `years_of_fertility` baseline alongside high-fantasy lifespans due to CK3 engine constraints (`FEMALE_FERTILITY_CUTOFF_AGE = 45`).

## Core Concepts & Changes

- **General Opinion Cap & Vassal Opinion Redistribution**:
  - `common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt` (`benediction` track): `general_opinion` reduced from `2.5` to `1.5` per rank (`+15` at cap); added `vassal_opinion = 1` per rank (`+10` at cap).
  - `common/traits/bm_blood_empowerment_trait.txt` (`presence` track): `general_opinion` reduced from `2.5` to `1.5` per rank (`+15` at cap); added `vassal_opinion = 1` per rank (`+10` at cap).
  - Combined ceiling: Total General Opinion strictly bounded at `+30` max, with `+20` Vassal Opinion preserving social mastery.
- **Economic Multipliers Softening**:
  - `common/traits/bm_blood_empowerment_trait.txt` (`prosperity` track):
    - `monthly_income_mult` reduced from `0.03` to `0.02` per rank (`+20%` at cap).
    - `men_at_arms_maintenance` softened from `-0.03` to `-0.02` per rank (`-20%` at cap).
    - `domicile_building_cost_mult` softened from `-0.03` to `-0.02` per rank (`-20%` at cap).
    - `domain_tax_mult` and `holding_build_gold_cost` remain at `0.02` / `-0.02` (`20%` at cap).
- **Eugenics Compounding Adjustment**:
  - `common/traits/bm_blood_empowerment_trait.txt` (`dynasty` track):
    - `positive_random_genetic_chance` adjusted from `0.05` to `0.025` per rank (`+25%` at cap).
    - `positive_inactive_inheritance_chance` adjusted from `0.05` to `0.025` per rank (`+25%` at cap).
- **Documentation & Architecture Synchronization**:
  - `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-traits.md` and `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-blood-empowerment.md` updated to match live values.

## Key Decisions

- **Fertility Engine Boundary**: Verified that CK3 `life_expectancy` does not affect female fertility cutoff age (`FEMALE_FERTILITY_CUTOFF_AGE = 45`). Retained `years_of_fertility = 1` per rank to ensure fertile longevity for semi-immortal rulers.
- **Opinion Redistribution Target**: Vassal opinion chosen for excess social power over attraction or courtier opinion to provide meaningful realm stability without making all characters globally passive.
- **Prosperity Maintenance Cap**: Capped MaA maintenance reduction at `-20%` so army upkeep cannot be reduced to 0% purely in combination with basic stewardship perks.
