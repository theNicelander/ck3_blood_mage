# v1.20.38: Current Situation Healing Alert, Faith Decision Integration, and Description Polish

## Summary & Motivation
Add a prominent "You Can Heal Someone" warning in the Current Situation dropdown for Blood Mages and Blood Knights, integrate the Blood Cultist conversion decision into the Situation decisions roster, and polish mod descriptions and duel navigations.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Current Situation Healing Alert (`bm_action_can_heal`)**:
  - Displays grouped warning `@health_icon! You Can Heal Someone ($NUM$)` under Current Situation.
  - Automatically identifies sick or wounded characters within the player's immediate circle: self, spouse, children, lovers/soulmates, friends/best friends, liege, councillors, and knights.
  - Deduplicates candidates across overlapping relationships via temporary list.
  - Clicking any entry directly launches the `bm_heal_ailment` character interaction modal.
- **Healing Interaction Target Scope**:
  - Broadened `bm_heal_ailment` target validation to explicitly include friends, best friends, and councillors who may not reside in the player's direct court.
- **Blood Cultist Decision Integration**:
  - Added `become_blood_cultist_decision` to the Situation decisions roster in `bm_blood_mage_story.txt`.
  - Updated decision criteria to include Devotion rank >= 3 for both Blood Mages and Blood Knights.

## Key Decisions
- **Eager Notification**: The warning appears whenever an eligible character is sick or wounded, even if the player currently lacks sufficient Lifeforce or Piety, ensuring players are notified early to gather resources before conditions worsen.
- **Blood Knight Ailment Limits**: Blood Knights cannot heal deadly ailments (reserved for Blood Mages), so the notification trigger honors this limitation and only fires for minor or major ailments when the player is purely a Blood Knight.
