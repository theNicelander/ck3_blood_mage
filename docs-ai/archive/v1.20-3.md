# v1.20-3

## Summary & Motivation
This branch cherry-picks and integrates verified, non-faith fixes and gameplay enhancements from unmerged PR #94 into the v1.20 line. The goal is to bring in bug fixes (such as the double piety charge) and 1.19 compatibility updates (such as healing for 1.19 ailments) while deliberately excluding religion overhaul and faith-related changes.

## Core Concepts & High-Level Changes
- **Piety Deduction Fix**: Eliminated duplicate piety costs in minor self blood magic interactions (`bm_cast_blood_magic_self_minor.txt`). Interactions now rely solely on the interaction `cost` block rather than double-charging via `on_accept`.
- **CK3 1.19 Ailment Healing**: Extended Blood Mage healing interactions (`bm_cure_illness_interactions.txt`) to cover 1.19 age-related ailments:
  - `heal_disease_major` now clears `withering_mind`, `clouded_eyes`, and `fragile_bones` alongside `infirm`.
  - `heal_disease_major` references `lunatic_1` to align with 1.19 trait tiers.
  - `heal_disease_benediction` now clears `faltering_heart`.

- **Blood Mage Lifecycle Effects & Prevalence Rules**: Integrated `bm_blood_mage_lifecycle_effects.txt` and `bm_game_rules.txt` from PR #94:
  - Replaced all direct `add_trait = lifestyle_blood_mage` calls with `bm_become_blood_mage_effect = yes` across decisions and interactions (`bm_become_a_blood_mage.txt`, `bm_grant_blood_magic.txt`, `bm_become_blood_mage_decision.txt`, `bm_convert_from_witch.txt`, `bm_debug_decisions.txt`).
  - Added `bm_blood_mage_prevalence` and `bm_physical_alteration` game rules and their English localization.
  - Added `bm_blood_mage_prevalence_ai_acquisition_modifier` to scale AI willingness for becoming a Blood Mage according to the prevalence rule.
  - Added `potential` condition to `lifestyle_blood_mage` trait to block AI generation when the Player Only game rule is active.
  - Added on-actions to enforce prevalence retention and initialize story cycles on birth (`bm_blood_mage_prevalence_on_actions.txt`), yearly pulse (`bm_yearly_pulse.txt`), and game start (`bm_blood_mage_story_on_actions.txt`).
  - Added `bm_blood_mage_story` cycle definitions, values, texticons, and story list effects to back `bm_ensure_blood_mage_story_effect`.

## Key Decisions
- **Excluded Faith & Religion Overhauls**: We decided to ignore all faith, doctrine, and religion-family migrations from PR #94 (including `bm_initiation_faith_requirement` and `bm_lore` rules, and `bm_meets_blood_mage_initiation_faith_requirement_trigger` checks) because religion changes are out of scope for this branch and should remain isolated from core mechanics.
- **Preserved Mod Identity & Compatibility**: We decided not to take PR #94's changes to `descriptor.mod` (which altered Workshop ID and mod naming) or its trait modifications (removing `genetic` / `group`), keeping existing inheritance and third-party compatibility intact.
