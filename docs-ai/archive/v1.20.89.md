# v1.20.89: Open Transmute Lifeforce to Blood Acolytes & Add Raid Speed to Furious Blood

## Motivation

- `bm_transmute_lifeforce_decision` was already visible to all Blood Mage tiers (`is_blood_mage_any_tier_trigger`), and Blood Acolytes can obtain Major Lifeforce, yet the **Transmute Major Lifeforce** option in `bm_transmute_lifeforce.0001` remained gated behind `is_blood_mage_tier_2_or_higher_trigger = yes`.
- Furious Blood focuses on martial mobility and raiding warfare, making Raid Speed a natural fit alongside army movement and siege speed.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Transmute Lifeforce (`bm_transmute_lifeforce.0001`)**:
  - Changed the `bm_transmute_lifeforce.0001.major` option trigger from `is_blood_mage_tier_2_or_higher_trigger = yes` to `is_blood_mage_any_tier_trigger = yes` so Blood Acolytes (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`) and all higher tiers can transmute Major Lifeforce into specialized reservoirs.
- **Furious Blood (`lifeforce_modifier_furious`)**:
  - Added `raid_speed = 0.05` (`+5%` Raid Speed per stack).
- **Permanent Tiered Blood Blessings (`Blessing of X`)**:
  - Replaced the 3 temporary Bless Blood modifiers (`bm_blessed_blood_sovereign_modifier`, `bm_blessed_blood_iron_modifier`, `bm_blessed_blood_crucible_modifier`) and the redundant **Channel Minor Lifeforce** ritual (`bm_minor_channel`, `bm_channel_lifeforce_wisdom_minor.001`, and `*_lifeforce_modifier`s) with 18 permanent, tiered modifiers across the 6 Blood Mage tracks (**Radiance**, **Fortune**, **Fury**, **Cunning**, **Wisdom**, **Ancient**).
  - Attribute lines grant `+1/+3/+5` skill, `+5/+10/+15` matching monthly lifestyle XP, and `+5%/+10%/+20%` all-lifestyle XP gain; **Blessing of Ancient** grants `+5/+10/+15` all-lifestyle XP, `+5%/+10%/+20%` XP mult, `+5%/+15%/+25%` Fertility, and `+2/+4/+8` Years of Fertility.
  - Castable on self via `bm_self_blessing_decision` or `bm_blood_blessing_interaction`, and on children, dynasty kin, spouses/consorts, knights, councillors, or liege via `bm_blood_blessing_interaction` (`bm_blood_blessing.001`).
  - Minor/Major/Superior cost `50`/`150`/`250` Piety, Devotion `1`/`3`/`5`, and Minor/Major/Superior Lifeforce, with a shared 1-year per-target cooldown (`bm_blessing_cooldown`).
