# v1.20.24

## Summary & Motivation

Branch `v1.20.24` introduces Superior Lifeforce (`lifeforce_modifier_superior`), a third and apex tier of the core magical resource, and updates Blood Golem creation to require it:
- Manifested through condensation of Minor and Major Lifeforce via `bm_manifest_superior_lifeforce_decision`.
- Blood Golem creation (`blood_golem_creation_decision`) now consumes Superior Lifeforce, with Piety cost adjusted to 500.
- Exhaustion backlash (`lifeforce_modifier_negative_superior`) and temporary exhaustion effect (`temporary_negative_lifeforce_effect_superior`) integrated.
- Story panel and recount effect track and display Superior Lifeforce stacks alongside Major and Minor.

## Core Concepts & Changes

- **Superior Lifeforce Modifier**:
  - `lifeforce_modifier_superior`: Stacking permanent modifier (`+0.6` Health, `+6` Life Expectancy, `+6` Fertility Years, `+6` Epidemic Resistance, `+0.6` Negate Health Penalty, `+3` Prowess).
  - `lifeforce_modifier_negative_superior`: Stacking 3-year backlash modifier (`-0.6` Health, `-6` Life Expectancy, `-6` Fertility Years, `-6` Epidemic Resistance, `-0.6` Negate Health Penalty, `-4` Prowess).
- **Manifest Superior Lifeforce**:
  - `bm_manifest_superior_lifeforce_decision`: Costs 250 Piety + 1 Minor + 1 Major Lifeforce. 2-year cooldown. Learning skill check.
  - Consumes Minor and Major Lifeforce upfront. Critical Success grants 1 Superior Lifeforce (+10 Enlightenment XP). Success grants 1 Superior Lifeforce and 1-year Major exhaustion (+5 Enlightenment XP). Failure inflicts 3-year Major exhaustion and refunds Major Lifeforce (+2 Enlightenment XP). Critical Failure inflicts 3-year Major exhaustion and 1-year Minor exhaustion.
- **Blood Golem Creation**:
  - Requires `has_character_modifier = lifeforce_modifier_superior`.
  - Piety cost reduced from 750 to 500.
  - Consumes via `blood_magic_used_superior = yes`.
- **UI & Panel Display**:
  - `bm_blood_mage_story_list_effects.txt` recounts `bm_lifeforce_superior_stacks` (wrapped in `hidden_effect` to prevent stack loop tooltips).
  - Situation panel displays Superior, Major, and Minor stacks with updated explanatory tooltip.

## Key Decisions

- **Distillation Ratio**: 1 Minor + 1 Major Lifeforce per Superior Lifeforce.
- **Upfront Resource Consumption & Refund on Failure**: Minor and Major Lifeforce are always consumed on cast; normal failure refunds Major Lifeforce while applying 3-year Major exhaustion.
- **Cost Rebalance**: Reduced Golem Piety cost from 750 to 500 Piety to account for separate manifestation expenditure.
