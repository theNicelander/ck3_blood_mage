# v1.20.46: Live Summary Document (Release Notes v1.15 -> v1.20) & Documentation Skill

## Summary & Motivation
Synthesizes all changes across 45+ version branches since the June 2025 baseline release (v1.15, git hash `0a94aa6372f037c9ea3729c2d7e93f729aea9d40`) into a public-facing, living release notes document (`RELEASE_NOTES.md`) structured specifically for players and Steam Workshop release announcements. Establishes the `update-release-notes` skill and updates AI rules (`AGENTS.md`, `pr-context.md`, `update-docs`) to maintain `RELEASE_NOTES.md` as an ongoing living document across future releases.

## Key Decisions
- **Safe Nullable House Context Switching**: CK3 decision option list widgets evaluate item triggers globally across character states; using `house ?=` protects against context switch failure when unlanded or lowborn characters are inspected.
- **Character Modifier Compliance for Lineage Covenants**: House modifiers apply their attributes to individual house members; modifier attributes must belong to the character category (`cultural_head_acceptance_gain_mult` instead of culture-only `cultural_acceptance_gain_mult`).
- **Stave Carving in Major Grimoire**: Staves utilize Major Lifeforce for swapping and are accessed alongside other major blood rites in Cast Major Blood Magic, eliminating unnecessary standalone decisions from the situation hub while keeping stave binding accessible with clean backout navigation.
- **Progressive Blood Rune Gating**: Showing rune options only when at the active tier of inscription (Minor only if no rune, Major only if holding Minor, Superior only if holding Major) prevents cluttering grimoires with obsolete or leap-frogged inscription rites while maintaining `show_as_unavailable` failure tooltips when eligible.
- **Contiguous Category Index (`index = 16`)**: CK3 crashes if category indexes have gaps. Vanilla categories occupy 0 to 15. Placing Blood Magic at index 16 integrates it modularly without touching vanilla `00_character_interaction_categories.txt`.
- **Unified "Blood Magic" Category**: Consolidating friendly and hostile blood magic interactions into a single thematic category clarifies the right-click menu, making all mod spells and rites immediately identifiable and distinct from vanilla diplomatic, personal, and hostile actions.
- **Interface Toast Outcome Encapsulation**: Effects outside `send_interface_toast` execute silently without visual player feedback. Moving modifiers, track XP, and stack recounts inside `send_interface_toast` while dropping static `custom_tooltip` overrides ensures players receive clear, native engine tooltips showing exactly what was gained or lost.
- **Lifeforce over Fluffy Flavour**: Replaced vague flavour like "vitality" and "vital arts" with the concrete mechanic name "Lifeforce" across gameplay alerts, tooltips, and guidelines to ensure player clarity. Flavour is restricted to narrative bodies.
- **Engine-Native Count in Situation Alerts**: Omitted count tokens in `_combined_group_label` because CK3 natively formats and appends the item count in the list header.
- **Standalone Widget Decision for Lineage Covenants**: Because lineage covenants are long-term house investments rather than personal somatic transformations, housing them in a dedicated inline option-list decision with visual track icons and clear tier-based requirements provides optimal UX.
- **Replacing Modifiers per Tier**: Replacing lower-tier modifiers with upgraded variants keeps character and house modifier lists clean while preserving cumulative bonuses.
- **Permanent Covenants over Temporary Buffs**: Removed temporary 10-year Bloodline Surges so Empower Dynasty Bloodline functions strictly as an enduring, milestone-based lineage covenant investment without cluttering house modifiers or diluting the Lifeforce economy.
- **Unified White Hair Progression**: Unified Blood Knights to use the same progressive white hair as Blood Mages, discarding golden blonde to maintain aesthetic consistency across both blood disciplines.
- **Skill Superiority Gating for Wilderness Recruitment**: Persuading wilderness travelers or sworn knights to join court now requires demonstrating intellectual or martial superiority (Learning for the warrior-poet, Prowess for the knight), making court recruitment an earned triumph rather than a free roll.
- **Local County Faith for Wilderness Encounters**: Spawning encounters with the local county's faith grounds wilderness events organically into the regional culture and religious landscape rather than defaulting to the player's foreign faith.
- **Net Diff Presentation**: Intermediate development steps (e.g., Crimson Warrior interaction → Crimson Knight trait → stepping stone to Blood Mage) are collapsed forward into their net final state (Blood Knight as a permanent Devotion-anchored martial peer to Blood Mage).
- **Public-Facing vs. Developer Documentation**: Architecture documents in `docs-ai/architecture/` remain the source of high-density technical specifications (variable names, script values, engine mechanics), while `RELEASE_NOTES.md` serves players and Steam Workshop subscribers.
