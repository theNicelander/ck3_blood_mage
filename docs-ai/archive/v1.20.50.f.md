# v1.20.50.f: 5 Interactive Narrative & Interpersonal Events

## Summary & Motivation
Introduces 5 interactive narrative and interpersonal events for Blood Mages and Blood Knights, enriching personal relationships, martial comradeship, golem wardship, and court intrigues. These events fire through a yearly pulse (with a 3-year cooldown) or dynamically upon winning battles with sworn knights.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Yearly Interpersonal Pulse (`common/on_action/bm_yearly_pulse.txt`)**:
  - Adds `bm_yearly_interpersonal_pulse` to `random_yearly_everyone_pulse`, gated to human players (`is_ai = no`) who are Blood Mages or Blood Knights and lack `bm_interpersonal_event_cooldown`.
  - Dynamically weights eligible narrative events (`bm_interpersonal_events.0001`, `.0002`, `.0004`, `.0005`) and sets a 3-year cooldown flag upon firing.
- **Combat Victory Hook (`common/on_action/bm_combat_on_actions.txt`)**:
  - In `bm_on_combat_end_winner`, rolls a 15% chance to trigger `bm_interpersonal_events.0003` (The Red Banquet) when the player is a victorious commander/participant with an eligible knight and no active cooldown.
- **Interactive Narrative Events (`events/bm_interpersonal_events.txt`)**:
  - `bm_interpersonal_events.0001` (**A Drop on the Lips** — Lover / Soulmate / Spouse):
    - Option A: Share a drop of Lifeforce (1 Minor Lifeforce -> Partner gains `bm_blessed_blood_modifier` for 10 yrs, +30 Opinion, -30 Stress for both, +3 Benediction/Vanguard XP).
    - Option B: Drink from offered vein (Partner takes 1-yr `lifeforce_modifier_negative_minor` -> Player gains 1 Minor Lifeforce or 1 Major Lifeforce if Soulmate, +2 Hematurgy/Slaughter XP).
    - Option C: Initiate into Crimson Mysteries (1 Major Lifeforce -> Bestows Blood Mage or Blood Knight trait, elevates to Soulmate, +5 Bloodline/Vanguard XP).
  - `bm_interpersonal_events.0002` (**The Fever of Awakening** — Child / Ward Age 6–15):
    - Option A: Guide pulse with Minor Lifeforce (1 Minor Lifeforce -> Child gains `bm_guided_pulse_child_modifier` [+1 Learning, +1 Martial, +2 Prowess], +20 Opinion, player +3 Bloodline/Vanguard XP).
    - Option B: Force awakening early (1 Major Lifeforce -> Child awakens to Blood Mage or Blood Knight with +10 Ancient XP, player +5 Bloodline/Vanguard XP).
    - Option C: Soothe fever with prayer (+75 Piety, +2 Enlightenment XP).
  - `bm_interpersonal_events.0003` (**The Red Banquet After Battle** — Sworn Knight):
    - Option A: Drink together as comrades (Both gain 1 Minor Lifeforce, +25 Opinion, +3 Vanguard/Bloodline XP).
    - Option B: Claim entire harvest as Warlord (Player gains 1 Major Lifeforce, +4 Slaughter/Hematurgy XP, knight loses 15 Opinion).
    - Option C: Consecrate slain (+150 Piety, +3 Enlightenment XP).
  - `bm_interpersonal_events.0004` (**Echoes in the Clay** — Blood Golem in Court):
    - Option A: Bind as Eternal Guardian of Heir/Spouse (Ward target gains `bm_golem_aegis_modifier` [+4 Prowess, -20% Enemy Hostile Scheme Success, +0.25 Health for 10 yrs], +3 Bloodline/Vanguard XP).
    - Option B: Awaken ancient martial memory (Golem gains `lifestyle_blademaster` or +4 Prowess, +3 Enlightenment XP).
    - Option C: Siphon excess ichor from core (Player gains 1 Major Lifeforce, +3 Hematurgy/Slaughter XP, golem takes `bm_siphoned_golem_modifier` [-4 Prowess for 5 yrs]).
  - `bm_interpersonal_events.0005` (**The Whispering Sanctum** — Snooping Courtier):
    - Option A: Drain in the dark (Intrigue/Prowess duel -> Win: 1 Minor Lifeforce, +15 Dread, +3 Hematurgy/Slaughter XP, courtier gets `lifedrained_modifier`).
    - Option B: Buy silence with Minor Lifeforce (1 Minor Lifeforce -> Courtier gains `lifeforce_modifier_minor`, +40 Opinion, player gains `loyalty_hook`, +3 Benediction/Vanguard XP).
    - Option C: Disperse ritual mist (+3 Enlightenment XP, -20 Stress).
- **New Modifiers (`common/modifiers/bm_interpersonal_modifiers.txt`)**:
  - `bm_guided_pulse_child_modifier`: `learning = 1`, `martial = 1`, `prowess = 2`.
  - `bm_golem_aegis_modifier`: `prowess = 4`, `enemy_hostile_scheme_success_chance_add = -20`, `health = 0.25`.
  - `bm_siphoned_golem_modifier`: `prowess = -4`.

## Key Decisions
- **Shared Class Balance**: Provided distinct, balanced trait XP branches for both Blood Mages and Blood Knights across all 5 events, ensuring neither class misses out on narrative immersion or progression.
- **Cooldown & Target Protection**: Enforced a 3-year character flag cooldown (`bm_interpersonal_event_cooldown`) and rigorous target validity checks so events only fire when relevant characters (lovers, wards, golems, or courtiers) are physically present.
- **Native Toast Outcome Formatting**: Enclosed all resource gains, opinion adjustments, and lifeforce recounting within `send_interface_toast` blocks without custom tooltips to let the game engine display effects cleanly.
