# v1.20.45: Blood Empowerment Overhaul & Inline Option-List Decision

## Summary & Motivation
Overhaul `lifestyle_blood_empowerment` to eliminate redundant stat overlap with `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight`, support both landed and adventurer playstyles, introduce distinct Fame-anchored 50 XP milestones and 100 XP capstones across all 5 tracks, sync vitality baselines, and promote Channel Blood Empowerment to a first-class inline option-list widget decision (`bm_channel_blood_empowerment_decision`).

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Vitality Baseline Synchronization**: Synced `@bm_common_life_expectancy = 2.5` and `@bm_common_years_of_fertility = 1.25` in `lifestyle_blood_empowerment` across tiers 10–40 and 60–90 (8 steps total = `+0.8` Health, `+20` Life Exp, `+10` Fertility Yrs, `+8` Epidemic Res per track; `+4.0` Health, `+100` Life Exp, `+50` Fertility Yrs, `+40` Epidemic Res across all 5 tracks).
- **Stat De-Overlap & Fame/Somatic Milestones**:
  - `dynasty`: Sanguine eugenics, congenital trait strengthening, and kinship bonds. 50 XP: `-50%` inbreeding chance, `+25%` negate fertility penalty, `+5%` renown mult. 100 XP: `-100%` inbreeding chance (complete immunity), `+50%` negate fertility penalty, `+15%` renown mult.
  - `mastery`: World-walker speed, safety, and personal schemes. 50 XP: `+1` Max Personal Schemes, `+1` Learning per Fame level, `+15%` Cultural Acceptance. 100 XP: `+2` Max Personal Schemes, `+2` Learning per Fame level, Ignore Negative Culture Opinion (`ignore_negative_culture_opinion = yes`).
  - `presence`: Mesmeric allure, stress mastery, and legendary authority. 50 XP: `+1` Diplomacy & `+1` Martial per Fame level, `+15%` Fame Impact. 100 XP: `+1` Max Fame Level (`character_max_prestige_level_add = 1`), Ignore Different Faith Opinion (`ignore_different_faith_opinion = yes`), `+1.5` Diplomacy & Martial per Fame level.
  - `prosperity`: War chest, army logistics, and infrastructure. 50 XP: `+1` Men-at-Arms Regiment Size (`men_at_arms_cap = 1`), `+1` Stewardship per Fame level, `+0.25` Gold/mo per Devotion level. 100 XP: `+2` MaA Regiment Size, `+1` MaA Regiment Slot (`men_at_arms_limit = 1`), `+1` Domain Limit, `+1.5` Stewardship per Fame level.
  - `shadows`: Occult plot defense, premonition, and counter-intrigue. 50 XP: `-15%` Enemy Hostile Scheme Max Success Cap (`enemy_hostile_scheme_success_chance_max_add = -15`), `+1` Intrigue & Prowess per Fame level. 100 XP: `-30%` Enemy Hostile Scheme Max Success Cap, `+1` Max Hostile Schemes (`max_hostile_schemes_add = 1`), `+1.5` Intrigue & Prowess per Fame level.
- **Standalone Inline Option-List Decision (`bm_channel_blood_empowerment_decision`)**:
  - Converted the advancement ritual into a dedicated decision using `decision_view_widget_option_list_generic` with `show_from_start = yes`.
  - Displays each track's dedicated icon (`gfx/interface/icons/trait_level_tracks/<track>.dds`) and rich descriptions with step bonuses, 50 XP milestones, and 100 XP capstones.
  - Includes `bm_empower_versatility` fallback when all 5 tracks are at 100 XP.
  - Registered in `bm_blood_mage_story` decisions list and Decisions tab.
  - Removed nested empowerment option from `bm_cast_blood_magic_major.001` and retired `events/bm_blood_empowerment_event.txt`.
- **Devotion Apex Capstones (`lifestyle_blood_mage` & `lifestyle_blood_knight`)**:
  - Replaced non-functional `character_max_piety_level_add = 1` on 100 XP capstones of `ancient`, `enlightenment`, and `benediction` across both traits. In CK3 1.20, `currency_levels_cap` in government types hard-clamps character devotion levels to `DEFAULT_PIETY_LEVEL_CAP = 5` for all non-Mandala governments.
  - Upgraded 100 XP capstones to working, tangible Devotion modifiers across all governments:
    - `ancient`: Replaced `character_max_piety_level_add = 1` with `piety_level_impact_mult = 0.325` (bringing cumulative Devotion Impact from `ancient` to `+50%`, amplifying all Level of Devotion effects).
    - `enlightenment`: Replaced `character_max_piety_level_add = 1` and upgraded `learning_per_piety_level` to `1.0` (bringing cumulative Learning per Devotion level to `+2.0`, up to `+10` Learning at Level 5 Devotion).
    - `benediction`: Replaced `character_max_piety_level_add = 1` and upgraded `diplomacy_per_piety_level` to `1.0` (bringing cumulative Diplomacy per Devotion level to `+2.0`, up to `+10` Diplomacy at Level 5 Devotion).
    - All tracks retain universal age prowess preservation (`no_prowess_loss_from_age = yes`).
- **Blood Magic Appearance & Blood Knight Golden Blonde Hair**:
  - Extended portrait alteration modifiers to Blood Knights (`lifestyle_blood_knight`).
  - Blood Knights receive the same supernatural blood-red eyes as Blood Mages (subtle red at 100 XP, supernatural red at 200 XP, vivid blood-red at 300 XP, pale eyes at 400 XP, white eyes at 500 XP / On).
  - Blood Knights develop radiant golden-yellow blonde hair (`mode = replace` with coordinates `x = 0.46`), transitioning gradually at 300 XP (warm blonde hue, `y = 0.25`), 400 XP (bright blonde, `y = 0.10`), and 500 XP / On (radiant golden blonde as possible, `y = 0.00`).
  - Consolidated the appearance game rule (`bm_physical_alteration`) into three clear options: Off (`bm_physical_alteration_off`), Gradual based on XP gain (`bm_physical_alteration_gradual`), and On (`bm_physical_alteration_on`, default) which immediately displays max supernatural eyes and hair for anyone with the trait.

## Key Decisions
- **Fame vs. Devotion Anchors**: While `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight` scale their milestones off Devotion (`piety_level`), Blood Empowerment scales its milestones off Fame (`prestige_level`), establishing Blood Empowerment as somatic and worldly ascendancy.
- **Inbreeding Immunity**: Providing `-100%` inbreeding chance at Dynasty 100 rewards players who want to build a concentrated dynasty of blood-mage and blood-knight scions without hereditary collapse.
- **Dedicated Widget Decision**: Replacing the nested event chain with an inline option-list decision aligns Blood Empowerment with Lifeforce Attunement and Galdrastafir Stave selection, displaying track icons and avoiding un-cancellable resource deductions.
- **Zero-Overwrite Devotion Capstones**: Rather than overwriting vanilla government definitions to add `currency_levels_cap = { piety = 8 }` (which breaks total conversions like AGOT and Elder Kings) or globally overriding `DEFAULT_PIETY_LEVEL_CAP` (which raises base cap to 8 for all characters worldwide), replaced `character_max_piety_level_add` with direct Devotion Impact (`+50%`) and `+2.0` stat-per-devotion scaling that functions 100% cleanly in every government type.
- **Unified Appearance Game Rule**: Consolidated eye and hair settings into a unified 3-state rule (Off, Gradual, On default). The 'On' default setting allows characters with the trait to showcase their full supernatural appearance immediately, while 'Gradual' retains progressive track XP scaling.
- **Golden Blonde Palette via `mode = replace`**: Set Blood Knight hair coordinates to `(0.46, 0.0)` in CK3's hair palette with `mode = replace` so all Blood Knights achieve the brightest, most vibrant golden blonde hair regardless of their character base DNA hair color.
