# v1.20.32: Cumulative Trait Track Benefits Architecture Spec & Documentation Refresh

## Summary & Motivation
Establish a comprehensive living architecture reference for cumulative total benefits across all 15 tracks of the three core levelable traits (`lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_blood_knight`, and `lifestyle_blood_empowerment`) at 10, 50, and 100 XP, and synchronize architecture documentation across the mod.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Cumulative Trait Benefits Reference (`blood-mage-trait-benefits.md`)**:
  - Outlines exact cumulative benefits at 10, 50, and 100 XP in a 4-column matrix (`Track | 10 XP | 50 XP | 100 XP`), one row per track.
  - Documents Blood Mage (5 schools), Blood Knight (2 martial + 3 shared schools), and Blood Empowerment (5 domain tracks).
  - Explicitly documents vitality skipping at tiers 50/100 in Blood Mage / BK shared tracks (`ancient`, `benediction`, `enlightenment`) vs universal vitality retention in Empowerment and BK martial tracks (`vanguard`, `slaughter`).
  - Documents exact mechanics for somatic resilience on `ancient` (`negate_health_penalty = 0.25 / 0.50`, prowess age lock), `mastery` language scheme progression (tiers 30, 60, 90, 100), and base trait stats.
- **Blood Mage and Blood Knight Trait Track Restructure**:
  - Reorganized tracks across `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight` into 3 shared tracks and 2 exclusive tracks each:
    - **Shared (3 tracks, 1:1 on both)**: `ancient` (Piety, longevity, somatic agelessness), `benediction` (Diplomacy, healing, elevation), and `enlightenment` (Learning, lifeforce generation, self-enhancement).
    - **Blood Mage Exclusive (2 tracks)**: `bloodline` (Stewardship, dynasty, golems) and `hematurgy` (Intrigue, lifedrain, dark rites).
    - **Blood Knight Exclusive (2 tracks)**: `vanguard` (Martial, commanding armies, battlefield protection) and `slaughter` (Prowess, single combat fatal duels, lethal harvesting).
  - Retired `resilience` from Blood Knight; moved `negate_health_penalty` and age prowess lock into `ancient` at 50 and 100 XP.
  - Aligned `enlightenment` milestones to `learning_per_piety_level = 1` (at 50 and 100 XP).
  - Unified `bm_manifest_lifeforce_decision` for both traits to award +3 `enlightenment` XP via a `learning` skill check.
  - Updated elevation trial (`bm_elevate_to_blood_mage_decision`) to carry over `ancient`, `benediction`, and `enlightenment` 1:1, converting 50% of martial tracks (`vanguard` -> `bloodline`, `slaughter` -> `hematurgy`).
  - Retuned track-specific modifiers:
    - `hematurgy`: replaced passive `-5` scheme defense per tier with "The Blood Stalker" (`owned_hostile_scheme_success_chance_add = 2` and `owned_scheme_secrecy_add = 2`), plus `max_hostile_schemes_add = 1` milestone at 100 XP.
    - `ancient`: moved `monthly_dynasty_prestige = 0.1` and `monthly_lifestyle_xp_gain_mult = 0.05` to Ancient (shared 1:1 across Mage and Knight).
    - `enlightenment`: moved piety gain (`monthly_piety = 0.2` and `monthly_piety_gain_mult = 0.05`) to Enlightenment (shared 1:1 across Mage and Knight).
- **Egill Skallagrímsson Duel Master Configuration**:
  - Configured Egill in `events/bm_geyser_duel_events.txt` to start at age 100 (raised from 26).
  - Bestowed both `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight` traits.
  - Granted 50 XP across all tracks for both traits (`ancient`, `enlightenment`, `benediction`, `bloodline`, `hematurgy`, `vanguard`, `slaughter`).
  - Granted 10 stacks of Lifeforce (`lifeforce_modifier_minor`).
  - Removed trait-level `opposites` blocks from `common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt` and `common/traits/bm_blood_knight_trait.txt` so legendary characters can possess both, while decisions and interactions retain mutual exclusivity triggers.
- **Trial of Blood Exclusivity & Ascension Gating**:
  - Excluded characters with `lifestyle_blood_knight` from Trial of Blood decisions (`bm_blood_cultist_become_blood_mage_decision` and `bm_enhance_blood_ritual_decision`) and from the initiation Learning duel option in `bm_geyser_duel.0001`. Blood Knights must ascend to Blood Mage via their dedicated ascension ritual instead.
  - Retuned `bm_elevate_to_blood_mage_decision` ("Ascend to Blood Mage"): increased requirements to Piety level >= 3 and Learning > 30, and raised cost from 100 Piety to 500 Piety.
- **Geyser Duel Rebalance**:
  - Rebalanced `bm_geyser_duel.0001` duels to clean milestone scaling against Egill.
  - Learning duel: set difficulty `value = 20`, scaling at 2.5% per stat point (10 -> 25%, 20 -> 50%, 30 -> 75%, 40 -> 90% cap with min 10% / max 90% clamp via `min = -40` / `max = 40`).
  - Prowess duel: set difficulty `value = 30`, scaling at 1.67% per stat point (15 -> 25%, 30 -> 50%, 45 -> 75%, 60 -> 90% cap with min 10% / max 90% clamp).
- **Blood Knight Healing Expansion (`bm_heal_ailment`)**:
  - Blood Knights can now use Minor Lifeforce to heal minor ailments on others, and both Minor Lifeforce (minor ailments) and Major Lifeforce (major ailments, e.g. `weak`, `wounded_2`, `incapable`, `maimed`) on self. Deadly ailments remain strictly reserved for Blood Mages.
  - Self-healing major ailments awards +4 `enlightenment` XP to Blood Knights (`bm_heal_major_cost_and_xp_effect`).
- **Geyser Duel Injury Cap (`bm_geyser_duel.0001`)**:
  - Duel defeat injuries against Egill now strictly cap at `wounded_2` (rank 2), preventing any escalation to `wounded_3` (rank 3) or `maimed`.
- **Blood Empowerment Prosperity Track Rebalance**:
  - Rebalanced `prosperity` track in `lifestyle_blood_empowerment` to grant `monthly_income = 0.1` and `monthly_income_mult = 0.025` per tick (10 XP).
  - Removed legacy money-related modifiers (`domain_tax_mult`, `men_at_arms_maintenance`, `holding_build_gold_cost`, `domicile_build_gold_cost`).
  - At full track cap (100 XP), yields `+1.0` gold/month and `+25%` monthly income.
- **Blood Empowerment Presence Track De-duplication**:
  - Removed redundant `owned_personal_scheme_success_chance_add = 5` per tier from `presence` track (already granted under `mastery`).
  - `presence` now cleanly focuses on stress relief (`stress_loss_mult = 0.05`) and general/vassal diplomacy (`general_opinion = 1.5`, `vassal_opinion = 1`).
- **Architecture Documentation Sync**:
  - Registered `blood-mage-trait-benefits.md` in `docs-ai/architecture/AGENTS.md` and `docs-ai/architecture/README.md`.
  - Harmonized architecture documentation across `docs-ai/architecture/*.md` to reflect current 1.20 CK3 implementation.
- **Runtime Error Log Fixes**:
  - Removed invalid character modifier `scheme_resistance = 3` from all 10 tiers of `shadows` track in `bm_blood_empowerment_trait.txt`.
  - Replaced invalid modifier `all_attributes` with discrete skills and prowess in `bm_modifiers.txt` (`bm_blessed_blood_modifier` and `bm_blood_curse_modifier`).
  - Replaced non-existent traits `lifestyle_blademaster_3` and `lifestyle_hunter_1` with base traits and track XP in `bm_become_blood_mage_decision.txt` and `bm_geyser_duel_events.txt`.
  - Removed trait group names (`beauty_bad`, `intellect_bad`, `physique_bad`) from `remove_trait` effects in `bm_cast_blood_magic_minor_events.txt`.
  - Added self-cast guard `trigger = { NOT = { scope:actor = scope:recipient } }` to `opinion_modifier` in `bm_ai_value_modifiers.txt`.
  - Promoted scopes to characters with `SCOPE.sC` in `lifedrain_execution_effect` debug log string (`bm_drain_all_prisoners_effects.txt`).
  - Populated 5 missing DDS icons in `gfx/interface/icons/trait_level_tracks/` (`dynasty.dds`, `mastery.dds`, `presence.dds`, `prosperity.dds`, `shadows.dds`).

## Key Decisions
- **4-Column Track Format**: Chose `Track | 10 XP | 50 XP | 100 XP` with 1 row per track to provide maximum comparison density across tiers.
- **Shared vs Exclusive Tracks**: Shared the universal mystical/foundation tracks (`ancient`, `benediction`, `enlightenment`) between both Mage and Knight, giving Blood Knight martial/prowess supremacy (`vanguard`, `slaughter`) and Blood Mage governance/subterfuge supremacy (`bloodline`, `hematurgy`).
- **Dedicated Spec vs In-Trait Duplication**: Placed cumulative calculation matrix into dedicated `blood-mage-trait-benefits.md` while keeping `blood-mage-traits.md` focused on concepts and milestones.
