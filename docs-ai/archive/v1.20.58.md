# v1.20.58: Blood Magic Men-at-Arms, 7-Tier Blood Temples Overhaul & Old Age Rejuvenation Simplification

## Summary & Motivation
Introduce two Piety-maintained Men-at-Arms regiments for Blood Mages (`lifestyle_blood_mage`) and Blood Knights (`lifestyle_blood_knight`): **Blood Berserkers** (`bm_blood_berserkers`, heavy infantry shock troops unlocked at Devotion 2+) and **Blood-Bound Behemoths** (`bm_blood_behemoths`, monstrous siege-breaking constructs unlocked at Devotion 4+).
Rename and expand the 4-tier Duchy Blood Universities (`bm_university_0..3`) into a 7-tier regular building line: **Blood Temples** (`bm_blood_temple_01` through `bm_blood_temple_07` in `common/buildings/bm_buildings.txt`), constructible in all holding types (castles, cities, temples, and tribal settlements). Remove negative `monthly_income` gold upkeep across all tiers, gate higher tiers by Devotion Level (`piety_level`), replace cultural fascination and lifestyle XP multipliers with county development growth (flat `+0.1` to `+0.8` and factor `+5%` to `+40%`) and monthly county control growth (`+0.1` to `+0.8`), grant `+0.2` health and `+2` life expectancy per step (`+0.2`/`+2` at Tier I up to `+1.4`/`+14` at Tier VII), and introduce thematic capstone bonuses on Tiers V–VII (genetic inheritance on Great Blood Temple/Pantheon/Acropolis, and fort level/defender advantage/prowess on Blood Acropolis).
Rework old age degenerative trait healing (`infirm`, `clouded_eyes`, `fragile_bones`, `withering_mind`, `faltering_heart`) in the Rejuvenate Old Age event (`bm_heal_old_age.0001`): consolidate from 3 options down to 2 options per trait (11 total options including Cancel), auto-curing traits when track XP is within removal threshold (<= 20 XP for Minor, <= 50 XP for Major) and reducing XP otherwise, accompanied by dynamic button labels and interface toasts.

Counters Pikemen (`1.5`), Skirmishers (`1.0`), and Archers (`1.0`). Terrain bonuses: Taiga/Forest/Mountains (`+12` Damage, `+8` Pursuit), Mild/Harsh Winter (`+10` Damage, `+5` Toughness), Desert/Floodplains (`-8` Damage, `-4` Toughness). Uses `varangian_veterans` icon.

Terrain bonuses: Mountains/Desert Mountains/Hills/Drylands (`+15` Toughness), Wetlands (`-30` Damage, `-15` Toughness), no winter/desert penalties. Requires `lifestyle_blood_mage` or `lifestyle_blood_knight` and `piety_level >= 4` (visible grayed out via `should_show_when_unavailable` for Blood Mages/Knights at lower Devotion). ## Key Decisions
- **Devotion-Gated Piety Men-at-Arms (`bm_blood_berserkers` & `bm_blood_behemoths`)**: Tying recruitment and monthly maintenance to Piety and gating behind Devotion Level 2+ (`Blood Berserkers`) and Devotion Level 4+ (`Blood-Bound Behemoths`) gives Blood Mages and Blood Knights a compelling military Piety sink while keeping both units visible in the Men-at-Arms recruitment panel (`should_show_when_unavailable`) from the moment a character awakens to blood magic.
- **Dynamic Auto-Cure over Redundant Separate Options**: Having a separate "Cure" button that costs Major Lifeforce while Major Rejuvenation also costs Major Lifeforce created confusion and forced characters with low XP (e.g. 0-10 XP) to navigate clunky sub-options. Auto-curing whenever track XP is reduced to 0 (or starts below the tier's XP reduction power: <=20 for Minor, <=50 for Major) makes rejuvenation natural, responsive, and clean.
- **7-Tier Regular Blood Temples vs 4-Tier Duchy Universities**: Duchy capital slots are strictly limited to 1 per duchy, locking out counts and tribal rulers. Converting and expanding the line into 7 regular **Blood Temple** tiers (`Blood Altar` -> `Blood Acropolis`) gated by Devotion Level allows rulers across any holding type (including tribal) to progressively sanctify their domain without negative gold upkeep, earning `+0.2` health and `+2` life expectancy per step alongside Piety, Renown, Development, and Control growth.
- **Unified Grant Lifeforce for Self & Universal Lifedrain Cleansing**: Rather than creating a redundant separate self-interaction for lifedrain removal, conditionally exposing `grant_lifeforce_interaction` on oneself when `lifedrained_modifier` is active keeps spell interactions streamlined. Showing the interaction grayed out with explicit Major Lifeforce + Piety requirements when resources are lacking adheres to the mod principle of "Nothing hidden, but often unavailable".
- **Blood Hunt Availability via Failures-Only Preview**: Gating "Go on a Blood Hunt" (`seek_power_decision`) in `is_valid_showing_failures_only` rather than `is_shown` ensures players always know the hunt exists while providing clear contextual tooltips when traveling, attending an activity, imprisoned, or incapable. Matching `ai_potential` ensures AI characters never attempt to launch wilderness hunts while abroad or incapacitated.
