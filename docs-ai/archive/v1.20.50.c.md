# v1.20.50.c: Crimson Sworn-Guard Court Position, Vanguard Anointment, Ritual Sparring & Champion Cull

## Summary & Motivation
Stream C introduces dedicated martial rites and retinue positions for Blood Knights and Blood Mages:
1. **Crimson Sworn-Guard (`bm_blood_bodyguard_court_position`)**: A specialized court bodyguard position (up to 2 positions) requiring Blood Knights or immortal Blood Golems (`house:bm_house_golem`), scaling hostile scheme defense and dread baseline with prowess, martial, and vanguard/slaughter track progression.
2. **Anoint Vanguard Champion (`bm_anoint_knight_interaction`)**: Consecration of sworn knights and Blood Knight courtiers with Minor Lifeforce, granting `bm_anointed_champion_modifier` (+4 prowess, +0.5 health, +0.5 negate health penalty) for 5 years.
3. **Ritual Sparring (`bm_ritual_spar_interaction`)**: Contested non-lethal prowess duel against knights, blood knights, or martial friends (2-yr cooldown), yielding track XP and condensing a drop of Minor Lifeforce on Critical Success.
4. **Cull Fallen Champion (`bm_cull_champion_interaction`)**: Sacrificial harvest of prowess 12+ prisoners, blood knight captives, or broken followers, granting Major Lifeforce and rolling prowess-scaled chance (capped at 50%) for Superior Lifeforce.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Court Position (`common/court_positions/types/bm_court_positions.txt`)**:
  - `bm_blood_bodyguard_court_position`: Available to Blood Mage and Blood Knight lieges. Requires combatant candidate with `lifestyle_blood_knight` or member of `house:bm_house_golem`. Aptitude scales with prowess (x2), martial, vanguard and slaughter XP (x0.25), loyal (+20), and giant (+15). Grants scaling employer hostile scheme success reduction (-2 to -15) and dread baseline (+5 to +15).
- **Character Modifiers (`common/modifiers/bm_knight_modifiers.txt`)**:
  - `bm_anointed_champion_modifier`: +4 prowess, +0.5 health, +0.5 negate health penalty.
  - `bm_spar_honed_modifier`: +2 prowess, +1 martial (for non-blood knight sparring partners).
- **Character Interactions (`common/character_interactions/bm_knight_rites.txt`)**:
  - `bm_anoint_knight_interaction`: 50 Piety + Minor Lifeforce. Awards +2 vanguard (Knight) or +2 benediction (Mage) XP.
  - `bm_ritual_spar_interaction`: 50 Piety, 2-yr cooldown. Duel with prowess target. Critical success condenses 1 Minor Lifeforce and grants +3 XP to both.
  - `bm_cull_champion_interaction`: 50 Piety. Executes target with `death_lifedrain_reason`, grants 1 Major Lifeforce, +3 slaughter/hematurgy XP, and rolls `recipient.prowess` % (max 50%) for 1 Superior Lifeforce.
- **Script Value (`common/script_values/bm_cull_champion_values.txt`)**:
  - `bm_cull_champion_superior_chance_value`: Evaluates `scope:recipient.prowess` clamped between 0 and 50.

## Key Decisions
- **Court Position Restrictions**: Limited strictly to Blood Knights and Golems to reinforce blood-bound martial retainers.
- **Duel Resolution**: Used native `duel = { skill = prowess target = scope:recipient }` with `compare_modifier` on `scope:duel_value` to reward high prowess differences.
- **Cull Scaling**: Evaluates recipient prowess prior to execution to reward defeating and culling elite warriors.
