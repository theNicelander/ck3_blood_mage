# v1.20.54: Interface Toast Enclosure Audit, Engine Error Fixes & Trait Track XP Rebalance

## Summary & Motivation
Audit and enforce interface toast encapsulation across all events, decisions, and roll outcomes granting modifiers, traits, Lifeforce stacks, stats, or XP, and resolve data-binding and engine syntax issues across character interactions and events.
Rebalance XP progression for `lifestyle_blood_knight` and `lifestyle_blood_mage` traits, resolving Vanguard over-accumulation without battles, Enlightenment vs Benediction healing misrouting, and excessive decision/event XP.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Trait Track XP Rebalance**:
  - **Vanguard Non-Combat Depuration**: Purged non-combat family rites, childbirth, union consecration, anointing heirs, and congenital trait manifestation from flooding the martial `vanguard` track.
  - **Childbirth Milestones**: Rerouted Blood Knight childbirth milestone from `vanguard` to `ancient` (+1 base, +2 with inheriting trait or Feingur).
  - **Healing Consolidation**: Consolidated all healing (self and other characters) to grant strictly `benediction` XP (+1 for minor ailments, +2 for major/deadly) across both Blood Mages and Blood Knights, fixing the erroneous `enlightenment` and `vanguard` grants.
  - **Family Rites**: Consecrate Union, anoint heir, and bloodline blessings rerouted to `benediction` (+1 to +2 XP) for Blood Knights.
  - **Congenital Manifestations**: Targeted by attribute: Physique -> `vanguard` (+1 standard / +2 superior), Intellect -> `enlightenment` (+1 standard / +2 superior), Beauty -> `ancient` (+1 standard / +2 superior). Removed excess superior Enlightenment XP bonus.
  - **Manifest Lifeforce Pacing**: Flattened Manifest Lifeforce decision XP from +1/+3/+5/+7 to +1/+1/+2/+2 `enlightenment`.
  - **Education Enhancements**: Scaled down Master Education (5★) from +20 XP to +3 to +5 `enlightenment`, and Second Education Trait to +3 XP.
  - **Channel Blood Power & Minor Channel**: Scaled bonus Enlightenment to +1 XP, and purged duplicate Blood Knight XP triggers.
  - **Yearly Interpersonal Events**: Toned down event option gains from +3–+5 to +1 XP, mapping domestic options to `benediction` or `ancient`.
- **Rules Update**:
  - `.agents/rules/ck3-scripting.md`: Expanded Rule 10 to mandate `send_interface_toast` for any event or decision effect granting modifiers, traits, Lifeforce, stats, or roll rewards.
  - `.agents/rules/ck3-events.md`: Mandate toasts for all event options and roll outcomes awarding traits/modifiers/lifeforce.
  - `.agents/rules/ck3-decisions.md`: Mandate toasts for decision completion effects awarding modifiers/traits/attunements.
- **Events Enclosure**:
  - `events/bm_education_new.txt`: Enclose new education trait grants and XP in interface toasts.
  - `events/bm_trait_drain_events.txt`: Enclose duel success trait drains (intellect, physique, beauty, fecund, giant, pure_blooded) and fallback major Lifeforce grant inside interface toasts.
  - `events/bm_channel_lifeforce_enlightenment_minor_events.txt`: Enclose all 7 stat modifier boosts and XP in interface toasts.
  - `events/bm_reshape_personality_events.txt`: Move personality trait replacement logic inside interface toasts so changes render on popup.
- **Decisions & Scripted Effects Enclosure**:
  - `common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_attune_lifeforce_decision.txt`: Enclose all 7 attunement modifier additions in interface toasts.
  - `common/decisions/become_mage/bm_convert_from_witch.txt`: Enclose witch conversion and `lifestyle_blood_mage` grant inside interface toast.
  - `common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_cast_blood_magic.txt`: Enclose minor purge trait removals/XP and rune Lifeforce recount effects inside interface toasts.
  - `common/scripted_effects/bm_empower_bloodline_effects.txt`: Enclose house covenant modifiers inside interface toasts.
- **Interaction Polish**:
  - `common/character_interactions/bm_become_a_blood_mage.txt`: Enclose cannibal trait gain upon draining maimed prisoner in interface toast.
  - `common/character_interactions/bm_curse_blood.txt`: Enclose blood curse and ill trait afflictions on recipient inside interface toast.
- **Engine Data-Binding & Scoping Fixes**:
  - Fix trigger data errors by binding subject to recipient and using `CHARACTER` loc syntax in Consecrate Union and Siphon Stored Lifeforce.
  - Move `show_as_unavailable` out of trigger blocks in `blood_golem.002`.
  - Guard `primary_heir` comparison with native `is_primary_heir_of` in `bm_inscribe_rune_other`.
  - Shield `lifedrain_execution_effect` debug logging against unset victim scopes during option tooltip evaluations.

## Key Decisions
- Rerouted Blood Knight childbirth to `ancient` (generational blood continuity) and family rites to `benediction` (healing and blessing kin) rather than allowing them to artificially advance the combat `vanguard` track.
- Re-anchored all healing magic to `benediction` across both Blood Mages and Blood Knights (+1 minor, +2 major/deadly).
- Chose to enclose native effects directly in toasts rather than wrapping them in `custom_tooltip` so CK3 engine natively formats traits, modifiers, and XP in toast banners.
