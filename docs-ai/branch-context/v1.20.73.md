# v1.20.73: Bidirectional Lifeforce Transmutation (Condense & Split)

## Motivation

- Complete the Lifeforce conversion loop: Previously, characters could only condense upward (Minor -> Major and Major + Minor -> Superior), creating bottlenecks when characters held higher-tier Lifeforce but needed lower-tier fuel for minor rites, healing, or lineage sharing.
- Symmetrical, Piety-gated transmutation: Enable Blood Mages to dissolve higher-tier Lifeforce reservoirs into lower tiers (Superior -> 2 Major, Major -> 2 Minor) while harmonizing condensation costs with 6-month cooldowns to prevent instantaneous infinite loops.
- Prevent invalid script assumptions: Formally record the CK3 engine limitation prohibiting triggers from checking multi-stack modifiers (`has_character_modifier` only checks `>= 1` stack) across mod rules and architecture documentation.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Split Lifeforce Operations (`common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_cast_blood_magic.txt`)**:
  - Added `bm_superior_split` to `bm_cast_blood_magic_superior_decision`:
    - Spend 350 Piety to dissolve 1 Superior Lifeforce into 2 Major Lifeforces.
    - 6-month cooldown flag: `bm_split_superior_lifeforce_cooldown` (`days = 180`).
    - Awards +3 `ancient` XP.
  - Added `bm_major_split` to `bm_cast_blood_magic_major_decision`:
    - Spend 150 Piety to dissolve 1 Major Lifeforce into 2 Minor Lifeforces.
    - 6-month cooldown flag: `bm_split_major_lifeforce_cooldown` (`days = 180`).
    - Awards +2 `ancient` XP.
- **Harmonized Condense Lifeforce Operations (`common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_cast_blood_magic.txt`)**:
  - Updated `bm_minor_condense` in `bm_cast_blood_magic_minor_decision`:
    - Spend 150 Piety to transmute 1 Minor Lifeforce into 1 Major Lifeforce.
    - Added 6-month cooldown flag: `bm_condense_minor_lifeforce_cooldown` (`days = 180`).
    - Awards +2 `ancient` XP.
  - Updated `bm_major_condense` in `bm_cast_blood_magic_major_decision`:
    - Spend 350 Piety to transmute 1 Major Lifeforce into 1 Superior Lifeforce (no longer requires Minor Lifeforce or Learning duel).
    - 6-month cooldown flag: `bm_condense_major_lifeforce_cooldown` (`days = 180`).
    - Awards +3 `ancient` XP.
- **Superior Decision Access (`common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_cast_blood_magic.txt`)**:
  - Expanded `bm_cast_blood_magic_superior_decision` `is_shown` so any Blood Mage holding Superior Lifeforce (`has_character_modifier = lifeforce_modifier_superior`) can view the decision and split their Superior Lifeforce, even before attaining Archmage rank.
- **Engine Rule & Architecture Documentation**:
  - Added Rule 14 to `.agents/rules/ck3-scripting.md`: Explicitly document that CK3 script triggers cannot check for multiple stacks (`>= 2`) of a modifier.
  - Updated `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-lifeforce.md` Gotchas and Master Sources & Sinks Matrix.

### Other changes

- Added complete localization strings, cooldown text, and toasts in `localization/english/bm_cast_blood_magic_self_l_english.yml`.
- Updated `RELEASE_NOTES.md` with new transmutation capabilities.
