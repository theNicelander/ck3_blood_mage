# v1.20.81: Blood Sparring Knight Requirement & Dynamic Loc Polish

## Motivation

- Blood Sparring Ring allowed mundane (non-mage) knights to be selected for the ritual practice circle, inappropriately granting them Bloodlust track XP and condensing Major Lifeforce without arcane capacity.
- Empower Dynasty Bloodline decision tooltips displayed verbose multi-tier text walls with colored tags; players requested simplified plain-text tooltips showing only the next tier's modifier benefits.
- Covenant of Warband army movement speed was replaced with additional knights (1/2/3 per tier) to strengthen martial synergy with knight mechanics.
- Encounter event option tooltips in "Blades on the Lonely Road" (`seek_power.003`) failed with engine data errors (`ERROR:ERROR:[recipient...]`) and missing negative trigger localization keys (`not_seek_power.003.c.learning_trigger_tt_first`).
- Blood Magic Men-at-Arms (Blood Berserkers and Blood-Bound Behemoths) appeared greyed out or unrecruitable in the recruitment interface with no explanation in the unit flavor/description of why they were locked.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Blood Sparring Ring Blood Mage Knight Requirement (`bm_ritual_spar_decision`)**:
  - Gated candidate eligibility in `is_valid` and candidate selection sampling in `effect` on `is_blood_mage_any_tier_trigger = yes`.
  - Only sworn knights holding the Blood Mage lifestyle trait at any tier (Acolyte, Blood Mage, or Archmage) can enter the sparring circle.
  - Updated decision requirement and event narrative text to clearly specify sworn Blood Mage knights.

- **Blood Magic Men-at-Arms Recruitment Clarity (`bm_blood_berserkers`, `bm_blood_behemoths`)**:
  - Added explicit `#help` explanatory requirement lines to `bm_blood_berserkers_flavor` and `bm_blood_behemoths_flavor` displaying required Blood Mage trait, Level of Devotion ([piety_level|E] Faithful / Paragon of Virtue), and Piety costs (250 / 400).
  - Explicitly aligned `can_recruit` in `bm_men_at_arms_types.txt` to require `is_blood_mage_any_tier_trigger = yes` alongside devotion gates (`piety_level >= 2` and `piety_level >= 4`), ensuring non-mages cannot recruit occult regiments and requirements match UI tooltips.

- **Empower Dynasty Bloodline Tooltip Simplification (`bm_empower_bloodline_decision`)**:
  - Replaced the multi-tier colored tooltips across all 6 covenants (`bm_covenant_<covenant>_tooltip`) with dynamic custom localization (`BM_COVENANT_<COVENANT>_TOOLTIP` in `bm_covenant_custom_loc.txt`).
  - Tooltips dynamically display only the next tier's modifier benefits (Tier I if none, Tier II if Minor active, Tier III if Major active).
  - All color codes (`#P`, `#V`, `#Y`, `#bold`) removed in favor of concise plain-text summaries.

- **Covenant of Warband Knight Limit (`dynasty_blood_warband_modifier_1/2/3`)**:
  - Replaced `movement_speed = 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.15` with `knight_limit = 1 / 2 / 3` across all three tiers.
  - Updated modifier loc descriptions and `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-dynasty.md`.

- **Encounter Option Requirement Tooltips (`seek_power.003`, `bm_can_drain_traits_from_target_trigger`)**:
  - Bound target characters (`subject = scope:recipient` and `subject = scope:escort_knight`) in `custom_description` blocks so the localization engine resolves `[CHARACTER.GetShortUINameNoTooltip]`.
  - Added missing negative trigger requirement strings (`NOT_seek_power.003.c.learning_trigger_tt` and `NOT_seek_power.003.recruit_knight.prowess_trigger_tt`) for unavailable challenge options.

- **Blood Mage Progression Recalibration (~100-Year Archmage Target)**:
  - Recalibrated track XP gains across all magic tiers and subsystems so ascending through the sequential 3-tier hierarchy (Acolyte → Mage → Archmage, requiring 2x 100 Ancient XP and 2x 150+ discipline XP) is achievable in roughly ~100 years (~45 years as Acolyte, ~55 years as Blood Mage).
  - Scaled Minor actions to +2 XP (Minor Heals, Channel Minor Lifeforce +3, Purge Impurities +2, Minor Courtier Lifedrain +2, Bless Bloodline +2, Minor Initiation +2, Manifest T1 +2, Transmute Minor +2).
  - Scaled Major actions to +3 to +5 XP (Prisoner Lifedrain +3, Major Heals +4, Heir Anointing +4, Major Kin Initiation +4, Education 1★-4★ +4, Trait Theft +3/+5 rare, House Covenants +3/+5/+8, Transmute Major +4, Manifest T2 +3, T3 +5).
  - Scaled Superior actions to +6 to +8 XP (Deadly Heals +6, Master Education 5★ +4/+4 on win, Embrace New Education +6, Forge Blood Golem +8, Manifest T4 +7).
  - Scaled combat victories (+2 to +4 for Commanders, +2 to +4 for Knights) and Blood Hunt encounters (+2 to +3).
  - Rebalanced Lifeforce condensation and splitting: Minor Condense (+1 Ancient XP), Major Split (+1 Ancient XP), Major Condense (+2 Ancient XP), Superior Split (+2 Ancient XP).
  - Tuned Blood Distillation (`bm_blood_distillation_decision`): grants +7 Ancient XP for -10 Discipline XP (Acolyte) and -12 Discipline XP (Blood Mage, down from -15), preventing players from erasing their total XP progress while distilling toward the 100 Ancient XP cap.
  - Repaired legacy `has_trait = lifestyle_blood_mage` triggers in `bm_seek_power_events.txt`, `bm_interpersonal_events.txt`, `bm_bestow_traits_effects.txt`, `bm_important_actions.txt`, `bm_heal_triggers.txt`, and `bm_convert_from_witch.txt` to properly use `is_blood_mage_any_tier_trigger = yes`, restoring event and alert access to Acolytes and Archmages.

- **Blood Power Track Symmetry & Tooltip Simplification (`lifestyle_blood_power`)**:
  - Refactored `lifestyle_blood_power` across all 6 tracks (`presence`, `warband`, `prosperity`, `shadows`, `mastery`, `dynasty`) so that milestones 10, 30, 50, 70, and 100 XP grant identical recurring baseline bonuses (+0.2 monthly piety per step, +1.0 total), enabling players to immediately know future milestone rewards by inspecting tier 10.
  - Unique capstone effects trigger exclusively at 50 and 100 XP: `presence` (+1 max personal schemes at 50/100, +2 total), `warband` (+1 MaA max size and +1 MaA regiments at 50/100, +2 total on both), `shadows` (+1 max hostile schemes at 50/100, +2 total), `dynasty` (-50% inbreeding chance at 50/100, -100% total immunity), and common 50/100 milestones (+10% piety mult, +5 life expectancy, +0.25 health, +0.02 piety per knight, plus age prowess lock at 100).
  - Streamlined `bm_cast_blood_magic_self_l_english.yml` decision/event option tooltips from verbose 9-line walls of text down to concise track summaries highlighting capstone rewards.

- **Faith Hierarchy & Rite Organization (Catholicism-Style Rite Tree)**:
  - Reorganized the 6 flat blood faiths under `blodtru_religion` into 3 regional faiths (`blodtru_faith`, `ketsudo_faith`, `xuedao_faith`), each cleanly nesting its organized mainline rite and ancestral unreformed variant rite (`old_*_faith`) in the religion interface.
  - Removed redundant `old_*_faith` standalone faith declarations from `bm_blodtru.txt`, `bm_ketsudo.txt`, and `bm_xuedao_faith.txt`.
  - Configured `bm_rite_types.txt` with complete metadata (`name`, `desc`, `icon`, `faith`) for all 6 rites, applying `unreformed_faith_doctrine` to the three Old rites.
  - Updated `events/bm_faith_conversion_events.txt` (`bm_faith_conversion.0002`) to set character faith to the parent faith and apply `set_character_rite` for the chosen ancient rite.

- **Story UI On-Demand Lifeforce Stack Recount (`bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect`)**:
  - Removed ~167 redundant `bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect = yes` call sites across 32 interaction, decision, event, `on_action`, and scripted-effect files (along with empty `hidden_effect` / `scope` wrappers left behind).
  - Kept `bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect` inside `bm_refresh_blood_magic_rosters_effect`, which already runs on Story `on_setup`, when opening the Situations/Story panel (`bm_prepare_blood_magic_story_gui`), and when clicking the panel's Refresh button (`bm_refresh_story_character_lists_gui`).
  - Updated `.agents/rules/ck3-scripting.md`, `.agents/skills/add-interaction-send-options/SKILL.md`, and `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-lifeforce.md`.
