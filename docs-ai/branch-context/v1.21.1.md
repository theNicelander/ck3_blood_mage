# v1.21.1: CK3 1.21 Modifier & District Compatibility

## Motivation

- CK3 1.21 overhauled county development, control, county opinion, tax, and duchy/port building systems (`69` modifier definitions removed, `937` added), and split the Ligurian coast (`c_genoa` / `b_monaco` replaced by `c_savona` / `b_ventimiglia` on province `2022`).
  - Pre-commit modifier validation (`scripts/check_modifiers.py`) failed on `12` allowlist entries and `31` mod usages across Blood Temples, Icelandic traditions, Fortune covenants, Fortunate Lifeforce, beggar drain modifier, and Blóðtrú identity doctrine.
  - `bm_blood_temple_01..07` also used `type = duchy_capital` directly on building definitions (moved to `common/districts/` in 1.21), `bm_icelandic` referenced removed `innovation_development_01`, `bm_tradition_verbudir` used removed 1.20 tradeport parameters, and the `monaco` Blóðtrú holy site referenced removed `b_monaco`.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **1.21 Modifier Migration:**
  - Replaced `development_growth` / `development_growth_factor` / `character_capital_county_monthly_development_growth_add` with `prosperity_growth` / `prosperity_growth_factor` / `character_capital_county_monthly_prosperity_growth_add` across `bm_blood_temple_01..07`, `bm_tradition_althingi`, `dynasty_blood_fortune_modifier_1..3`, and `lifeforce_modifier_fortunate`.
  - Replaced `monthly_county_control_growth_add` with `monthly_control_change_when_below_baseline_add` and added `control_baseline_add` (`+2`..`+15` on `bm_blood_temple_01..07`, `+5` on `bm_tradition_althingi`).
  - Replaced `county_opinion_add` with `popular_opinion_add` on `drained_beggar_holding` and `different_faith_county_opinion_mult` with `different_faith_popular_opinion_mult` on `bm_blodtru_identity_doctrine`.
- **1.21 Districts, Port Parameters, Holy Sites & Innovations:**
  - Moved `type = duchy_capital` out of `bm_duchy_buildings.txt` into a modular `common/districts/bm_duchy_districts.txt` (`bm_blood_temple_district`) so Blood Temples remain constructible in 1.21 duchy capital slots.
  - Updated `bm_tradition_verbudir` to 1.21 Port District parameters (`next_level_port_districts`, `port_district_enabled_for_tribals`, `port_district_stationed_maa_bonus`, `seafarers_port_building_slot`, `seafarers_port_maa_control`, `maritime_mercantilism_coastal_districts`).
  - Updated `monaco` holy site (`common/religion/holy_site_types/bm_europe_holy_sites.txt`) from `c_genoa` / `b_monaco` to `c_savona` / `b_ventimiglia` (province `2022`).
  - Updated `history/cultures/bm_icelandic.txt` from `innovation_development_01` to `innovation_prosperity_01`.
- **Merchant & Trade Support:**
  - Enabled `bm_domicile_blood_shrine_01..05` for `merchant_company` and `east_asian_estate` domiciles.
  - Added 1.21 district/trade bonuses to `lifeforce_modifier_fortunate` (`district_build_gold_cost`/`speed`, `trade_competitiveness_mult`, `trade_capacity_mult`), `dynasty_blood_fortune_modifier_1..3` (`district_build_gold_cost`, `monthly_income_from_trade_mult`, `trade_competitiveness_mult`, and `+1` `max_trade_routes` at Tier III), and `bm_stave_nabrokarstafur` (`+10%` trade income and trade competitiveness).

### Other changes

- Updated `scripts/check_modifiers.py`, `scripts/vanilla_all_definitions.json`, and `scripts/modifiers_{character,county,domicile}_allowlist.json` for CK3 1.21 (`2,853` definitions; added `county_holder_character_modifier` and `faith_character_modifier` block coverage).
- Synced `AGENTS.md`, `.agents/rules/`, `RELEASE_NOTES.md`, and `docs-ai/architecture/` specs (`AGENTS.md`, `blood-mage-character-modifiers.md`, `blood-mage-blood-runes.md`, `blood-mage-culture.md`, `blood-mage-dynasty.md`, `blood-mage-lifeforce.md`, `blood-mage-religions.md`, `blood-mage-shared-scripting.md`) to CK3 1.21.
