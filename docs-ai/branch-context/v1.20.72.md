# v1.20.72: Trim Trait Track Tooltips & Introduce Mojo Lifeforce Subsystem

## Motivation

- Tooltip bloat and stat duplication: Blood Mage trait tracks had accumulated 4–6 overlapping modifiers per 10-XP step (flat and percentage prestige/piety, double-dipped scheme success and defense, redundant vassal and general opinion), causing overwhelming UI tooltips.
- Decouple active fertility from combat Lifeforce: Standard Lifeforce modifiers (`minor`, `major`, `superior`) and the base trait tracks awarded passive fertility years, making ancient immortals effortlessly fertile while blurring the line between combat vitality and lineage cultivation.
- Dedicated lineage fuel: Consolidate active fertility, congenital inheritance chance, and trait strengthening into a dedicated, stackable resource ("Mojo") with controlled piety-gated transmutation loops.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Trait Track Tooltip Trim (`common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt`)**:
  - Streamlined all 6 lifestyle tracks across `lifestyle_bm_acolyte`, `lifestyle_blood_mage`, and `lifestyle_bm_archmage` to 2–3 distinct bonuses per track.
  - Removed `years_of_fertility` from repeating 10-XP track steps and from base Mage (+30) and Archmage (+50) definition blocks.
  - Cut `positive_inactive_inheritance_chance` from the `bloodline` track, preserving `dynasty` in `lifestyle_blood_power` as the primary passive genetic progression.
  - Track benefits per 10 XP:
    - `ancient`: Vitality + Monthly Renown Mult % (+ Monthly Legitimacy Add at 20, 40, 60, 80).
    - `enlightenment`: Vitality + Monthly Piety Mult %.
    - `benediction`: Vitality + General Opinion (+ Monthly Prestige Mult % at 20, 40, 60, 80).
    - `bloodline`: Vitality + Domain Tax Mult % (+ Monthly Renown Mult % at 20, 40, 60, 80).
    - `hematurgy`: Vitality + Hostile Scheme Success (+ Enemy Hostile Scheme Success at 20, 40, 60, 80).
    - `bloodlust`: Vitality + Knight Effectiveness % (+ Sworn Knight Limit at 20, 40, 60, 80).
- **Mojo Lifeforce Subsystem (`common/modifiers/bm_lifeforce.txt`)**:
  - Added stackable `lifeforce_modifier_mojo`: `+10%` fertility, `+5` fertility years, `+5%` inactive inheritance chance, `+5%` genetic trait chance, `+5%` strengthen blood chance, `+5` attraction opinion.
  - Stripped `years_of_fertility` from standard Lifeforce modifiers (`minor`, `major`, `superior`), negative exhaustion modifiers (`negative_minor`, `negative_major`, `negative_superior`), and `lifedrained_modifier`.
- **Transmutation & Conversion Loop (`common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_cast_blood_magic.txt`)**:
  - Added `bm_minor_channel_mojo`: spend 50 Piety to transmute 1 Minor Lifeforce into 1 Mojo Lifeforce.
  - Added `bm_major_channel_mojo`: spend 100 Piety to transmute 1 Major Lifeforce into 3 Mojo Lifeforce.
  - Added `bm_minor_reclaim_mojo`: spend 75 Piety to dissolve 1 Mojo Lifeforce into 1 Minor Lifeforce.
  - Anti-exploit piety gate ensures conversion loops cannot generate free Lifeforce or Piety.
- **Transfers & Blessings (`common/character_interactions/bm_blood_blessing.txt`)**:
  - Dynamically integrated Mojo into `blessing_grant_lifeforce`: mages can bestow 1 Mojo stack directly upon spouse, consort, lover, or kin (costs 25 Piety, awards +1 `bloodline` XP).
  - Retired legacy `blessing_consecrate_union` and replaced Crucible fallback in `blessing_bless_blood` with 1 Mojo stack.
- **Childbirth Consumption (`common/on_action/bm_blood_mage_prevalence_on_actions.txt`)**:
  - When a child is born, each Blood Mage parent holding Mojo consumes 1 stack to grant +3 `bloodline` XP and a +15% trait awakening roll (+30% if both parents hold Mojo).
- **Story Panel Integration (`common/scripted_effects/bm_blood_mage_story_list_effects.txt`)**:
  - Added `lifeforce_modifier_mojo` counting and display to `bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect` (`var:bm_lifeforce_mojo_stacks`).
