# v1.20.79: Trait Appearance on All Tiers & Standardized Scheme & Army Scaling

## Motivation

- Portrait alteration modifiers for hair and eyes were restricted to Tier II Blood Mage (`lifestyle_blood_mage`), ignoring Tier I Blood Acolyte (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`) and Tier III Blood Archmage (`lifestyle_bm_archmage`).
  - Characters starting as Acolytes or ascending to Archmages failed to render white hair or supernatural eyes regardless of the `bm_physical_alteration` game rule setting.
- Scheme potential (hostile, personal, enemy hostile) and Army Damage scaling across Blood Mage lifestyle tracks (`hematurgy`, `benediction`, `bloodlust`) were uneven (e.g. +12% / +8% / -16% / +12% at 100 XP on Acolyte).
  - Standardized all 4 percentage modifiers to scale cleanly and predictably across tiers: +15% / -15% at 100 XP on Acolyte (+3% / -3% per 10/30/50/70/100 threshold), +30% / -30% on Mage (+6% / -6% per threshold, +9% / -9% base floor), and +50% / -50% on Archmage (+10% / -10% per threshold, +27% / -27% base floor).

## Changes

### Key changes

- Trait portrait modifiers:
  - Added `lifestyle_bm_acolyte` and `lifestyle_bm_archmage` alongside `lifestyle_blood_mage` to `traits` filters across all tiers of hair alteration (`bm_blood_mage_divine_hair_3` through `_5`) and eye alteration (`bm_blood_mage_divine_eyes_1` through `_5`).
  - Restored full appearance changes (white eyes and white hair) under game rule `On` (`bm_physical_alteration_on`) and progressive threshold scaling under game rule `Gradual` (`bm_physical_alteration_gradual`) across all three lifestyle tiers.
- Standardized Trait Track & Base Floor Progression (`common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt`):
  - **Blood Acolyte (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`)**:
    - `hematurgy`: +3% Hostile Scheme Potential (`owned_hostile_scheme_success_chance_max_add`) and -3% Enemy Hostile Scheme Potential (`enemy_hostile_scheme_success_chance_max_add`) across all 5 thresholds (10, 30, 50, 70, 100 XP) -> +15% / -15% total at 100 XP.
    - `benediction`: +3% Personal Scheme Potential (`owned_personal_scheme_success_chance_max_add`) across all 5 thresholds -> +15% total at 100 XP.
    - `bloodlust`: +3% Army Damage (`army_damage_mult = 0.03`) across all 5 thresholds -> +15% total at 100 XP.
  - **Blood Mage (`lifestyle_blood_mage`)**:
    - Base trait anti-nerf floor: Absorbs 50 XP Tier I progression at +9% Personal Scheme, +9% Hostile Scheme, -9% Enemy Hostile Scheme, and +9% Army Damage.
    - `hematurgy`: +6% Hostile Scheme Potential and -6% Enemy Hostile Scheme Potential across all 5 thresholds -> +30% / -30% track total at 100 XP (+39% / -39% combined total).
    - `benediction`: +6% Personal Scheme Potential across all 5 thresholds -> +30% track total at 100 XP (+39% combined total).
    - `bloodlust`: +6% Army Damage (`army_damage_mult = 0.06`) across all 5 thresholds -> +30% track total at 100 XP (+39% combined total).
  - **Blood Archmage (`lifestyle_bm_archmage`)**:
    - Base trait anti-nerf floor: Absorbs Tier I + Tier II 50 XP progression at +27% Personal Scheme, +27% Hostile Scheme, -27% Enemy Hostile Scheme, and +27% Army Damage.
    - `hematurgy`: +10% Hostile Scheme Potential and -10% Enemy Hostile Scheme Potential across all 5 thresholds -> +50% / -50% track total at 100 XP (+77% / -77% combined total).
    - `benediction`: +10% Personal Scheme Potential across all 5 thresholds -> +50% track total at 100 XP (+77% combined total).
    - `bloodlust`: +10% Army Damage (`army_damage_mult = 0.10`) across all 5 thresholds -> +50% track total at 100 XP (+77% combined total).
