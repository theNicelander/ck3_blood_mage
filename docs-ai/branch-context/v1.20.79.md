# v1.20.79: Apply Supernatural Hair & Eye Appearance to All Blood Mage Tiers

## Motivation

- Portrait alteration modifiers for hair and eyes were restricted to Tier II Blood Mage (`lifestyle_blood_mage`), ignoring Tier I Blood Acolyte (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`) and Tier III Blood Archmage (`lifestyle_bm_archmage`).
  - Characters starting as Acolytes or ascending to Archmages failed to render white hair or supernatural eyes regardless of the `bm_physical_alteration` game rule setting.

## Changes

### Key changes

- Trait portrait modifiers:
  - Added `lifestyle_bm_acolyte` and `lifestyle_bm_archmage` alongside `lifestyle_blood_mage` to `traits` filters across all tiers of hair alteration (`bm_blood_mage_divine_hair_3` through `_5`) and eye alteration (`bm_blood_mage_divine_eyes_1` through `_5`).
  - Restored full appearance changes (white eyes and white hair) under game rule `On` (`bm_physical_alteration_on`) and progressive threshold scaling under game rule `Gradual` (`bm_physical_alteration_gradual`) across all three lifestyle tiers.

### Other changes

- Synchronized `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-game-rules.md` to reflect portrait modifications applying to all three Blood Mage tiers.
- Purged deprecated legacy references to "golden-blonde hair" and "Blood Knights" from appearance game rule localization descriptions.
