# v1.20.22

## Summary & Motivation

Branch `v1.20.22` executes an aggressive overhaul across the entire mod codebase, systematically renaming all occurrences of "Crimson", "Sanguine", and related color terms to "Blood":
- Renames secondary lifestyle trait `lifestyle_crimson_empowerment` $\rightarrow$ `lifestyle_blood_empowerment` ("Blood Empowerment").
- Renames trait file, event file, scripted triggers, and XP helper effects (`add_blood_empowerment_xp`).
- Overhauls Blood Runes subsystem: namespace `bm_blood_rune`, modifiers `minor_blood_rune_modifier`, `major_blood_rune_modifier`, `superior_blood_rune_modifier`.
- Overhauls Dynastic modifiers: `dynasty_crimson_*` $\rightarrow$ `dynasty_blood_*` (Charisma, Fury, Prosperity, Shadows, Insight, Resilience, Legacy, Expertise).
- Overhauls Domicile buildings: `bm_domicile_sanguine_font_*` $\rightarrow$ `bm_domicile_blood_shrine_*` (Blood Shrine), aura modifiers `bm_blood_shrine_aura_tier_*`, triggers, on-actions.
- Overhauls Story panel and retinue: variable list `bm_crimson_retinue` $\rightarrow$ `bm_blood_retinue` and label `BM_BLOOD_RETINUE` ("Blood Knights and Champions").
- Overhauls Blóðtrú religion: holy orders `holy_order_knights_of_the_blood_chalice`, `holy_order_blood_covenant`, `holy_order_blood_disciples`, `holy_order_blood_tide`, `holy_order_blood_vanguard`, deities ("The Blood Sustainer", "The Blood Mother", "The Blood Hoard", "The Blood Shadow", "The Nocturnal Blood-Drainer", "The Blood Tide"), and religious head ("The Blood Conclave").
- Reorganizes self decisions into `bm_cast_blood_magic_major.txt` and `bm_cast_blood_magic_minor.txt`.

## Core Concepts & High-Level Changes

- **Trait & XP Progression**:
  - `lifestyle_blood_empowerment` replaces `lifestyle_crimson_empowerment`.
  - `add_blood_empowerment_xp` replaces `add_crimson_empowerment_xp` with backward-compatible alias.
  - `bm_has_blood_empowerment_xp_trigger` checks track XP.
- **Rune System**:
  - `bm_blood_rune.001` event and `bm_blood_rune` namespace.
  - Modifiers `minor_blood_rune_modifier`, `major_blood_rune_modifier`, `superior_blood_rune_modifier`.
- **Domicile External Buildings**:
  - 5-tier Blood Shrine (`bm_domicile_blood_shrine_01`..`05`).
  - Domicile parameters `bm_blood_shrine_tier_1`..`5`.
  - Courtier aura modifiers `bm_blood_shrine_aura_tier_1`..`5`.
- **Religion Architecture**:
  - Blóðtrú holy orders and divine epithets standardized on Blood terminology.
