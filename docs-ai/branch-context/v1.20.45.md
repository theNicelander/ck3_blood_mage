# v1.20.45: Trait Track Rebalance: Ancient Health Penalty, Bloodline Renown, & Blood Knight Devotion Prowess

## Summary & Motivation
Clean up and rebalance lifestyle trait tracks across Blood Mage (`lifestyle_blood_mage`) and Blood Knight (`lifestyle_blood_knight`):
1. Remove `negate_health_penalty_add` modifier from the `ancient` track.
2. Remove recurring flat `monthly_dynasty_prestige = 0.25` across all incremental tiers of the `bloodline` track, replacing it with a 50 XP milestone granting `+10%` Dynasty Renown Mult (`monthly_dynasty_prestige_mult = 0.10`) and `+10` Dynasty Opinion (`dynasty_opinion = 10`).
3. Restrict devotion prowess scaling (`prowess_per_piety_level = 1` at 50 XP) exclusively to the 2x Blood Knight specific tracks (`vanguard` and `slaughter`), removing it from shared tracks (`ancient`, `benediction`, `enlightenment`) and Blood Mage tracks.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Ancient Track Health Penalty Removal**: Completely removed `negate_health_penalty_add = 0.25` (at 50 XP) and `negate_health_penalty_add = 0.5` (at 100 XP) from `ancient` track in `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight`.
- **Bloodline Track Dynasty Renown & Kinship**: Replaced flat personal prestige spam across tiers 10–90 with a milestone reward at 50 XP in `bloodline`:
  - `monthly_dynasty_prestige_mult = 0.10` (+10% Dynasty Renown generation).
  - `dynasty_opinion = 10` (cohesive dynasty bonds).
  - Preserved existing domain & governance powers (+3 Domain Limit, +30% Income, +15 Vassal Limit, build speed/cost, development growth, and +1.5 Stewardship/Piety).
- **Devotion Prowess Scoped to Blood Knight Specific Tracks**:
  - `lifestyle_blood_knight`: `vanguard` and `slaughter` grant `prowess_per_piety_level = 1` at 50 XP milestone.
  - Shared tracks (`ancient`, `benediction`, `enlightenment`) and Blood Mage specific tracks (`bloodline`, `hematurgy`): Do not grant `prowess_per_piety_level`.
- **Architecture Documentation**: Updated `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-traits.md` cumulative benefit tables for both traits to reflect the updated milestones and track stats.

## Key Decisions
- **Martial Exclusivity**: Scoped `prowess_per_piety_level` strictly to the martial Blood Knight class specific tracks (`vanguard`, `slaughter`), preserving thematic identity between martial knights and spellcasters/shared disciplines.
- **Dynasty Renown over Flat Prestige**: Replacing flat monthly prestige with a dynasty renown multiplier and dynasty opinion grounds `bloodline` in actual dynastic prestige and house cohesion rather than personal currency generation.
- **Minimal Ancient Track**: Preserved `ancient` track at 50 XP with pure Level of Devotion Impact (`piety_level_impact_mult = 0.175`) and 100 XP with Apex Devotion capstone per user preference.
