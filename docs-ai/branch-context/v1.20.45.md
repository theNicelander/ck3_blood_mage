# v1.20.45: Trait Track Rebalance: Ancient Health Penalty, Bloodline Prestige, & Blood Knight Devotion Prowess

## Summary & Motivation
Clean up and rebalance lifestyle trait tracks across Blood Mage (`lifestyle_blood_mage`) and Blood Knight (`lifestyle_blood_knight`):
1. Remove `negate_health_penalty_add` modifier from the `ancient` track.
2. Remove `monthly_dynasty_prestige = 0.25` from the `bloodline` track.
3. Restrict devotion prowess scaling (`prowess_per_piety_level = 1` at 50 XP) exclusively to the 2x Blood Knight specific tracks (`vanguard` and `slaughter`), removing it from shared tracks (`ancient`, `benediction`, `enlightenment`) and Blood Mage tracks.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Ancient Track Health Penalty Removal**: Completely removed `negate_health_penalty_add = 0.25` (at 50 XP) and `negate_health_penalty_add = 0.5` (at 100 XP) from `ancient` track in `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight`.
- **Bloodline Track Dynasty Prestige Removal**: Removed `monthly_dynasty_prestige = 0.25` across all tiers (10, 20, 30, 40, 60, 70, 80, 90) in the `bloodline` track of `lifestyle_blood_mage`.
- **Devotion Prowess Scoped to Blood Knight Specific Tracks**:
  - `lifestyle_blood_knight`: `vanguard` and `slaughter` grant `prowess_per_piety_level = 1` at 50 XP milestone.
  - Shared tracks (`ancient`, `benediction`, `enlightenment`) and Blood Mage specific tracks (`bloodline`, `hematurgy`): Do not grant `prowess_per_piety_level`.
- **Architecture Documentation**: Updated `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-traits.md` cumulative benefit tables for both traits to reflect the removed dynasty prestige, health penalty negation, and the restricted prowess scaling.

## Key Decisions
- **Martial Exclusivity**: Scoped `prowess_per_piety_level` strictly to the martial Blood Knight class specific tracks (`vanguard`, `slaughter`), preserving thematic identity between martial knights and spellcasters/shared disciplines.
- **Bloodline Economy Focus**: Stripped recurring dynasty prestige gain from the `bloodline` track to focus on domain building, tax income, and vassal capacity.
