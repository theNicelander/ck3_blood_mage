# v1.21.13: Blood Golems, Blood Artifacts, Mage Drain Shield & AI Trait Drain Rebalance
PR: #208

## Motivation
- Implement valid feature ideas and balance fixes from historical Steam feedback alongside AI trait-draining tuning.
  - Give Blood Golems a dedicated decaying construct trait and portrait look instead of stacked vanilla traits.
  - Make Major Lifedrain against Blood Mages respect their stored Lifeforce reserves and reward executing higher-tier mages.
  - Add forgeable Blood Magic artifacts with a late-game tempering duel against Egill.
  - Stop AI Blood Mages from turning into giants indiscriminately or draining traits at ~95%+ regardless of Piety, Health, or personality.

## Changes

### Key changes
- **Blood Golem construct trait & appearance** (`bm_blood_golem`): replaced legacy `giant`/`albino`/`beardless_eunuch` on golems with a 10-step decaying construct trait and custom portrait modifiers.
  - Spawns at 100 XP (`+10` Life Exp, `+1` Prowess, `+0.2` Health per 10 XP step; `no_prowess_loss_from_age`, `+15` Epidemic Resistance, `+2` base Prowess, `-100%` Stress Gain, `+15` Dread, marriage/inheritance blocked).
  - Decays `-2` XP yearly; refuelable via Cast Blood Blessing Grant Lifeforce (`+20` Minor / `+50` Major / `+100` Superior) and interpersonal feeding (`+25` / `+15`).
  - Added `bm_blood_golem_physique` and `bm_blood_golem_appearance` portrait modifiers (giant stature, pale scaly skin, blood-red eyes, hairless; gated by `bm_physical_alteration` game rule).
  - Consolidated golem checks into `bm_is_blood_golem_trigger` across rosters, events, AI vetoes, and rune targeting.
- **Blood Mage Lifeforce shield & tier harvest** (`bm_major_lifedrain_execute_or_shield_effect`): Major Lifedrain and Mass Lifedrain now interact with a victim mage's stored Lifeforce.
  - Draining a Blood Mage with stored Lifeforce strips and transfers 1 stack of their highest Lifeforce, wounding and sparing them until dry.
  - Executing a dry Blood Mage grants tier-scaled bonus Lifeforce (`+1` Minor / `+1` Major / `+1` Superior) and Ancient/Wisdom XP, with minor backlash when draining a higher-tier mage.
- **Blood Magic Artifacts & Egill tempering duel** (`bm_forge_blood_artifact_decision`, `bm_temper_blood_artifact_decision`): craftable discipline artifacts with an upgrade duel.
  - Forge Masterwork artifact across 5 slots x 6 disciplines (`250` Piety, `100` Gold, `1` Major Lifeforce, 3-yr cooldown).
  - Temper at Reykjavik or Tsushima (`250` Piety, `1` Superior Lifeforce, 5-yr cooldown) via Prowess SCE or Learning duel vs Egill to upgrade to Illustrious with a Tempered discipline modifier.
- **Egill refactor**: shared `bm_create_egill_effect`/`bm_vanish_egill_effect` (`bm_egill_effects.txt`) for geyser and tempering duels; fixes tempering Egill leaking each attempt; dropped dead `dynasty_myramenn` branch.
- **AI trait draining rebalance** (`bm_trait_drain.001`, `bm_blood_drain_interaction`, `seek_power.003.b`): differentiated per-trait weights and added Piety, Health, and personality guards.
  - Scaled AI decline weight (`base = 60`, `-30` ambitious/callous/sadistic/deceitful, `+100` compassionate/content/just) for ~60% / ~40% / ~20% drain rates.
  - Restricted `giant` (`base = 5`, `+15` only for martial/prowess/wrathful/brave, vetoed on `health <= good_health`/craven/weak/physique_bad) and gated `fecund` on fertility, celibacy, eunuch, and female age 45+.
  - Added `health <= fine_health`, `lifeforce_modifier_negative_major`, and Piety affordability vetoes; lowered `seek_power.003.b` from `500` to `50` and `drain_trait` bonus from `+20` to `+10`.
  - Aligned AGOT, LotR, and EK2 trait-drain events to the same weight scale, safety guards, and `is_ai = no` back-button loop prevention.
- **Blóðtrú sin fix** (`blodtru_religion`): removed `witch` from `sins` so it no longer contradicts `doctrine_witchcraft_accepted`.
