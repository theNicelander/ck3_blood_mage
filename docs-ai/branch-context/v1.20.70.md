# v1.20.70: Devotion Prowess Scaling Floor & Trait Capstone Age Lock Polish

## Motivation

- Martial Blood Mages suffered a combat prowess regression upon elevating from Blood Acolyte to Blood Mage or Archmage.
  - While other attributes had their lower-tier 50 XP Devotion milestones absorbed into the higher-tier base trait floors (`diplomacy_per_piety_level`, `martial_per_piety_level`, etc.), `prowess_per_piety_level` was missing from the base stats of Mage and Archmage, causing characters with Bloodlust XP to drop in prowess when blank-slate elevation reset their tracks.
- Redundant track definitions cluttered higher-tier trait capstones.
  - All six 100 XP blocks on Mage and Archmage included `no_prowess_loss_from_age = yes` despite both base traits already providing the age prowess lock permanently.
- Blood Acolytes and Archmages were locked out of adopting the Blóðtrú faith.
  - The decision to embrace the faith (`become_blood_cultist_decision`) checked for the legacy `lifestyle_blood_mage` trait only, hiding and blocking conversion for newly initiated Blood Acolytes and ascended Archmages.

## Changes

### Key changes

- Devotion Prowess Anti-Nerf Floors:
  - Added `prowess_per_piety_level = 1` to Tier II Blood Mage (`lifestyle_blood_mage`) base stats, matching the absorbed Tier I Bloodlust 50 XP milestone.
  - Added `prowess_per_piety_level = 2` to Tier III Blood Archmage (`lifestyle_bm_archmage`) base stats, matching the absorbed Tier II Bloodlust 50 XP milestone.
- Trait Capstone Age Lock Deduplication:
  - Removed `no_prowess_loss_from_age = yes` from all six 100 XP blocks (`bloodline`, `hematurgy`, `benediction`, `enlightenment`, `bloodlust`, `ancient`) on `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_bm_archmage`.
  - Retained `no_prowess_loss_from_age = yes` across all six 100 XP capstones on Tier I Blood Acolyte (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`) as an impactful milestone unlock.
- Blóðtrú Faith Conversion for All Mage Tiers:
  - Updated `is_shown` and `is_valid` in `become_blood_cultist_decision` to check `is_blood_mage_any_tier_trigger = yes`, enabling Blood Acolytes, Blood Mages, and Blood Archmages to embrace the Blóðtrú faith.

### Other changes

- Synchronized `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-traits.md`, `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-decisions.md`, and `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-religions.md`.
