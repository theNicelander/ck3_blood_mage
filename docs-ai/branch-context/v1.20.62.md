# v1.20.62: Standardize Event Exit Options, Reduce Cooldowns & Lower Berserker Provisions

## Motivation

- Multi-stage spell menus, diagnostics, and ritual events used inconsistent, verbose flavour text on cancellation and back-out options, cluttering dialogue menus.
- Five-year cooldowns on Manifest Congenital Traits and Blood Rune inscription, alongside 3-year wanderer cooldowns on Blood Hunt, restricted character pacing across lifespans.
- Blood Berserker men-at-arms had excessive provision upkeep (7 provisions), placing a heavy logistical drain on landless Blood Mages and Knights on the move.

## Changes

### Key changes

- Reduce Manifest Congenital Traits cooldown to 2 years (down from 5 years).
  - Shortens `bm_manifest_traits_cooldown` flag duration to 2 years across all 6 manifestation options in `events/bm_channel_manifest_traits_events.txt`.
- Reduce Inscribe Blood Rune cooldown to 3 years (down from 5 years).
  - Shortens self-inscription cooldown flag `bm_blood_rune_cooldown` to 3 years across Minor, Major, and Superior tiers in `common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_cast_blood_magic.txt`.
  - Shortens other-inscription cooldown flag `bm_blood_rune_other_cooldown` to 3 years in `common/character_interactions/bm_blood_blessing.txt`.
- Standardize Blood Hunt cooldown to 1 year for all characters (landed and unlanded/wanderers).
  - Unifies `bm_seek_power_cooldown_days` (365 days) and `bm_seek_power_cooldown_years` (1 year) in `common/script_values/bm_blood_mage_story_values.txt`, halved to 6 months (~182 days) with Superior Vegvísir stave.
- Lower Blood Berserkers provision cost from 7 to 5.
  - Decreases `@provisions_cost_infantry_moderate` in `common/men_at_arms_types/bm_men_at_arms_types.txt` to align provision usage with moderate infantry standards.
- Standardize all ritual, healing, and spell event cancellation and back-out options to concise `"Back"`.
  - Replaces verbose flavour text across `bm_heal_ailment`, `bm_heal_old_age`, `bm_reshape_personality`, `bm_channel_manifest_traits`, and `bm_bestow_traits`.

### Other changes

- Update AI guidelines in `AGENTS.md`, `.agents/rules/ck3-localization.md`, and `.agents/rules/ck3-events.md` establishing `"Back"` as the strict project standard for all menu navigation and exit options.
- Sync architecture documentation across `blood-mage-blood-runes.md`, `blood-mage-decisions.md`, `blood-mage-duels-and-education.md`, `blood-mage-lifeforce.md`, `blood-mage-magic-tiers.md`, `blood-mage-track-xp-knight.md`, `blood-mage-track-xp-mage.md`, and `blood-mage-track-xp.md`.
- Document reference lifestyle track design patterns from *Additional Lifestyles* mod in `docs-ai/ideas/additional-lifestyles-inspiration.md`.
