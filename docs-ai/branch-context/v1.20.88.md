# v1.20.88: Rebalance Lifeforce Reservoirs & House Covenants

## Motivation

- Stacking specialized Lifeforce reservoirs needed broader, smaller per-stack bonuses without granting core attribute stats (`learning = 1` on Wise Blood) or inflating lifestyle XP (`+5` -> `+3`).
  - Spreads each reservoir's identity across wider mechanics (reign stability, siege/army damage, all construction cost/speed, scheme caps/discovery, piety/devotion scaling, dynasty renown).
- House Covenants needed monthly lifestyle XP, lifespan bonuses, simpler yearly track XP, active AI adoption by both House Heads and house members, and an initiation covenant for house kin.

## Changes

### Key changes

- Rebalanced all 6 specialized Lifeforce reservoir modifiers (`common/modifiers/bm_lifeforce.txt`):
  - **All 6**: `+3` monthly lifestyle XP for the matching track (`monthly_lifestyle_xp_gain_add = 3` for Ancient).
  - **`lifeforce_modifier_radiant`**: `-5%` Short Reign, `+5%` Long Reign, `+5%` Legitimacy Gain, `+5%` Monthly Prestige Mult, `+5%` Fame Impact, `-5d` Personal Scheme Phase, `+3%` Personal Scheme Success, `+3%` Max Personal Scheme Cap, `+3` Vassal Opinion.
  - **`lifeforce_modifier_furious`**: `+1` Prowess, `+5%` Army Movement Speed, `+5%` Army Damage, `-5%` Siege Phase Time, `+0.1%` Knight Eff per Prowess, `+0.01` Piety/Knight, `+5%` Counter Efficiency.
  - **`lifeforce_modifier_fortunate`**: `+2%` Domain Tax Mult, `-3%` Building/Holding/Domicile Gold Cost & Construction Time, `+3%` Development Growth, `-3%` MaA Maintenance.
  - **`lifeforce_modifier_cunning`**: `-3%` Max Enemy Hostile Scheme Cap, `-5d` Hostile Scheme Phase, `+3%` Scheme Secrecy, `+3%` Hostile Scheme Success, `+3%` Max Hostile Scheme Cap, `+5%` Scheme Discovery Chance, `+3` Dread Baseline.
  - **`lifeforce_modifier_wise`**: removed `learning = 1`; `+5%` Monthly Piety Mult, `+5%` Devotion Impact, `+3%` Monthly Lifestyle XP Mult, `-5%` Stress Gain, `+5%` Stress Loss, `+0.1%` Knight Eff per Learning, `-5d` Learn Language Phase.
  - **`lifeforce_modifier_ancient`**: `+5%` Fertility, `+5` Fertility Yrs, `+5%` Inactive Inheritance / Random Genetic / Strengthen Blood Chance, `+5%` Monthly Renown Mult, `+3` Dynasty Opinion, `-5%` Inbreeding Chance, `+3` Attraction Opinion.
- Moved **Lifeforce Attunement** out of standalone `bm_attune_lifeforce_decision` into `bm_cast_blood_magic_minor_decision` (`bm_minor_attune` -> `bm_attune_lifeforce.001` with `Back` option), standardized at `50` Piety + `1` Minor Lifeforce + `1`-year cooldown (`bm_attune_lifeforce_cooldown`) for all attunements, and updated `bm_action_can_attune_lifeforce` to point to `bm_cast_blood_magic_minor_decision`.
- **House Covenants (`bm_channel_dynasty_modifiers.txt`, `bm_empower_bloodline_decision.txt`, `blood_mage_yearly_events.002`)**:
  - Added monthly lifestyle XP (`+2 / +4 / +6` per tier for Radiance/Fury/Fortune/Cunning/Wisdom; `+1 / +2 / +4` all-lifestyle XP for Ancient) and `+2 / +4 / +6` `life_expectancy` to Radiance/Fury/Fortune/Cunning/Wisdom (Ancient keeps `+2 / +4 / +8`).
  - Updated `blood_mage_yearly_events.002` yearly Blood Mage track XP to `0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5` avg per year (Tier I: 50% `+1`; Tier II: 100% `+1`; Tier III: 100% `+1` + 50% `+1`).
  - Enabled active AI adoption (`ai_check_interval = 6`, `ai_will_do = { base = 100 }`, removed `health <= fine_health` veto) for both House Heads and Blood Mage house members.
  - Added **Covenant of Initiation** (`dynasty_blood_acolyte_modifier`): one-off covenant costing `350` Piety + Major Lifeforce (Devotion 5+, Blood Mage or Archmage) granting `+1` Monthly Piety and unlocking `bm_covenant_acolyte_decision` (`common/decisions/become_mage/bm_covenant_acolyte_decision.txt`) for adult house members (`piety_level >= 3`, `250` Piety, active AI) to become Blood Mage Acolytes.
