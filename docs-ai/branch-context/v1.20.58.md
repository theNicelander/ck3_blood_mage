# v1.20.58: Blood Universities Regular Building Conversion & Growth Overhaul

## Summary & Motivation
Overhaul Blood Universities (`bm_buildings.txt`) from restricted duchy capital holdings into standard regular buildings constructible in all holding types (castles, cities, temples, and tribal settlements). Replace personal cultural fascination and lifestyle XP multipliers with robust development and control growth engines: flat development growth (`+0.1` to `+0.4`), percentage development growth factors (`+5%` to `+20%`), and monthly county control growth (`+0.1` to `+0.4`) under `county_modifier`.

## Key Decisions
- **Regular Building vs Duchy Capital**: Duchy capital slots are strictly limited to 1 per duchy, locking out counts and tribal rulers. Converting Blood Universities to regular buildings enables blood mages and knights to cultivate universities across any holding, including tribal lands.
- **Focus on Realm Infrastructure over Character Stats**: Cultural fascination mult and lifestyle XP mult overlap heavily with standard character lifestyles and cultural traditions. Replacing them with flat and percentage development growth alongside county control growth cements Blood Universities as potent county development hubs.
- **Consolidation under `county_modifier`**: As regular buildings, county growth modifiers are placed under `county_modifier` so they affect the county regardless of whether the barony is a duchy capital.
