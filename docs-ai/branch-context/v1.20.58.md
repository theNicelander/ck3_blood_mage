# v1.20.58: Blood Universities Development Growth Overhaul

## Summary & Motivation
Overhaul Duchy Blood Universities (`bm_duchy_buildings.txt`) to replace personal cultural head fascination and lifestyle XP multipliers with robust capital development engines: flat development growth (`+0.1` to `+0.4`) and percentage development growth factors (`+5%` to `+20%`) under `duchy_capital_county_modifier`, while removing redundant province modifier blocks.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Blood Universities Modifier Overhaul (`common/buildings/bm_duchy_buildings.txt`)**:
  - `bm_university_0`: Removed `cultural_head_fascination_mult = 0.05`. Replaced `development_growth = 0.1` in county and province with `duchy_capital_county_modifier = { development_growth = 0.1 development_growth_factor = 0.05 }`.
  - `bm_university_1`: Removed `cultural_head_fascination_mult = 0.10` and `monthly_lifestyle_xp_gain_mult = 0.03`. Replaced county and province growth with `duchy_capital_county_modifier = { development_growth = 0.2 development_growth_factor = 0.10 }`.
  - `bm_university_2`: Removed `cultural_head_fascination_mult = 0.15` and `monthly_lifestyle_xp_gain_mult = 0.06`. Replaced county and province growth with `duchy_capital_county_modifier = { development_growth = 0.3 development_growth_factor = 0.15 }`.
  - `bm_university_3`: Removed `cultural_head_fascination_mult = 0.25` and `monthly_lifestyle_xp_gain_mult = 0.10`. Replaced county and province growth with `duchy_capital_county_modifier = { development_growth = 0.4 development_growth_factor = 0.20 }`.
  - Cleaned up redundant `province_modifier` blocks across all tiers.
- **Documentation & Tracking**:
  - Updated `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-blood-runes.md` university table with the new bonuses.
  - Updated `RELEASE_NOTES.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Key Decisions
- **Focus on Realm Infrastructure over Character Stats**: Cultural fascination mult and lifestyle XP mult overlap heavily with standard character lifestyles and cultural traditions. Replacing them with flat and percentage development growth cements Blood Universities as potent duchy development hubs.
- **Consolidation under `duchy_capital_county_modifier`**: In vanilla CK3, development growth is a county property; duchy capital buildings cleanly place development growth and development growth factors in `duchy_capital_county_modifier`.
