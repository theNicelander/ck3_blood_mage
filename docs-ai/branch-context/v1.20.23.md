# v1.20.23 — Consolidate Cast Magic Decisions

## Summary & Motivation
Reduce decision menu bloat by consolidating individual blood magic casting decisions in `common/decisions/cast_magic/` into two structured hub decisions (`bm_cast_blood_magic_minor_decision` and `bm_cast_blood_magic_major_decision`) that trigger selection events. Standalone creation of Blood Golems remains untouched.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Minor Cast Hub**: `bm_cast_blood_magic_minor_decision` opens event `bm_cast_blood_magic_minor.001`, offering Channel Minor Lifeforce (75 piety), Lifeforce Attunement (25 piety), and Cancel.
- **Major Cast Hub**: `bm_cast_blood_magic_major_decision` opens event `bm_cast_blood_magic_major.001`, offering Blood Empowerment (150 piety), Enhance Education (scaled piety), Embrace New Education (1000 piety), Inscribe Blood Runes (piety + rank cost), Empower Dynasty Bloodline (350 piety), and Cancel.
- **Per-Rite Cooldowns**: Education upgrades, body runes, and dynasty bloodline empowerment track individual cooldowns through timed character flags (`bm_improve_education_cooldown`, `bm_new_education_cooldown`, `bm_blood_rune_cooldown`, `bm_channel_bloodline_cooldown`) rather than blocking the master decision.
- **Piety Deductions**: Shifted from decision execution to individual event option triggers and effects; opening either grimoire decision is free.

## Key Decisions
- **Two Hub Decisions over One Unified**: Separated into Minor and Major decisions to align with distinct Lifeforce resource requirements (`lifeforce_modifier_minor` vs `lifeforce_modifier_major`).
- **Blood Golem Kept Standalone**: `blood_golem_creation_decision` kept as an independent decision due to its distinct courtier creation and shaping duel flow.
