# v1.20-9

## Summary & Motivation

Branch `v1.20-9` refactors self-targeted blood magic actions into decisions, integrating fast rituals cleanly into the Situation panel while moving heavy ritual actions to the main decision list, and promotes the Cult of Quintessence faith conversion to a Major Decision:
- Promotes "Embrace the Blood Cultist Faith" (`become_blood_cultist_decision`) to a Major Decision (`decision_group_type = major`).
- Transitions 3 repeatable personal rituals ("Lifeforce Attunement", "Channel Minor Lifeforce", and "Channel Crimson Empowerment") from self-interactions into hidden decisions (`is_invisible = yes`) displayed directly in the Situation window via `bm_blood_mage_story`, removing their cooldowns for smooth management.
- Moves "Harvest lifeforce from prisoners" (`mass_lifedrain_prisoners_decision`) and "Manifest Lifeforce" (`manifest_lifeforce_decision`) into hidden situation decisions (`is_invisible = yes`), and purges all non-hidden decisions from the story cycle to keep the Situation panel compact.
- Transitions 2 major self-rituals ("Enhance Education" and "Inscribe with Blood Runes") from self-interactions into standard player decisions in the main decisions list under `bm_decision_group`.
- Cleans up character interaction files: removes `bm_cast_blood_magic_self_minor.txt` and streamlines `bm_cast_blood_magic_self_major.txt` to contain only secondary education acquisition.

## Core Concepts & High-Level Changes

- **Major Cult Faith Decision**:
  - `become_blood_cultist_decision` categorized under `decision_group_type = major` using `decision_major_religion.dds` so it is prominently displayed in the player's Major Decisions group.

- **Situation-Exclusive Hidden Ritual Decisions**:
  - `manifest_lifeforce_decision`: Converts piety into lifeforce via Learning duel. Hidden from decisions list.
  - `mass_lifedrain_prisoners_decision`: Mass harvest of dungeon captives. Hidden from decisions list.
  - `bm_attune_lifeforce_decision`: Spends minor lifeforce to attune blood magic progression to a selected track. Hidden from decisions list (no cooldown).
  - `bm_channel_minor_lifeforce_decision`: Spends minor lifeforce for short-term attribute bonuses. Hidden from decisions list (no cooldown).
  - `bm_crimson_empowerment_decision`: Spends major lifeforce to awaken and expand Crimson Empowerment traits. Hidden from decisions list (no cooldown).
  - All non-hidden decisions (`seek_power_decision`, `seek_power_decision_wanderer`, `channel_lifeforce_bloodline`, `blood_golem_creation_decision`) removed from the story cycle visualization to keep the Situation panel small and focused.

- **Standard Ritual Decisions in Decision List**:
  - `bm_enhance_education_decision`: Spends major lifeforce and scaled piety to duel arcane forces and upgrade education tiers (retains 2-year cooldown).
  - `bm_inscribe_blood_runes_decision`: Spends major and minor lifeforce to etch permanent magical runes into flesh (retains 5-year cooldown).

- **Interaction File Cleanup**:
  - Deprecated and removed `bm_cast_blood_magic_self_minor.txt`.
  - Removed improved education, blood runes, and crimson empowerment from `bm_cast_blood_magic_self_major.txt`, retaining only `bm_cast_blood_magic_self_major_new_education`.

## Key Decisions

- **Engine-Native `is_invisible = yes` for Situation Panels**:
  - Using CK3's built-in `is_invisible = yes` prevents spam in the main player decisions list while enabling decisions to be clicked directly from the Blood Magic story cycle inside the Situation panel.
- **Cooldown Removal on Minor and Empowerment Channeling**:
  - Allows blood mages to attune or spend available Lifeforce without artificial year-long time gates when they have the necessary vitality on hand.
- **Major Decision Status for Faith Conversion**:
  - Embracing the Cult of Quintessence is a monumental religious shift that warrants prominent placement among Major Decisions.
