# v1.20.48: Dynasty Blood Power Fertile Years Extension

## Summary & Motivation
Adds +3 years of fertility (`years_of_fertility = 3`) to every non-milestone tier (10, 20, 30, 40, 60, 70, 80, 90 XP) of the `dynasty` track in `lifestyle_blood_power`. Over 8 non-milestone tiers, this scales fertility duration by +12 years at the 50 XP milestone and +24 years total at the 100 XP capstone, augmenting the sanguine eugenics and lineage focus of the discipline.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Blood Power Trait Definition (`common/traits/bm_blood_power_trait.txt`)**:
  - Added `years_of_fertility = 3` to tiers 10, 20, 30, 40, 60, 70, 80, and 90 of track `dynasty`. Tiers 50 (milestone) and 100 (capstone) remain clean milestones focused on inbreeding immunity and renown.
- **Localization Updates (`localization/english/`)**:
  - `bm_cast_blood_magic_self_l_english.yml`: Updated `bm_empower_dynasty_desc` to highlight `+3 Years of Fertility` per step and `+24 Years of Fertility` total at the 100 XP capstone.
  - `bm_traits_l_english.yml`: Updated `trait_track_dynasty_desc` to mention extending fertile years alongside genetic refinement.
- **Architecture Updates (`docs-ai/architecture/`)**:
  - `blood-mage-blood-power.md`: Updated Dynasty track benefits table to reflect `+3` Years of Fertility per step and `+24` Years of Fertility total at capstone.
  - `blood-mage-traits.md`: Updated master Blood Power cumulative track benefits table with `+3` Years of Fertility at 10 XP, `+12` at 50 XP, and `+24` at 100 XP.

- **Influence Personality Interaction & Events (`bm_reshape_personality`)**:
  - Renamed interaction from "Harmonize Nature" to "Influence Personality".
  - Implemented Learning challenge duel (`duel = { skill = learning target = scope:recipient ... }`): 50% base chance at equal Learning, scales to 95% at +20 difference, floored at 5% at -20 difference (`multiplier = 2.25`, `min = -45`, `max = 45`).
  - Removed "Cast Major Blood Magic" debuff (`blood_magic_used_major = yes` replaced with direct `remove_character_modifier = lifeforce_modifier_major` and `bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect = yes`).
  - Removed opinion bonus (`+25` `bm_harmonized_mind_opinion`) from success.
  - Added failure resolution: on duel loss, recipient's traits remain intact, caster gains +1 enlightenment XP, and interaction cooldown is shortened to 1 year via `bm_influence_personality_failed_actor` variable while preserving the 5-year cooldown on success.
  - Fixed localization bug: replaced invalid `[actor_ptrait_1.GetNameNoTooltip(root.Self)]` with `[actor_ptrait_1.GetNameNoTooltip( ROOT.Char )]` to prevent script engine syntax errors.

## Key Decisions
- **Step Allocation (+3 Years per Step)**: Added +3 fertile years to each of the 8 incremental steps (tiers 10–40, 60–90) excluding tiers 50 and 100, maintaining milestone cleanliness while yielding a substantial +24 fertile years total across full mastery.
- **Learning Challenge Duel (50% Base, 95% Cap)**: Built duel around Learning contest between caster and recipient. Multiplier of 2.25 ensures +20 difference hits exactly +45 weight (95% win chance) with min/max clamps preventing overflow beyond 95% or below 5%.
- **Differential Cooldown (5 Years Success, 1 Year Failure)**: Succeeded communion retains standard 5-year interaction cooldown. Failed communion clears the 5-year engine cooldown via `remove_interaction_cooldown_against` and applies a 1-year target variable `bm_influence_personality_failed_actor` checked in interaction validation.
- **Debuff & Opinion Removal**: Purged `lifeforce_modifier_negative_major` debuff application and `bm_harmonized_mind_opinion` to keep the mental manipulation focused and unencumbered by vitality penalties.
