# v1.20.48: Blood Power 10 / 20 / 50 / 100 Stepping-Stone Overhaul

## Summary & Motivation
Overhauls all 5 tracks of the secondary lifestyle trait **Blood Power** (`lifestyle_blood_power`) into a clean, rhythmic 10 / 20 / 50 / 100 XP stepping-stone progression. Small steps (10–40, 60–90 XP) scale incremental mastery; medium stepping stones (20, 40, 60, 80 XP) deliver secondary structural milestones (fertile years, personal scheme mastery, fame scaling, MaA maintenance cuts, and enemy plot cap reductions); and major milestones (50 & 100 XP) deliver signature Devotion-scaling skill bonuses (`+1` Skill per Devotion Level = `+2` total at 100 XP), capacity expansions (personal/hostile schemes, domain limit), and universal age-lock capstones.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Blood Power Trait Definition (`common/traits/bm_blood_power_trait.txt`)**:
  - `dynasty`: Small steps (10–40, 60–90) grant `+5%` Positive Inactive Inheritance & Trait Strengthening (`+40%` total). Medium steps (20, 40, 60, 80) grant `+5` Years of Fertility (`+20` years total). 50 & 100 XP grant `-50%` Inbreeding Chance (`-100%` immunity) and `+10%` / `+15%` Monthly Renown (`+25%` total).
  - `mastery`: Small steps grant `+5%` Lifestyle XP Gain (`+40%` total). Medium steps grant `+5` Personal Scheme Success & `-5` Days Personal Scheme Phase Duration (`+20` success, `-20` days total). 50 & 100 XP grant `+1` Learning per Devotion Level (`+2` total, natively granting +2 Language Limit) and `+1` Max Personal Schemes (`+2` total), plus Ignore Negative Culture Opinion at 100 XP.
  - `presence`: Small steps grant `+3` General Opinion & `-5%` Stress Gain (`+24` opinion, `-40%` stress total). Medium steps grant `+5%` Fame Impact (`+20%` total). 50 & 100 XP grant `+1` Diplomacy per Devotion Level (`+2` total), `-10%` Short Reign Duration (`-20%` total), and `+10%` Long Reign Bonus (`+20%` total).
  - `prosperity`: Small steps grant `+5%` Monthly Income, `-3%` Holding Build Cost, and `-3%` Domicile Build Cost (`+40%` income, `-24%` build cost across both landed and unlanded holdings/estates/camps). Medium steps grant `-5%` Men-at-Arms Maintenance (`-20%` total). 50 & 100 XP grant `+1` Stewardship per Devotion Level (`+2` total) and `+1` Domain Limit (`+2` total).
  - `shadows`: Small steps grant `+5` Hostile Scheme Success & `-5` Enemy Hostile Scheme Success (`+40` / `-40` total). Medium steps grant `-5%` Enemy Hostile Scheme Max Success Cap (`-20%` total). 50 & 100 XP grant `+1` Intrigue per Devotion Level (`+2` total), `+1` Max Hostile Schemes (`+2` total), and `+15%` Own Hostile Scheme Max Success Cap at 100 XP.
  - All 5 tracks award `no_prowess_loss_from_age = yes` at 100 XP.

## Key Decisions
- **Ancient Track Cadence & Capstone Inclusion**: Applied `personal_tenet_slot_add = 1` across all 20 XP intervals (20, 40, 60, 80, and 100 XP) yielding +5 personal tenet slots at full mastery, rewarding long-lived characters with spiritual customization slots.
- **Step Allocation (+3 Years per Step)**: Added +3 fertile years to each of the 8 incremental steps (tiers 10–40, 60–90) excluding tiers 50 and 100, maintaining milestone cleanliness while yielding a substantial +24 fertile years total across full mastery.
- **Learning Challenge Duel (50% Base, 95% Cap)**: Built duel around Learning contest between caster and recipient. Multiplier of 2.25 ensures +20 difference hits exactly +45 weight (95% win chance) with min/max clamps preventing overflow beyond 95% or below 5%.
- **Differential Cooldown (5 Years Success, 1 Year Failure)**: Succeeded communion retains standard 5-year interaction cooldown. Failed communion clears the 5-year engine cooldown via `remove_interaction_cooldown_against` and applies a 1-year target variable `bm_influence_personality_failed_actor` checked in interaction validation.
- **Debuff & Opinion Removal**: Purged `lifeforce_modifier_negative_major` debuff application and `bm_harmonized_mind_opinion` to keep the mental manipulation focused and unencumbered by vitality penalties.
- **Education Visibility Exception**: Made education enhancement and mastery an explicit exception to "nothing hidden, but often unavailable". Because characters typically have only 1 (or at most 2) education disciplines, showing all 5 disciplines via `show_as_unavailable` caused severe window overflow and option truncation in the fixed-height event window. Hiding unowned disciplines guarantees that only relevant options and the Back button render cleanly.
- **Streamlined Trait Drain Tooltip**: Wrapped `bm_can_drain_traits_from_target_trigger` in a `custom_description` rather than letting the engine expand all 6 nested `AND`/`OR` branches into the option requirements, keeping the encounter decision clean and readable.
