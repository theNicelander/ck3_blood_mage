# v1.20.48: Dynasty Blood Power Fertile Years Extension

## Summary & Motivation
Adds +3 years of fertility (`years_of_fertility = 3`) to every non-milestone tier (10, 20, 30, 40, 60, 70, 80, 90 XP) of the `dynasty` track in `lifestyle_blood_power`. Over 8 non-milestone tiers, this scales fertility duration by +12 years at the 50 XP milestone and +24 years total at the 100 XP capstone, augmenting the sanguine eugenics and lineage focus of the discipline.

- **Blood Power Trait Definition (`common/traits/bm_blood_power_trait.txt`)**:

## Key Decisions
- **Step Allocation (+3 Years per Step)**: Added +3 fertile years to each of the 8 incremental steps (tiers 10–40, 60–90) excluding tiers 50 and 100, maintaining milestone cleanliness while yielding a substantial +24 fertile years total across full mastery.
