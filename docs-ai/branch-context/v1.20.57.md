# v1.20.57: Blood Power Fury Track, Flat Monthly Lifestyle XP, Blood Shrine & University Calibrations, & Character Interactions Consolidation

## Summary & Motivation
- Consolidate and declutter Blood Magic character interactions—especially on dynasty members, heirs, spouses, and knights—reducing simultaneous right-click interactions from 12–14 down to 4–6 contextual actions.
- Introduce a 6th track, **Fury** (`fury` — Warfare & Command), to the secondary lifestyle trait `lifestyle_blood_power`, providing a dedicated martial specialization covering advantage, army maintenance, siege speed, knight capacity, and Devotion-scaling martial and prowess.
- Implement an engine for granting **flat monthly Lifestyle XP** (not multipliers) across all 6 Blood Power disciplines, paying out silently every 3 months via the native `quarterly_playable_pulse` (+15 XP per quarter = +5 XP/month equivalent per 10 track XP, up to +150 XP/quarter = +50 XP/month at 100 XP) across Diplomacy (`presence`), Martial (`fury`), Stewardship (`prosperity`), Intrigue (`shadows`), Learning (`mastery`), and the currently active lifestyle focus (`dynasty`).
- Increase monthly piety generation on the Blood Shrine external domicile building line from 0.2 to 0.5 per tier (up to +2.5 monthly piety at Tier V for owner).
- Calibrate the radiated companion follower/courtier aura to 50% potency across all stats (piety, health, lifespan, genetics), giving the builder full potency while reasonably empowering court members and camp followers.
- Rework Duchy Blood Universities (`bm_university_0..3`) into a positive landed Piety generation line (+0.5 to +2.5 flat monthly Piety and +5% to +25% monthly Piety mult), remove upfront Piety construction costs and monthly Piety drain, shorten construction times (1, 2, 3, 5 years), and expand eligibility to Blood Mages, Blood Knights, and Blóðtrú rulers to establish Piety parity with wanderers.
- Recalibrate the Seek Power escort knight base prowess to `{ 10 15 }` and convert the post-combat recruitment option in "Clash of Blood and Steel" (`seek_power.006`) into an active Prowess skill duel rather than an automatic courtier recruitment.

## Key Decisions
- **Contextual Interaction Consolidation**: Consolidated 5 overlapping character interactions into multi-option interactions (`bm_bless_bloodline_interaction`, `grant_lifeforce_interaction`, `bm_heal_ailment`) and hid completed blessings and friendly-target hostile spells in `is_shown` to eliminate right-click menu bloat on heirs and dynasty members.
- **Earned Recruitment via Prowess Duel**: Winning a Single Combat bout shouldn't guarantee unquestioned subservience. Converting the recruitment option into a prowess duel ensures recruiting skilled warriors remains an earned martial contest rather than a free courtier generator.
- **No Consolation Rewards on Recruitment Refusal**: When a spared opponent refuses fealty, they withdraw without offering service or lifeforce, preserving high stakes between harvesting them for guaranteed lifeforce vs gambling on courtier recruitment.
- **Tied Flat Lifestyle XP to Blood Power**: Leveraged the existing secondary lifestyle trait rather than adding multiple separate character modifiers, eliminating character sheet clutter while rewarding long-term somatic investment.
- **Quarterly Pulse over Monthly Ticks**: Utilized the engine's native `quarterly_playable_pulse` with 3-month aggregated values (+15 XP per 10 track XP) to maintain clean performance while delivering a steady drip of flat lifestyle XP.
- **Active Focus Dynamic Routing for Dynasty**: Mapped Dynasty to the character's currently active lifestyle focus so the bloodline track directly accelerates whichever discipline the ruler is currently studying.
- **Silent Background Execution**: Kept quarterly XP additions silent without popups or toasts, matching the seamless feel of vanilla monthly XP.
- **Half Potency Aura**: Scaled follower aura companion modifiers to 50% of the owner's cumulative bonuses rather than 1:1 mirroring, maintaining leadership primacy while retaining significant realm benefits.
- **Landed Piety Parity via Duchy Blood Universities**: Reworked Duchy Blood Universities from an occult piety drain into a positive piety generator matching the Domicile Blood Shrine cap (+2.5/mo), enabling landed feudal, clan, and tribal rulers to reliably fuel rituals without needing camp domiciles or wanderer contract spam.
