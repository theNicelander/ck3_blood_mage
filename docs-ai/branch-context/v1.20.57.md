# v1.20.57: Blood Shrine Piety Buff & Aura Calibrations

## Summary & Motivation
- Increase monthly piety generation on the Blood Shrine external domicile building line from 0.2 to 0.5 per tier.
- Calibrate the radiated companion follower/courtier aura to 50% potency across all stats (piety, health, lifespan, genetics), giving the builder full potency while reasonably empowering court members and camp followers.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Blood Shrine Domicile Line (`bm_domicile_blood_shrine_01..05`)**:
  - Increased `domicile_monthly_piety_add` from 0.2 to 0.5 per tier (up to +2.5 monthly piety at Tier V for owner).
  - Calibrated follower aura companion modifiers (`bm_blood_shrine_aura_tier_1..5`) to 50% potency:
    - Tier I: +0.25 Piety, +0.1 Health, +1 Lifespan, +2.5% Genetics
    - Tier II: +0.50 Piety, +0.2 Health, +2 Lifespan, +5.0% Genetics
    - Tier III: +0.75 Piety, +0.3 Health, +3 Lifespan, +7.5% Genetics
    - Tier IV: +1.00 Piety, +0.4 Health, +4 Lifespan, +10.0% Genetics
    - Tier V: +1.25 Piety, +0.5 Health, +5 Lifespan, +12.5% Genetics
  - Updated domicile parameter descriptions and architecture documentation to reflect the calibrated 50% aura values.

## Key Decisions
- **Half Potency Aura**: Scaled follower aura companion modifiers to 50% of the owner's cumulative bonuses rather than 1:1 mirroring, maintaining leadership primacy while retaining significant realm benefits.
