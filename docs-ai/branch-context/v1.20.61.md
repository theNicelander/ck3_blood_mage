# v1.20.61: Restructure Congenital Manifestation, Bestow Blood Traits & Consolidate Modal Interactions

## Motivation

- Congenital trait manifestation divided ranks arbitrarily at rank 2, leaving vanilla apex traits scattered across tiers without spiritual sacrifice or risk-reward parity, while rulers lacked rituals to impart bloodlines to kin or spouses.
  - High-tier manifestations lacked meaningful penalties or refunds, while positive traits could only be manifested on self.
- Right-click character context menus suffered severe clutter from 8+ separate blood magic interactions.
  - Spreading lifedrains, siphoning, tithes, lineage blessings, heir anointing, rune carving, and union consecrations across separate entries degraded target UX.
- Blood Healing interaction was locked at the character level when lacking resources, preventing players from opening the diagnosis menu to inspect missing costs or cure affordable ailments.
  - Deadly ailments awkwardly required both Minor and Major Lifeforce simultaneously instead of cleanly utilizing the Superior tier.

## Changes

### Key changes

- Restructure Manifest Congenital Traits (`bm_manifest_traits_decision`) across Major Magic (Ranks 1–3) and Superior Magic (Ranks 4–5).
  - Scale Piety costs (250–1500) and Devotion requirements across all five trait ranks.
  - Advancing to Rank 3 in Major magic or Ranks 4–5 in Superior magic now sacrifices 1 Devotion level upon success.
  - Standard failure refunds a lower-tier Lifeforce token (Minor for Major cast, Major for Superior cast); critical failure inflicts lower-tier Lifeforce exhaustion debuff alongside psychological trauma.
  - Scale lifestyle XP on success to 3/4/5/6/7/8 based on achieved trait rank (Cleanse/neutral: +3 XP, Rank 1: +4 XP, Rank 2: +5 XP, Rank 3: +6 XP, Rank 4: +7 XP, Rank 5: +8 XP; `bloodline` for Mages, `vanguard` for Physique / `enlightenment` for Intellect & Beauty for Knights).
- Add `bm_bestow_traits_interaction` ("Bestow Blood Traits") to pass positive congenital and physical traits to dynasty kin or spouses.
  - Costs 250 Piety and 1 Major Lifeforce with a 5-year cooldown; awards +5 `bloodline` / `vanguard` XP.
  - Step-by-step trait elevation is guaranteed for lower ranks; upgrading to an equal rank requires winning a Learning self-duel contest.
- Consolidate positive character interactions into unified modal interaction `bm_blood_blessing_interaction` ("Blood Blessing").
  - Houses 6 exclusive send options (`send_options_exclusive = yes`): Empower Blood Mage/Knight, Grant Lifeforce, Bless Blood, Annoint Heir, Consecrate Union, and Inscribe Blood Rune.
  - Preserves exact target filters, costs, cooldowns, and XP yields while eliminating context-menu clutter.
- Consolidate hostile extraction interactions into unified modal interaction `bm_blood_drain_interaction` ("Blood Drain").
  - Houses 4 exclusive send options: Major Life Drain, Minor Life Drain, Drain Stored Lifeforce, and Demand Blood Tithe.
  - Preserves standalone initiation rites ("Make Them a Blood Mage", "Make Blood Knight") and updates Important Action `bm_action_can_lifedrain_prisoner` to route directly to the modal window.
- Unblock Blood Healing interaction availability and shift Deadly Ailments to Superior Lifeforce (`bm_heal_ailment`):
  - Removed resource validity gate from character interaction so `bm_heal_ailment` remains clickable whenever target has an ailment, allowing players to open event dialogs (`bm_heal_ailment.0001` / `bm_heal_old_age.0001`) and inspect exact missing costs via grayed-out tooltips.
  - Moved resource gate to `ai_potential` so AI characters only initiate healing when capable of paying for at least one affliction.
  - Updated Deadly Ailments (Leprosy, Bubonic Plague, Cancer, etc.) to cost 1 Superior Lifeforce (yielding +3 Benediction XP via `benediction_magic_cast_effect_superior`) instead of requiring both Minor and Major Lifeforce simultaneously.
  - Unblocked entry to Old Age submenu from `bm_heal_ailment.0001.open_old_age` so old-age trait costs can also be inspected.
