# v1.20.61: Restructure Congenital Trait Manifestation

## Motivation

- Congenital trait manifestation previously divided ranks arbitrarily at rank 2, leaving vanilla apex traits scattered across tiers.
- High-rank manifestation lacked spiritual sacrifice and risk-reward parity with other capstone blood magic.
- Contest outcomes needed standardizing with consistent Lifeforce refunds on standard failure and lower-tier recoil on critical failure.

## Changes

- Restructured congenital trait manifestation across Major Blood Magic (Ranks 1–3) and Superior Blood Magic (Ranks 4–5).
- Scaled Piety costs and progressive Devotion requirements across all five trait ranks.
- Advancing to Rank 3 in Major magic or Ranks 4–5 in Superior magic now costs one Devotion Level upon success.
- Standard failure now refunds a lower-tier Lifeforce token (Minor for Major cast, Major for Superior cast).
- Critical failure inflicts an additional lower-tier Lifeforce exhaustion debuff alongside trauma.
