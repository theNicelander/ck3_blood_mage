# v1.20.74: Specialized Lifeforce Reservoirs (Blood Fury & Blood Focus)

## Motivation

- Decouple combat and intellectual bonuses from raw Immortality/Vitality Lifeforce, allowing Blood Mages to strategically channel their vital reserves into distinct, permanent reservoirs.
- Complete the specialized reservoir triad alongside Blood Mojo: provide dedicated stackable reserves for martial command (Blood Fury) and scholarship/stress management (Blood Focus).
- Consolidate the 3 core Blood Mage lifestyle traits (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`, `lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_bm_archmage`) from 10 steps to 5 clean milestones (10, 30, 50, 70, 100 XP) matching Blood Power, and eliminate overlap with Mojo, Fury, Focus, and Blood Power.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Core Trait Track Consolidation & De-duplication (`common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt`)**:
  - Streamlined `lifestyle_bm_acolyte`, `lifestyle_blood_mage`, and `lifestyle_bm_archmage` across all 6 tracks into 5 milestones (10, 30, 50, 70, 100 XP), cutting tooltip clutter while keeping cumulative 50 XP and 100 XP milestone bonuses intact.
  - Replaced overlapping modifiers with distinct perks per track:
    - `ancient`: Monthly Dynasty Renown (`monthly_dynasty_prestige`), Monthly Legitimacy (`monthly_legitimacy_add`), Level of Devotion Impact % (`piety_level_impact_mult`), `+1` Personal Tenet Slot at 50 & 100 XP (`+2` total), and `+1` Max Piety Level (`character_max_piety_level_add`) at 100 XP (absorbed into base traits as `+1` for Blood Mage and `+2` for Archmage).
    - `enlightenment`: Monthly Piety (`monthly_piety`) and Monthly Piety % (`monthly_piety_gain_mult`), preserving `learning_per_piety_level` at 50 & 100 XP (stripped lifestyle XP % which now lives on Blood Focus & Mastery).
    - `benediction`: General Opinion (`general_opinion`), Max Personal Scheme Success Chance (`owned_personal_scheme_success_chance_max_add`), `+1` Max Personal Schemes at 50 XP (`max_personal_schemes_add`), and `diplomacy_per_piety_level` at 50 & 100 XP.
    - `bloodline`: Monthly Income % (`monthly_income_mult`), Army Maintenance reduction (`army_maintenance_mult`), Capital County Development Growth (`character_capital_county_monthly_development_growth_add`), Monthly Income per Piety level (`monthly_income_per_piety_level`), and `stewardship_per_piety_level` at 50 & 100 XP.
    - `hematurgy`: Max Hostile Scheme Success Chance (`owned_hostile_scheme_success_chance_max_add`), Enemy Max Hostile Scheme Success Chance reduction (`enemy_hostile_scheme_success_chance_max_add`), `+1` Max Hostile Schemes at 50 XP (`max_hostile_schemes_add`), and `intrigue_per_piety_level` at 50 & 100 XP.
    - `bloodlust`: Sworn Knight Limit (`knight_limit = 1` across all 5 milestones 10/30/50/70/100; +3 base on Blood Mage and +6 base on Archmage), Army Damage % (`army_damage_mult`), and `martial_per_piety_level` + `prowess_per_piety_level` at 50 & 100 XP.
- **Blood Power Warband De-duplication (`common/traits/bm_blood_power_trait.txt`)**:
  - Replaced overlapping Knight and Advantage bonuses with a dedicated Men-at-Arms mastery package: `+10%` Counter Efficiency per milestone (`+50%` at 100 XP), `+1` Men-at-Arms Max Size at 30, 50, 70, 100 XP (`+4` total), and `+1` Men-at-Arms Regiments at 50 & 100 XP (`+2` total).
- **Core Lifeforce Immortality Refinement (`common/modifiers/bm_lifeforce.txt`)**:
  - Removed `prowess = 3` from `lifeforce_modifier_superior`. Core Lifeforce (`minor`, `major`, `superior`) now strictly governs vitality and longevity (`health`, `life_expectancy`, `epidemic_resistance`, `negate_health_penalty_add`).
- **Specialized Reservoir Modifiers (`common/modifiers/bm_lifeforce.txt`)**:
  - Added stackable, permanent `lifeforce_modifier_fury`: `+2` Prowess, `+10%` Knight Effectiveness, `+2%` Army Movement Speed, `+2` Travel Safety per stack.
  - Added stackable, permanent `lifeforce_modifier_focus`: `+1` Learning, `-10%` Stress Gain, `+5%` Monthly Lifestyle XP Gain per stack.
- **Transmutation & Reclaim Loops (`common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_cast_blood_magic.txt`)**:
  - **Minor Transmutations**: Added `bm_minor_channel_fury` and `bm_minor_channel_focus` (50 Piety + 1 Minor Lifeforce -> +1 reservoir stack, awards +1 `bloodlust` or `enlightenment` XP).
  - **Minor Reclaims**: Added `bm_minor_reclaim_fury` and `bm_minor_reclaim_focus` (75 Piety + 1 reservoir stack -> +1 Minor Lifeforce, awards +1 `bloodlust` or `enlightenment` XP).
  - **Major Transmutations**: Added `bm_major_channel_fury` and `bm_major_channel_focus` (100 Piety + 1 Major Lifeforce -> +3 reservoir stacks, awards +2 `bloodlust` or `enlightenment` XP).
  - Maintained anti-exploit piety gating (125 Piety net round-trip) to prevent infinite Lifeforce or Piety generation loops.
- **Story Panel Integration (`common/scripted_effects/bm_blood_mage_story_list_effects.txt`, `localization/english/bm_blood_mage_story_l_english.yml`)**:
  - Added `var:bm_lifeforce_fury_stacks` and `var:bm_lifeforce_focus_stacks` tracking to `bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect`.
  - Expanded Story Panel Lifeforce readout to display separate counts for Mojo, Fury, and Focus reserves alongside core reserves.
- **Ascend Blood Magic Tier Lifeforce Requirement Removal (`common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_elevate_tier_decision.txt`)**:
  - Removed Lifeforce modifier requirements (`lifeforce_modifier_major` / `lifeforce_modifier_superior`) from `is_valid` for both Blood Acolytes and Blood Mages.
  - Removed Lifeforce modifier consumption from decision `effect`, allowing characters to ascend based on Ancient XP, total discipline XP, Blood Rune mastery, Devotion, and Piety without sacrificing their vital reserves.
- **Blood Mirror Ascension Duel Reflection Opponent Tuning (`events/bm_ascension_duel_events.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/bm_ascension_duel_effects.txt`, `common/scripted_character_templates/bm_character_templates.txt`, `localization/english/event_localization/bm_ascension_duel_events_l_english.yml`)**:
  - Opponent dynasty and house are now unconditionally set to "Blood Ghost" (`bm_blood_ghost_dynasty_name`), creating a distinct supernatural adversary rather than borrowing the player's house name.
  - Standardized the reflection's mental and governing attributes (diplomacy, martial, stewardship, intrigue, learning) to 20 across the board via `bm_synchronize_character_skills_effect` and template defaults.
  - Kept reflection prowess dynamically matched to player prowess (`root.prowess`), maintaining duel combat parity.
- **Ascension Unspent Discipline XP Extraction Fix (`common/script_values/bm_blood_mage_story_values.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/bm_blood_mage_lifecycle_effects.txt`)**:
  - Fixed mathematical division in unspent non-ancient discipline XP extraction: replaced broken inline `set_variable = { divide = 20 floor = yes }` blocks (which left raw XP undivided and awarded 500 stacks) with the dedicated script value `bm_elevation_overflow_lifeforce_stacks_value`.
  - Accurately pools unspent discipline XP across `bloodlust`, `benediction`, `bloodline`, `hematurgy`, and `enlightenment`, awarding exactly 1 Lifeforce stack per 20 unspent XP (25 stacks at 500 maxed XP).
