# v1.21.2: Rebalance Fury Knight Effectiveness & Trait Track Piety

## Motivation

- `fury` granted `knight_effectiveness_per_learning` at every milestone (`10`, `30`, `50`, `70`, `100` XP), unlike the other four skill tracks whose signature extras unlock only at `50` and `100` XP.
- Monthly Piety on lifestyle tracks was concentrated exclusively in `ancient` (`+0.50` per step), leaving the five skill tracks with no Piety progression.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Fury Knight Effectiveness (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`, `lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_bm_archmage`):**
  - Moved `knight_effectiveness_per_learning` out of `10`, `30`, and `70` XP and placed `+1.0%` (`0.01`) at `50` XP and `+1.0%` (`0.01`) at `100` XP (`+2.0%` total at 100 XP) across all three trait tiers.
  - Preserved base trait floors (`1.0%` on `lifestyle_blood_mage`, `2.0%` on `lifestyle_bm_archmage`).
- **Track Monthly Piety (`radiance`, `fury`, `fortune`, `cunning`, `wisdom`, `ancient`):**
  - Added `monthly_piety = 0.1` to every milestone (`10`, `30`, `50`, `70`, `100` XP) on all five skill tracks (`+0.5` cumulative per track at 100 XP).
  - Reduced `ancient` track `monthly_piety` from `0.50` to `0.2` per milestone (`+1.0` cumulative at 100 XP).
  - Kept base trait `monthly_piety` (`3.00` on `lifestyle_blood_mage`, `6.00` on `lifestyle_bm_archmage`) unchanged.

### Other changes

- Synced `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-traits.md`, `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-fury.md`, and `RELEASE_NOTES.md`.
