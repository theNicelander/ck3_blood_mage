# v1.20.35: Move Blood Mage Decisions to Situation Panel

## Summary & Motivation
Relocate all blood magic and blood knight operational decisions out of the standard realm Decisions tab and into the "Blood Magic" Situation panel (`bm_blood_mage_story` / `window_situation_list.gui`). This eliminates decision menu bloat while centralizing all active blood rites, manifestations, harvesting, seeking power, and elevation inside the dedicated management panel.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Situation Decisions Integration (`bm_blood_mage_story.txt`)**:
  - Populated `decisions = { ... }` in `bm_blood_mage_story` with all 9 Blood Mage and Blood Knight decisions:
    - `bm_cast_blood_magic_minor_decision` (Cast Minor Blood Magic)
    - `bm_cast_blood_magic_major_decision` (Cast Major Blood Magic)
    - `bm_cast_blood_magic_superior_decision` (Cast Superior Blood Magic)
    - `bm_manifest_lifeforce_decision` (Manifest Lifeforce)
    - `bm_manifest_superior_lifeforce_decision` (Manifest Superior Lifeforce)
    - `mass_lifedrain_prisoners_decision` (Harvest Lifeforce from Prisoners)
    - `blood_golem_creation_decision` (Create Blood Golem)
    - `seek_power_decision` (Seek Power in Nature's Domain - Dynamic 2-yr landed, 1-yr unlanded)
    - `bm_elevate_to_blood_mage_decision` (Elevate Blood Knight to Blood Mage)
- **Standard Decision List Declutter (`is_invisible = yes`)**:
  - Added `is_invisible = yes` to all relocated decisions, hiding them from the realm Decisions window (`window_decisions.gui`) while allowing `Story.GetDecisions` and `Decision.IsShownForPlayer` to render them seamlessly inside `window_situation_list.gui`.
  - Initiation decisions (`bm_blood_cultist_become_blood_mage_decision`, `bm_enhance_blood_ritual_decision`, `convert_to_blood_magic_from_witch`) remain in the standard realm Decisions menu for uninitiated characters who do not yet possess the Blood Magic Situation panel.
  - Religious decisions (`become_blood_cultist_decision`, `bm_repair_blodtru_reformation_decision`) remain under the Religious decision group.
- **GUI Decision Flowcontainer Polishing (`window_situation_list.gui`)**:
  - Added `spacing = 6` to the decisions `flowcontainer` in `vbox_story_cycle_entry` for clean button separation.
- **Blood Knight Story Lifecycle Synchronization**:
  - Added `bm_ensure_blood_mage_story_effect = yes` to `bm_become_blood_knight_effect` to ensure immediate Situation panel availability upon acquiring `lifestyle_blood_knight`.
- **Architecture Documentation Consolidation**:
  - Merged `blood-mage-progression.md` into `blood-mage-track-xp.md` (Ritual XP Gates table).
  - Merged `blood-mage-trait-benefits.md` into `blood-mage-traits.md` (10 / 50 / 100 XP cumulative benefit matrix across 12 tracks).

  - Added on-action and scripted effect `bm_initialize_starting_counties_and_holders_effect` to initialize counties and holders on game start.

## PR #147: Rebalance Manifesting Traits vs. Draining Traits
- **Motivation**: Manifesting congenital traits out of thin air (*ex nihilo*) was previously safer, cheaper, and mathematically easier than hunting down, imprisoning, and draining a rare prisoner with congenital traits. This inverted the thematic and mechanical hierarchy of blood magic.
- **Lifeforce Gating**:
  - Capped Major Blood Magic ex-nihilo manifestation at Rank 2 (Quick / Intelligent, Hale / Robust, Comely / Pretty), requiring Piety level 3.
  - Gated Rank 3 (Genius, Herculean, Beautiful) and Transcendent tiers (Ranks 4–5) behind Superior Blood Magic (`bm_cast_blood_magic_superior.001` -> `bm_channel_manifest_traits.002`), requiring Superior Lifeforce and 1000–1500 Piety.
  - Prisoner Trait Draining remains accessible on Major Lifeforce for all ranks (1–5), making live donors the vastly superior and earlier route to peak congenital perfection.
- **Duel Difficulty & Failure Consequences**:
  - Ex-nihilo manifestation duels now test against calibrated difficulty (`value = 15` on Major duels, `value = 25` on Superior duels), rather than testing against DC 0.
  - Standard failure on ex-nihilo rituals now inflicts Lifeforce Exhaustion (`temporary_negative_lifeforce_effect_major` or `temporary_negative_lifeforce_effect_superior`).
  - Critical failure on ex-nihilo rituals inflicts Wounds, 50 Stress, and Lifeforce Exhaustion.
  - Cooldown for ex-nihilo rituals increased from 3 years to 5 years (`bm_manifest_traits_cooldown`).
- **Prisoner Trait Drain Smoothing**:
  - Smoothed punitive duel modifiers in `bm_drain_duel_values.txt`: reduced `trait_drain_modifier_1/2/3` from 50/100/200 down to 30/60/100, removing the near-guaranteed failure when draining high-tier donor prisoners.
- **Documentation & Localization**:
  - Updated `bm_channel_manifest_traits_l_english.yml` and `bm_cast_blood_magic_self_l_english.yml` to reflect Rank 1–2 vs Rank 3–5 gating and exhaustion tooltips.
  - Synchronized `blood-mage-magic-tiers.md`, `blood-mage-events-overview.md`, `description.txt`, and workshop descriptions.
