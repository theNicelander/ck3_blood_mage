# v1.20-8

## Summary & Motivation

Branch `v1.20-8` consolidates and refines the core blood mage experience across religious systems, world prevalence, AI interactions, dynasty tracking, and developer tooling:
- Consolidates the Cult of Quintessence religion into a single unified faith (`quintessence_faith`) with universal tolerance (`doctrine_pluralism_pluralistic`, `tenet_adaptive`, opinion buffering on `bm_quintessence_identity_doctrine`), and replaces Sacred Shadows with Ancestor Worship (`tenet_ancestor_worship`).
- Streamlines the "Embrace the Blood Cultist Faith" decision to require 1 Major Lifeforce and rank 3 Devotion without faith/piety costs.
- Recalibrates baseline blood mage prevalence to 1 in 500 (0.2%) and expands campaign game rule choices.
- Enables safe AI minor lifedrain on unlanded courtiers/guests with factor 0 safeguards, and prioritizes AI blood magic granting toward family and councillors.
- Integrates a living Dynasty Blood Mages roster and count into the Situation panel.
- Introduces standardized pre-commit hooks for whitespace, EOF, and CK3 repo validation.

## Core Concepts & High-Level Changes

- **Single Unified Blood Mage Faith (`quintessence_faith`)**:
  - Deprecated and removed the 7 redundant syncretic faith variants (`quintessence_faith_christian`, `_islamic`, `_jewish`, `_eastern`, `_sinitic`, `_asatru`, `_unreformed`) and their corresponding duplicate mainline rites.
  - Retained all 16 Eminent and 57 Regular Holy Sites on `quintessence_faith`.
  - Mainline rite in `common/religion/rite_types/bm_rite_types.txt` updated to match 1:1.
  - Tenets configured with `tenet_adaptive`, `tenet_ancestor_worship`, and `tenet_ritual_celebrations` (Sacred Shadows removed).

- **Universal Tolerance & Coexistence Systems**:
  - Replaced `doctrine_pluralism_righteous` with `doctrine_pluralism_pluralistic` in `bm_religion.txt`, activating `hostility_towards_us_mult = 0.5` which cuts foreign hostility opinion maluses in half and prevents vassal religious discontent.
  - Replaced tenet slot with `tenet_adaptive`, providing +15 liege opinion, -25% county opinion malus, and +10 minority opinion.
  - Added character modifier buffering to `bm_quintessence_identity_doctrine` (+15 `different_faith_opinion`, +10 `different_faith_liege_opinion`), neutralizing global religious opinion friction without requiring 8 separate syncretic faiths.
  - Simplified `bm_convert_to_blood_cult_by_heritage_effect` to directly convert characters to `quintessence_faith`.

- **Cult Conversion Decision Tuning**:
  - Reverted intermediate event popup flow in `bm_become_blood_cultist_decision.txt` back to an immediate, clean decision.
  - Removed arbitrary piety cost (`cost = { piety = 500 }`).
  - Requires Level of Devotion 3 (`piety_level >= 3`) and active Major Lifeforce (`has_character_modifier = lifeforce_modifier_major`).
  - Consumes one Major Lifeforce (`blood_magic_used_major = yes`) upon conversion.

- **Blood Mage Prevalence & World Generation**:
  - Rebalanced baseline random creation chance and birth chance to 1 in 500 (`0.2%`, previously 0.5% / 1 in 200).
  - Scaled yearly pulse checks and prevalence on-actions (`bm_blood_mage_prevalence_on_actions.txt`, `bm_yearly_pulse.txt`, `bm_blood_mage_lifecycle_effects.txt`).
  - Expanded campaign game rules (`bm_blood_mage_prevalence`) with 5 clear tiers:
    - Rare (1 in 2,000 / 0.05%)
    - Default (1 in 500 / 0.2%)
    - Uncommon (1 in 200 / 0.5%)
    - Common (1 in 100 / 1.0%)
    - Widespread (1 in 40 / 2.5%)

- **AI Interaction Improvements & Safeguards**:
  - **Minor Lifedrain on Courtiers & Guests**: Allowed AI blood mages in `bm_drain_lifeforce.txt` to feed on unlanded courtiers and guests when lifeforce is low or depleted.
  - **AI Safety Blocks**: Implemented comprehensive factor 0 weights and safeguards in `bm_ai_value_modifiers.txt` to prevent AI mages from lifedraining close relatives, spouse, friends, council members, rulers, or heirs.
  - **Targeted Bestowal Priority**: AI blood mages using `bm_grant_blood_magic.txt` now strongly prioritize their inner circle (spouse, children, liege, and councillors) rather than picking random characters.

- **Dynasty Blood Mages Roster & Situation Panel**:
  - Added `bm_dynasty_blood_mages` character list in `bm_blood_mage_story.txt`.
  - Populated via `bm_refresh_blood_magic_rosters_effect` to track all living members of the owner's dynasty possessing `lifestyle_blood_mage`.
  - Displays count in the foldout header and an expandable portrait grid within the Situation window.

- **Developer Tooling & Formatting**:
  - Configured `.pre-commit-config.yaml` to enforce whitespace trimming, trailing newlines, git conflict marker checks, and the repository's `scripts/check_repo.py` format validation.

## Key Decisions

- **Single Faith over Multiple Syncretic Variants**:
  - Avoids map and faith fragmentation while keeping all cultists unified under one religious banner.
- **Pluralism + Adaptive over Specific Syncretisms**:
  - Syncretism tenets only appease one religion at a time while leaving all other world religions considering the player Evil. Pluralism combined with Adaptive and cult identity modifiers provides global tolerance across all religions (Christian, Muslim, Jewish, Pagan, Eastern).
- **Ancestor Worship Tenet**:
  - Replaced Sacred Shadows with Ancestor Worship to bolster dynastic renown generation and ancestral guidance mechanics, fitting the mod's bloodline and generational focus.
- **Prevalence Tuned to 1 in 500**:
  - Prevents blood mages from saturating courts and losing their mystical rarity, while remaining readily discoverable across the game world.
- **Factor 0 AI Lifedrain Safeguards**:
  - Allows AI mages to sustain their lifeforce naturally without depopulating crucial court advisors or executing their own dynasty members.
- **Direct Decision for Cult Conversion**:
  - Eliminated unnecessary intermediate event prompts for a cleaner, more intuitive player interface.
