# v1.20-8

## Summary & Motivation

This branch consolidates the Cult of Quintessence religion into a single base faith (`quintessence_faith`), replaces syncretic faith variants with universal tolerance mechanics (`doctrine_pluralism_pluralistic`, `tenet_adaptive`, and opinion buffering on `bm_quintessence_identity_doctrine`), and converts the `become_blood_cultist_decision` into an interactive narrative event (`bm_blood_cultist.0001`).

## Core Concepts & High-Level Changes

- **Single Unified Blood Mage Faith (`quintessence_faith`)**:
  - Deprecated and removed the 7 redundant syncretic faith variants (`quintessence_faith_christian`, `_islamic`, `_jewish`, `_eastern`, `_sinitic`, `_asatru`, `_unreformed`) and their corresponding duplicate mainline rites.
  - Retained all 16 Eminent and 57 Regular Holy Sites on `quintessence_faith`.
  - Mainline rite in `common/religion/rite_types/bm_rite_types.txt` updated to match.

- **Universal Tolerance & Coexistence Systems**:
  - Replaced `doctrine_pluralism_righteous` with `doctrine_pluralism_pluralistic` in `bm_religion.txt`, activating `hostility_towards_us_mult = 0.5` which cuts foreign hostility opinion maluses in half and prevents vassal religious discontent.
  - Replaced tenet slot with `tenet_adaptive`, providing +15 liege opinion, -25% county opinion malus, and +10 minority opinion.
  - Added character modifier buffering to `bm_quintessence_identity_doctrine` (+15 `different_faith_opinion`, +10 `different_faith_liege_opinion`), effectively neutralizing remaining religious opinion friction worldwide without requiring 8 separate faiths.

- **Event-Driven Conversion (`bm_blood_cultist.0001`)**:
  - `become_blood_cultist_decision` now triggers character event `bm_blood_cultist.0001` in `events/bm_blood_cultist_events.txt`.
  - Option A: "Proclaim the Quintessence" (open conversion, capital county conversion, +piety and lifeforce).
  - Option B: "Walk the Veiled Path" (subtle conversion, intrigue lifestyle XP and intrigue lifeforce buff).
  - Option C: "Reconsider" (refunds the 500 piety decision cost).
  - Scripted conversion effect `bm_convert_to_blood_cult_by_heritage_effect` updated to directly convert to `quintessence_faith`.

## Key Decisions

- **Single Faith over Multiple Syncretic Variants**:
  - Avoids map and faith fragmentation while keeping all cultists unified under one religious banner.
- **Pluralism + Adaptive over Specific Syncretisms**:
  - Syncretism tenets only appease one religion at a time while leaving all other world religions considering the player Evil. Pluralism combined with Adaptive and cult identity modifiers provides global tolerance across all religions (Christian, Muslim, Jewish, Pagan, Eastern).
