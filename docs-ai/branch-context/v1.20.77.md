# v1.20.77: Add Knight Effectiveness per Prowess/Learning & Piety per Knight

## Motivation

- Synergize personal attributes with sworn champions: Blood Mages accumulate high Prowess and Learning through Devotion-scaling milestones and rituals, while expanding their sworn knight capacity via the `bloodlust` track and Blood Golems. Adding `knight_effectiveness_per_prowess` and `knight_effectiveness_per_learning` turns personal martial and scholarly mastery directly into retinue combat power.
- Reward maintaining a large sworn retinue with ritual fuel: Every blood magic rite, covenant, and golem shaping roll consumes substantial Piety. Adding `monthly_piety_gain_per_knight_add` and `monthly_piety_gain_per_knight_mult` to House Covenants, Duchy Blood Temples, and Domicile Blood Shrines turns sworn knights into active spiritual contributors for both landed rulers and landless adventurers.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Knight Effectiveness per Prowess & Learning on Primary Lifestyle Tracks (`common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt`)**:
  - **`bloodlust` (`knight_effectiveness_per_prowess`)**:
    - **Blood Acolyte (Tier I)**: `+0.5%` (`0.005`) at 50 XP and `+0.5%` at 100 XP (`+1.0%` track total).
    - **Blood Mage (Tier II)**: `+0.5%` absorbed on base trait floor, plus `+0.5%` at 50 XP and `+0.5%` at 100 XP (`+1.5%` combined total).
    - **Blood Archmage (Tier III)**: `+1.0%` (`0.01`) absorbed on base trait floor, plus `+1.0%` at 50 XP and `+1.0%` at 100 XP (`+3.0%` combined total).
  - **`enlightenment` (`knight_effectiveness_per_learning`)**:
    - **Blood Acolyte (Tier I)**: `+0.5%` (`0.005`) at 50 XP and `+0.5%` at 100 XP (`+1.0%` track total).
    - **Blood Mage (Tier II)**: `+0.5%` absorbed on base trait floor, plus `+0.5%` at 50 XP and `+0.5%` at 100 XP (`+1.5%` combined total).
    - **Blood Archmage (Tier III)**: `+1.0%` (`0.01`) absorbed on base trait floor, plus `+1.0%` at 50 XP and `+1.0%` at 100 XP (`+3.0%` combined total).
- **Knight Effectiveness & Piety per Knight on House Covenants (`common/modifiers/bm_channel_dynasty_modifiers.txt`)**:
  - **Covenant of Warband (`dynasty_blood_warband_modifier_1..3`)**:
    - **Tier I / II / III**: Grants `+0.02` / `+0.04` / `+0.06` monthly Piety per knight (`monthly_piety_gain_per_knight_add`) and `+0.2%` / `+0.5%` / `+1.0%` knight effectiveness per Prowess (`knight_effectiveness_per_prowess`).
  - **Covenant of Mastery (`dynasty_blood_mastery_modifier_1..3`)**:
    - **Tier I / II / III**: Grants `+1%` / `+2%` / `+3%` monthly Piety per knight (`monthly_piety_gain_per_knight_mult`) and `+0.2%` / `+0.5%` / `+1.0%` knight effectiveness per Learning (`knight_effectiveness_per_learning`).
- **Piety per Knight on Duchy Blood Temples & Domicile Blood Shrines (`common/buildings/bm_duchy_buildings.txt`, `common/domiciles/buildings/bm_domicile_buildings.txt`)**:
  - **Duchy Blood Temples (`bm_blood_temple_01..07`)**: Scales `monthly_piety_gain_per_knight_add` (`+0.02` / `+0.04` / `+0.06` / `+0.08` / `+0.10` / `+0.15` / `+0.20`) and `monthly_piety_gain_per_knight_mult` (`+1%` / `+1%` / `+2%` / `+2%` / `+3%` / `+4%` / `+5%`) across Tiers I–VII.
  - **Domicile Blood Shrines (`bm_domicile_blood_shrine_01..05`)**: Adds an incremental `+0.02` `monthly_piety_gain_per_knight_add` and `+1%` (`0.01`) `monthly_piety_gain_per_knight_mult` per tier, stacking to `+0.10` and `+5%` per knight at Tier V.
