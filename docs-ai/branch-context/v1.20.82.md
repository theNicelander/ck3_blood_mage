# v1.20.82: Simplify Blood Mage lifestyle tracks

## Motivation

- Previous Blood Mage tracks had ~30 different modifiers with tier-specific multipliers (0.5×/1.0×/1.5×), creating tooltip bloat and heavy per-piety scaling.
- Consolidating onto a uniform attribute-per-milestone structure makes progression readable and consistent across all three mage ranks.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Unified track scaling across all 3 ranks** (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`, `lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_bm_archmage`):
  - Every milestone (10, 30, 50, 70, 100 XP) on the 5 attribute tracks grants `+1` to its respective skill (`+5` total) and `+1` per Piety Level at 30/70 (`+2` total), plus standard Vitality steps (`+0.2` health, `+4` life expectancy at 10/30/70/100).
  - 50/100 XP track extras: Bloodline `+10%` monthly income mult (`+20%` total); Hematurgy `+1` max hostile schemes (`+2` total); Benediction `+1` max personal schemes (`+2` total); Enlightenment `+1` personal tenet slot (`+2` total); Bloodlust `+0.02` monthly piety per knight (`+0.04` total).
- **`ancient` track**:
  - Grants `+0.50` monthly piety (`+2.50` total) and `+0.40` monthly dynasty prestige (`+2.00` total) at every milestone, `+0.5%` knight effectiveness per Learning at 30/70 (`+1.0%` total), `+4` life expectancy at 50, and `+1` max piety level at 100.
  - Removed monthly legitimacy and devotion impact multipliers.
- **Simplified Mage & Archmage base floors**:
  - `lifestyle_blood_mage` base: `+3` to all 5 main attributes, `+1` per Piety Level, `+10%` monthly income mult, and half-track extras (`+0.60` renown, `+1.50` piety, `+1` tenet slot, `+1` max piety level, `+0.5%` knight eff per Learning, `+1` personal & hostile schemes, `+0.02` piety/knight), plus base health (`2`), life expectancy (`26`), and `no_prowess_loss_from_age`.
  - `lifestyle_bm_archmage` base: `+6` to all 5 main attributes, `+2` per Piety Level, `+20%` monthly income mult, and full-track extras (`+1.80` renown, `+3.00` piety, `+2` tenet slots, `+2` max piety levels, `+1.0%` knight eff per Learning, `+2` personal & hostile schemes, `+0.04` piety/knight), plus base health (`5`), life expectancy (`78`), and `no_prowess_loss_from_age`.
  - Removed flat prowess and non-track modifiers from base traits.
