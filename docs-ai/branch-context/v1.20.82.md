# v1.20.82: Simplify Blood Mage lifestyle tracks

## Motivation

- Previous Blood Mage tracks had ~30 different modifiers with tier-specific multipliers (0.5×/1.0×/1.5×), creating tooltip bloat and heavy per-piety scaling.
- Consolidating onto a uniform attribute-per-milestone structure makes progression readable and consistent across all three mage ranks.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Unified track scaling across all 3 ranks** (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`, `lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_bm_archmage`):
  - Every milestone (10, 30, 50, 70, 100 XP) grants `+0.2` health (`+1.0` per track) and tier-scaled life expectancy (`+2` Acolyte = `60` max; `+3` Mage = `90` + `40` base = `130` max; `+4` Archmage = `120` + `100` base = `220` max).
  - Attribute tracks grant `+1` to their respective skill at all 5 milestones (`+5` total) and `+1` per Piety Level at 30/70 (`+2` total).
  - 50/100 XP track extras: Bloodline `+10%` monthly income mult (`+20%` total); Hematurgy `+1` max hostile schemes (`+2` total); Benediction `+1` max personal schemes (`+2` total); Enlightenment `+1` personal tenet slot (`+2` total); Bloodlust `+0.02` monthly piety per knight (`+0.04` total).
- **`ancient` track**:
  - Grants `+0.50` monthly piety (`+2.50` total) and `+0.20` monthly dynasty prestige (`+1.00` total) at all 5 milestones, `+0.5%` knight effectiveness per Learning at 30/70 (`+1.0%` total), and `+1` max piety level at 100.
  - Removed monthly legitimacy and devotion impact multipliers.
- **Simplified Mage & Archmage base floors**:
  - `lifestyle_blood_mage` base: `life_expectancy = 40`, `monthly_dynasty_prestige = 1.00`, `+3` to all 5 main attributes, `+1` per Piety Level, `+10%` monthly income mult, and half-track extras (`+1.50` piety, `+1` tenet slot, `+1` max piety level, `+0.5%` knight eff per Learning, `+1` personal & hostile schemes, `+0.02` piety/knight), plus base `health = 2` and `no_prowess_loss_from_age`.
  - `lifestyle_bm_archmage` base: `life_expectancy = 100`, `monthly_dynasty_prestige = 2.00`, `+6` to all 5 main attributes, `+2` per Piety Level, `+20%` monthly income mult, and full-track extras (`+3.00` piety, `+2` tenet slots, `+2` max piety levels, `+1.0%` knight eff per Learning, `+2` personal & hostile schemes, `+0.04` piety/knight), plus base `health = 5` and `no_prowess_loss_from_age`.
  - Removed flat prowess and non-track modifiers from base traits.
