# v1.21.9: Blood Magic Polish, Spells, Conclave & Narrative Expansion
PR: TBD

## Motivation
- Cover remaining vanilla `category = health` congenital mental health variants and severe blood/fertility defects in Blood Healing.
- Polish Situation alerts, Grimoire option lists, Mass Lifedrain safety filters, and Transmute Lifeforce UX.
- Expand repeatable Minor blood magic (`Channel Focus`), court mage gameplay (`Convene a Blood Conclave`), and emergent narrative/risk events.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Blood Healing (`bm_heal_ailment`)**:
  - **Major Tier** (`Major Lifeforce` + `35` Piety): Added `lunatic_genetic`, `possessed_genetic`, and `depressed_genetic`.
  - **Deadly Tier** (`Superior Lifeforce` + `100` Piety, Archmage): Added `bleeder`, `inbred`, and `infertile`.
- **Situation Alerts (`bm_important_actions.txt`)**:
  - Added `bm_action_can_ascend_tier` and `bm_action_can_carve_galdrastafir` alerts opening their respective decision views.
- **Grimoire Polish (`bm_cast_blood_magic.txt`)**:
  - Removed duplicate `Cast Blood Blessing` entries from Major and Superior Grimoires (kept in Minor Grimoire), assigned discipline-matched icons across all three tiers, and updated `bm_minor_purge` to apply standard Minor exhaustion (`blood_magic_used_minor`).
- **Mass Lifedrain (`bm_mass_lifedrain.001`)**:
  - Expanded Option B ("Spare the valuable ones") to also protect `20+` Prowess Blood Pit candidates, Blood Mages, and landed prisoners.
  - Added Option C for non-lethal mass Minor Lifeforce siphoning (`20` Piety each, 3-month victim cooldown, non-lethal wound).

### Other changes
- **Architecture docs**: Updated curable ailment count (`34` -> `40`) in `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-interactions.md` and `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-events-overview.md`.
