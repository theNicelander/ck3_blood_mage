# v1.21.9: Blood Healing, Covenant Fix, Spells & Narrative Events
PR: #204

## Motivation
- Cover remaining vanilla genetic mental health variants and severe blood/fertility defects in Blood Healing.
- Fix broken House Covenant applications in Empower Dynasty caused by leftover pre-`v1.21.8` modifier keys.
- Polish Situation alerts, Grimoire option icons/exhaustion, and Mass Lifedrain safety filters.
- Expand repeatable Minor blood magic, Blood Hunt variety, and emergent/milestone events.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Blood Healing (`bm_heal_ailment`)**: Added 6 curable health traits (total `34` -> `40`).
  - **Major Tier** (`Major Lifeforce` + `35` Piety): Added `lunatic_genetic`, `possessed_genetic`, and `depressed_genetic`.
  - **Deadly Tier** (`Superior Lifeforce` + `100` Piety, Archmage): Added `bleeder`, `inbred`, and `infertile`.
- **Empower Dynasty Bloodline Fix (`bm_empower_bloodline_effects.txt`, `bm_covenant_triggers.txt`)**: Fixed silent failure where upgrading covenants left houses with no modifier.
  - Updated `bm_upgrade_house_covenant_effect` and `bm_can_upgrade_covenant_trigger` from stale `dynasty_blood_$COVENANT$_modifier_1/2/3` keys to `bm_covenant_$COVENANT$_minor/major/superior_modifier`.
- **Situation Alerts (`bm_important_actions.txt`)**: Added missing milestone alerts using `@bm_ancient_discipline_icon!`.
  - `bm_action_can_ascend_tier`: Surfaces when eligible to take `bm_elevate_blood_mage_tier_decision`.
  - `bm_action_can_carve_galdrastafir`: Surfaces for Archmages holding `superior_blood_rune_modifier` with no stave carved.
- **Grimoire Polish (`bm_cast_blood_magic.txt`)**: Streamlined spellbooks, icons, and exhaustion.
  - Removed duplicate `Cast Blood Blessing` items from Major and Superior Grimoires (kept in Minor).
  - Set Burn, Split, and Condense Lifeforce icons to default `gfx/interface/icons/bm_blood_magic_icon.dds`, and Manifest Traits icons to `gfx/interface/icons/traits/intellect_good_2.dds`.
  - Updated `bm_minor_purge` to apply standard Minor exhaustion (`blood_magic_used_minor`).
- **Mass Lifedrain (`bm_mass_lifedrain.001`)**: Improved prisoner filtering and added non-lethal siphoning.
  - Option B ("Spare the valuable ones") now also spares `20+` Prowess fighters, Blood Mages, and landed prisoners.
  - Added Option C for non-lethal mass Minor Lifeforce siphoning (`20` Piety each, 3-month cooldown, non-lethal wound, `+1` `cunning` XP).
- **Channel Blood Focus (`bm_minor_channel_focus` / `bm_channel_focus_interaction`)**: Added repeatable Minor spell.
  - Costs `50` Piety + `1 Minor Lifeforce` (3-year cooldown, Minor exhaustion, `+2` matching discipline XP).
  - Applies a 5-year situational modifier (`bm_channel_focus_*`) across Radiance, Fury, Fortune, Cunning, Wisdom, or Ancient.
- **Narrative & Age Milestone Events (`bm_interpersonal_events.0006`–`.0011`)**: Added earned-risk, court, and longevity milestone events.
  - `.0006` **The Red Thirst**: Overdrain/exhaustion strain (higher weight when overdrained; endure, feed secretly without applying `lifedrained_modifier`, or burn Minor Lifeforce).
  - `.0007` & `.0011` **Age 85 & Age 100 Milestones**: One-time player-only yearly pulse events granting `+5` XP (age `85+`) or `+10` XP (age `100+`) to any chosen Blood Mage track.
  - `.0008` **A Challenger in Crimson**: Wandering Tier II+ mage duel or court recruitment.
  - `.0009` **Fractured Clay**: Blood Golem binding reinforcement (`bm_reinforced_golem_modifier`) or decay.
  - `.0010` **The Eager Acolyte**: Court acolyte combat drill, arcane study, or Major Lifeforce infusion.
- **Non-Martial Blood Hunt Encounters (`seek_power.012`–`.014`)**: Added 3 non-Prowess wilderness encounters to `seek_power.001`.
  - `.012` **Sunken Blood Shrine** (Learning), `.013` **Plague-Touched Hermit** (Learning/Diplomacy vs Intrigue/Learning), and `.014` **Rival Coven's Cache** (Intrigue/Stewardship).

### Other changes
- **Docs, Rules & Release Notes**: Synced guidelines, architecture docs, and public notes.
  - Documented `gfx/interface/icons/bm_blood_magic_icon.dds` as the default Blood Mage icon in `AGENTS.md`.
  - Updated `blood-mage-events-overview.md`, `blood-mage-magic-tiers.md`, `blood-mage-decisions.md`, and `blood-mage-interactions.md` (removed stale retired file refs).
  - Updated `RELEASE_NOTES.md` with net player-facing features, bugfix, and `v1.21.9` version history entry.
