# v1.20.41: Manifest Lifeforce Scaling Popup Architecture

## Summary & Motivation
Transforms Manifest Lifeforce from a flat, single-tier decision into an interactive Character Interaction modal popup (`bm_manifest_lifeforce_interaction`) offering four scaling piety investment tiers (100, 250, 500, and 1000 Piety) via exclusive radio button options (`send_option`), modeled after the initiation interaction popups. Adopts the **Guaranteed Floor + Escalating Ceiling** model where higher devotion guarantees increasingly safe minimum baselines while unlocking chances for higher-tier essence (including Superior Lifeforce), with probability curves dynamically scaling against the caster's Learning skill.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Interactive Character Interaction Popup (`bm_manifest_lifeforce_interaction`)**:
  - Accessible directly on self via character right-click or launched seamlessly from `bm_manifest_lifeforce_decision` via `open_interaction_window`.
  - Gated at `piety >= 100` and `piety_level >= 2` (1-year cooldown on interaction and decision flag).
  - Four exclusive devotion options (`send_option`), each with tailored Piety costs, Enlightenment XP gains, and Learning duels:
    1. **Tier 1 (100 Piety, +3 XP)**: Modest meditation. Minor Lifeforce is likely (~50-63%), Major Lifeforce is unlikely (~22-34%), failure and recoil exhaustion possible.
    2. **Tier 2 (250 Piety, +5 XP)**: Focused communion. Recoil exhaustion is 100% eliminated. Minor Lifeforce is dependable (~52-45%), Major Lifeforce becomes a realistic target (~41-55%).
    3. **Tier 3 (500 Piety, +8 XP)**: Profound crucible. **Zero Failure** (Minor Lifeforce is the guaranteed baseline floor, ~28-18%). Major Lifeforce is dominant (~66-70%). Elusive **tiny chance for Superior Lifeforce (~6.4% to 11.5%)**.
    4. **Tier 4 (1000 Piety, +12 XP)**: Transcendent epiphany. **Zero Failure, Zero Minor** (Major Lifeforce is the guaranteed baseline floor, ~67-52%). **Strong chance for Superior Lifeforce (~33% to 48%)**.
- **Player Quality of Life**:
  - Radio button options clearly display costs and odds. Players can easily change selection or close the modal window without penalty.
- **Story Panel & System Recount**:
  - Automatically invokes `bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect = yes` on every Lifeforce gain to keep story cycles and Situation panel counters perfectly synchronized.

## Key Decisions & Trade-Offs
- **Character Interaction Modal vs Event Popup**: Instead of triggering a narrative character event window, uses CK3's interaction window with `send_options_exclusive = yes` (matching `grant_blood_magic_interaction`), allowing instant side-by-side radio option comparison and dynamic cost display.
- **Dynamic Duels vs Static Checks**: Used CK3's native `duel` mechanism with `skill = learning` and calibrated `compare_modifier` weights rather than flat random chances, rewarding characters who build high Learning attributes (e.g. at 50 Learning, Tier 4 achieves nearly 1-in-2 Superior Lifeforce!).
- **Childbirth Major Lifeforce for Blood Mages (`bm_on_birth_bloodline_xp`)**:
  - Whenever a child is born, any parent with `lifestyle_blood_mage` (`scope:mother` and/or `scope:real_father`) gains `+1 Major Lifeforce` (`lifeforce_modifier_major`) alongside existing bloodline XP.
  - Blood Knights receive martial Vanguard XP without Lifeforce gain on birth.
- **Seek Power in Nature Restricted to Blood Knights (`seek_power_decision`)**:
  - Restricts `is_shown` in `bm_seek_power_decision.txt` to `has_trait = lifestyle_blood_knight` and `NOT = { has_trait = lifestyle_blood_mage }`.
  - Aligns description and tooltip to the Blood Knight martial hunting flavor (`seek_power_decision_blood_knight_desc` / `seek_power_decision_blood_knight_tooltip`). Blood Mages can no longer undertake wilderness hunts.
