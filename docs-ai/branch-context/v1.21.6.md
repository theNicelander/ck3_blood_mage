# v1.21.6: Prisoner Blood Pit Duel & Seek Captive Brokers
PR: #201

## Motivation
- `bm_cull_champion_interaction` overlapped with **Major Life drain** as a risk-free prisoner execution with an awkward name.
  - Replacing it with a contested **Blood Pit Duel** inside the consolidated Blood Drain menu rewards taking martial risk against formidable (`20+` Prowess) prisoners with an uncapped chance at **Superior Lifeforce**.
- Blood Mages who are at peace or not raiding needed a gold-sink way to acquire dungeon prisoners for Lifeforce, Blood Pit Duels, and trait/memory draining.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Blood Pit Duel** (`bm_blood_drain_interaction` -> `drain_blood_duel`, `bm_prisoner_blood_duel.0001`): Added as a radial option inside Blood Drain for `20+` Prowess prisoners (`50` Piety, non-lethal `practice` Single Combat Engine bout).
  - **Victory**: Executes the prisoner with standard `lifedrain_execution_effect`, grants `+4` **Fury** XP, and rolls the prisoner's uncapped `Prowess%` for **+1 Superior Lifeforce** (or **+1 Major Lifeforce** otherwise).
  - **Defeat**: Steps up the caster's wound level non-lethally, inflicts `+20` Stress, grants **+1 Minor Lifeforce** and `+1` **Fury** XP as recompense, and lets the prisoner escape (`release_from_prison = yes`).
  - **Invalidation**: Refunds `50` Piety.
  - **AI**: Only challenges when `actor.prowess >= recipient.prowess` and respects all hostile-magic protective vetoes.
- **Seek Captive Brokers** (`bm_buy_captives_decision`, `bm_buy_captives.0001`/`.0002`): Added decision for Blood Mages to inspect and buy `1–3` random captives into their dungeon (`+2` **Fortune** XP per purchase; 1-year cooldown after buying, 90-day walk-away cooldown).
  - **Stewardship scaling**: Captive count weights scale smoothly with player Stewardship (`60/20/20%` at `10`, `20/60/20%` at `20`, `0/50/50%` at `30`, `0/0/100%` at `50`).
  - **Pricing**: Base `30` gold + `5 × Prowess` (`+50` at `20+` Prowess) + stacking per-trait surcharges (`+50/+100/+150` tiered genetic, `+75` `fecund`/`giant`/`pure_blooded`, `+150` Blood Mage, `+25` per drainable commander, lifestyle, or martial fame trait).
  - **Trait pool**: Captives have a low chance to spawn with drainable genetic, Blood Mage, commander, lifestyle, or martial fame traits.
- **Remove Cull Fallen Champion**: Deleted `bm_cull_champion_interaction`, its script value, and its icon.
- **Current Situation Alert** (`bm_action_can_blood_duel_prisoner`): Replaced `bm_action_can_cull_champion` to alert when holding an eligible `20+` Prowess prisoner, opening Blood Drain directly.

### Other changes
- Renamed `bm_icelandic` and `bm_name_list_icelandic` display localization to **Icelander** (and updated `bm_icelandic_culture` game rule text) to distinguish it from vanilla CK3 1.21's newly added **Icelandic** culture while keeping `bm_icelandic` IDs for save compatibility.
- Disallowed **Go on a Blood Hunt** while travelling.
- Synced architecture docs (`blood-mage-interactions.md`, `blood-mage-lifeforce.md`, `blood-mage-track-xp-mage.md`), `RELEASE_NOTES.md`, and agent docs/skills.
