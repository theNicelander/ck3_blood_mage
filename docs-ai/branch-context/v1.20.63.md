# v1.20.63: Align House Covenants and Blood Power Across Six Mirrored Tracks

## Motivation

- House Covenants (7 covenants) and Blood Power (6 tracks) had misaligned themes, inconsistent track identities, and lacked universal mana scaling to sustain mid-to-late campaign ritual costs.
- Passive yearly covenant pulses fed generic Bloodline/Benediction XP instead of synergizing with personal Blood Power progression, disconnecting dynastic achievements from character growth.

## Changes

### Key changes

- Consolidate House Covenants from 7 to 6 mirrored disciplines: Majesty, Warband, Prosperity, Shadows, Mastery, and Legacy.
  - Unifies Covenant of Insight and Covenant of Mastery into a single Mastery covenant in `bm_empower_bloodline_decision`.
  - Upgrades cost 150/350/750 Piety and Minor/Major/Superior Lifeforce, granting +1/+2/+3 XP in `bloodline` (Mages) or `benediction` (Knights) with a 1-year cooldown.
- Add universal Monthly Piety baseline and 1:1 Devotion attribute scaling to both systems.
  - House Covenants grant +0.1 / +0.3 / +0.5 Monthly Piety across Tiers 1/2/3 (+3.0 total maxed), +0.1 / +0.3 / +0.5 Monthly Prestige on Majesty, plus +1 mapped attribute per Devotion Level at Tier 3.
  - Blood Power tracks grant +0.1 Monthly Piety per 10 XP (+1.0 total maxed, up to +6.0 across all 6 tracks), plus +1 mapped attribute per Devotion Level at 50 XP and +1 at 100 XP (+2 total).
  - Stat mapping: Majesty (Diplomacy), Warband (Martial), Prosperity (Stewardship), Shadows (Intrigue), Mastery (Learning), Legacy (Prowess).
- Rework yearly covenant pulse `blood_mage_yearly_events.002` into an independent feedback loop for Blood Power.
  - Each active house covenant rolls independently once per year for living Blood Mages and Blood Knights: 50% at Tier 1, 67% at Tier 2, 100% at Tier 3.
  - Success grants +1 XP in the matching `lifestyle_blood_power` track, capped at 100 XP, delivered in a single combined interface toast.
- Rename Blood Power `fury` track to `warband`, integrating army damage (+16%), advantage (+4), speed (+12%), dread baseline (+25), and Men-at-Arms regiments and regiment size (+2 each; +1 each on Covenant Tier 3).
- Retune Channel Blood Power (`bm_blood_power_event.001`) to grant +3 `enlightenment` XP alongside +10 track XP.

### Other changes

- Add debug decision `debug_upgrade_all_covenants` to step all 6 covenants simultaneously across Minor, Major, and Superior tiers.
- Sync architecture documentation across `blood-mage-blood-power.md`, `blood-mage-dynasty.md`, `blood-mage-traits.md`, `blood-mage-track-xp.md`, `blood-mage-track-xp-power.md`, and `blood-mage-decisions.md`.
