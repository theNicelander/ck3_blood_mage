# v1.20.63: Align House Covenants and Blood Power Across Six Mirrored Tracks

## Motivation

- House Covenants (7 covenants) and Blood Power (6 tracks) had misaligned themes, inconsistent track identities, and lacked universal mana scaling to sustain mid-to-late campaign ritual costs.
- Passive yearly covenant pulses fed generic Bloodline/Benediction XP instead of synergizing with personal Blood Power progression, disconnecting dynastic achievements from character growth.

## Changes

### Key changes

- Consolidate House Covenants from 7 to 6 mirrored disciplines: Presence, Warband, Prosperity, Shadows, Mastery, and Legacy.
  - Unifies Covenant of Insight and Covenant of Mastery into a single Mastery covenant in `bm_empower_bloodline_decision`.
  - Upgrades cost 150/350/750 Piety and Minor/Major/Superior Lifeforce, granting +1/+2/+3 XP in `bloodline` (Mages) or `benediction` (Knights) with a 1-year cooldown.
- Add universal Monthly Piety baseline and 1:1 Devotion attribute scaling to House Covenants.
  - House Covenants grant +0.1 / +0.3 / +0.5 Monthly Piety across Tiers 1/2/3 (+3.0 total maxed), +0.1 / +0.3 / +0.5 Monthly Prestige on Presence, plus +1 mapped attribute per Devotion Level at Tiers 2 and 3.
  - Stat mapping: Presence (Diplomacy), Warband (Martial), Prosperity (Stewardship), Shadows (Intrigue), Mastery (Learning), Legacy (Prowess).
  - Blood Power tracks grant +0.1 Monthly Piety per 10 XP (+1.0 total maxed, up to +6.0 across all 6 tracks) and focus strictly on up to 4 core personal modifiers per track.
- Rework yearly covenant pulse `blood_mage_yearly_events.002` into an independent feedback loop for Blood Power.
  - Each active house covenant rolls independently once per year for living Blood Mages and Blood Knights: 50% at Tier 1, 67% at Tier 2, 100% at Tier 3.
  - Success grants +1 XP in the matching `lifestyle_blood_power` track, capped at 100 XP, delivered in a single combined interface toast.
- Rebalance and streamline Blood Power and House Covenants:
  - Capped House Covenants at ~1/3 to 1/2 of Blood Power's personal strength to preserve individual character investment incentive.
  - Removed `*_per_prestige_level` from Tier III covenants, keeping attribute scaling strictly to Devotion level (+1 at Tiers 2 & 3).
  - Transferred broad realm/dynastic bonuses into House Covenants; re-tuned Warband covenant to grant army damage (+5% / +10% / +15%), movement speed (+5% / +10% / +15%), and capped MaA cap/size at 1.
  - Restructured Blood Power tracks into 5 milestone tiers (10, 30, 50, 70, 100 XP) with additive delta definitions, flat monthly lifestyle XP (+5 to +50/mo), and shared 50 & 100 XP milestone bonuses (+10% monthly Piety mult, +5 life expectancy, +0.25 health, +0.02 monthly Piety per knight) that merge into single tooltip lines across maxed tracks.
  - Presence: personal scheme capacity (+2), prestige gain (+1.5/mo), general opinion (+25), personal scheme success (+40), and legitimacy gain (+30% mult); Warband: knights (+8), knight effectiveness (+75%), advantage (+10), move speed (+25%); Prosperity: gold (+2.0/mo), MaA maintenance (-30%), and universal monthly income (+30% mult); Shadows: hostile scheme success (+40), defensive warding against enemy schemes (-50), dread baseline (+30), hostile scheme capacity (+2); Mastery: lifestyle XP (+50%), stress composure (-50% gain, +50% loss); Legacy: strengthen congenital chance (+50%), fertile years (+25), complete inbreeding immunity (-100%).
- Standardize income and legitimacy across the mod:
  - Universalized `monthly_income_mult` across Blood Power Prosperity (+30% mult total), Blood Mage bloodline (+24% mult total), House Covenants (+15% mult total), and Blood Runes (`bm_stave_nabrokarstafur` +10% mult), ensuring equal benefit for landed and unlanded rulers.
  - Added legitimacy scaling: Covenant of Presence (+5% / +10% / +15% `legitimacy_gain_mult`), Blood Power Presence (+5% / +10% / +16% / +23% / +30% `legitimacy_gain_mult`), and Ancient lifestyle track (+0.1 `monthly_legitimacy_add` per 10 XP up to +1.0/mo = +12/yr at 100 XP).
- Retune Channel Blood Power (`bm_blood_power_event.001`) to grant +3 `enlightenment` XP alongside +10 track XP.
