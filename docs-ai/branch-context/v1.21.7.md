# v1.21.7: Duel Lifeforce Unification, Rite Balance & Fortune De-Bloat
PR: #202

## Motivation
- Winning a Prisoner **Blood Pit Duel** (`bm_prisoner_blood_duel.0001`), a **Blood Hunt** duel (`seek_power.006`), or any lethal single combat kill (`bm_on_character_death_duel`) each used slightly different Lifeforce formulas.
  - Unifying them onto a single shared reward effect guarantees **+1 Major Lifeforce** plus the opponent's **Prowess%** chance for **+1 Superior Lifeforce** on every real single combat victory, while keeping **Blood Sparring Ring** Major-only.
- "Meet the Brokers" displayed a 10-line Requirements checklist even when all requirements were met, unlike Blood Hunt.
- **Major Exsanguination** (`bm_bleed_own_vein_interaction`) applied `lifedrained_modifier` on top of `wounded_2` and 25 Stress.
- **The Blood Rite** (`seek_power.011`) kept a ~4.2% failure floor even at maxed `prowess + learning`.
- Fortune modifiers carried excessive 1.21 trade bloat.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Unified Duel Victory Lifeforce** (`bm_duel_victory_lifeforce_effect`, `bm_duel_superior_chance_value`): Shared scripted effect and opponent-scoped value granting **+1 Major Lifeforce** plus an independent `opponent.prowess`% (`0-100%`) chance for **+1 Superior Lifeforce**.
  - **Prisoner Blood Pit Duel** (`bm_prisoner_blood_duel.0001`): Replaced the mutually exclusive Superior-or-Major roll with `bm_duel_victory_lifeforce_effect`.
  - **Lethal Single Combat Kills** (`bm_on_character_death_duel`): Replaced the hardcoded `+1 Major` + gated `25%` Superior roll with `bm_duel_victory_lifeforce_effect` inside the victory toast (no `20+` Prowess gate).
  - **Blood Hunt Duels** (`seek_power.006`): Replaced `+1 Major` + flat `25%` Superior with `bm_duel_victory_lifeforce_effect`.
  - **Blood Sparring Ring** (`bm_ritual_spar_events.0002`): Intentionally unchanged (**+1 Major Lifeforce** only, never Superior).
- **The Blood Rite** (`seek_power.011`): Lowered failure floor so maxed stats reach `0%` fail chance.
  - Lowered `compare_modifier` `min` floors on `blood_escaped` (`-22` -> `-25`) and `blood_cursed` (`-13` -> `-15`) for `prowess + learning >= 41`.
- **Bleed Own Veins - Major Exsanguination** (`bm_bleed_own_vein_interaction`): Removed `lifedrained_modifier` from `major_bleed`.
  - Both `minor_bleed` (+1 wound tier) and `major_bleed` (+2 wound tiers: `none` -> `wounded_2`, `wounded_1` -> `wounded_3`) now block at `wounded_2`/`wounded_3`/`maimed` (`25` Stress unchanged).
- **Meet the Brokers** (`bm_buy_captives_decision`): Hid satisfied requirements on the decision card.
  - Switched from `is_valid` (`is_available_adult = yes`) to `is_valid_showing_failures_only` matching Blood Hunt's availability checks (`is_in_activity = no`, `is_travelling = no`, `is_imprisoned = no`, `is_incapable = no`) plus `bm_buy_captives_cooldown`.
- **Condense Lifeforce** (`bm_minor_condense`, `bm_major_condense`): Lowered Piety costs.
  - Minor -> Major: `150` -> `100` Piety; Major -> Superior: `350` -> `250` Piety (including `ai_chance` thresholds).
- **Fortune Modifiers De-Bloat**: Removed 1.21 trade bloat so Fortune aligns with the modifier priority hierarchy (all playstyles > landed rulers > niche playstyles).
  - **Blessing of Fortune** (`bm_blessing_fortune_{minor,major,superior}_modifier`): Removed `trade_capacity_mult`, `trade_expertise_growth_mult`, and `trade_category_all_sell_price_mult` (restoring 3-line symmetry).
  - **Covenant of Fortune** (`dynasty_blood_fortune_modifier_1..3`): Removed `trade_capacity_mult`, `trade_post_limit_add`, `monthly_income_from_trade_mult`, `trade_competitiveness_mult`, and `domicile_build_gold_cost`, keeping `max_trade_routes = 1` at Tier III.
  - **Fortunate Lifeforce** (`lifeforce_modifier_fortunate`): Trimmed from 14 to 8 lines by removing `holding_build_*`, `domicile_build_*`, `trade_competitiveness_mult`, and `trade_capacity_mult`.
  - **Nábrókarstafur** (`bm_stave_nabrokarstafur`): Removed `monthly_income_from_trade_mult` and `trade_competitiveness_mult` (back to 3-line symmetry).
  - **Covenant of Radiance** (`dynasty_blood_radiance_modifier_3_desc`): Fixed stale `"dynastic renown"` wording to `"a personal tenet slot"` (mechanics unchanged).
