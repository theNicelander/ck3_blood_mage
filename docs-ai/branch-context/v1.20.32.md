# v1.20.32: Cumulative Trait Track Benefits Architecture Spec & Documentation Refresh

## Summary & Motivation
Establish a comprehensive living architecture reference for cumulative total benefits across all 15 tracks of the three core levelable traits (`lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_blood_knight`, and `lifestyle_blood_empowerment`) at 10, 50, and 100 XP, and synchronize architecture documentation across the mod.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Cumulative Trait Benefits Reference (`blood-mage-trait-benefits.md`)**:
  - Outlines exact cumulative benefits at 10, 50, and 100 XP in a 4-column matrix (`Track | 10 XP | 50 XP | 100 XP`), one row per track.
  - Documents Blood Mage (5 schools), Blood Knight (2 martial + 3 shared schools), and Blood Empowerment (5 domain tracks).
  - Explicitly documents vitality skipping at tiers 50/100 in Blood Mage / BK shared tracks (`ancient`, `benediction`, `enlightenment`) vs universal vitality retention in Empowerment and BK martial tracks (`vanguard`, `slaughter`).
  - Documents exact mechanics for somatic resilience on `ancient` (`negate_health_penalty = 0.25 / 0.50`, prowess age lock), `mastery` language scheme progression (tiers 30, 60, 90, 100), and base trait stats.
- **Blood Mage and Blood Knight Trait Track Restructure**:
  - Reorganized tracks across `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight` into 3 shared tracks and 2 exclusive tracks each:
    - **Shared (3 tracks, 1:1 on both)**: `ancient` (Piety, longevity, somatic agelessness), `benediction` (Diplomacy, healing, elevation), and `enlightenment` (Learning, lifeforce generation, self-enhancement).
    - **Blood Mage Exclusive (2 tracks)**: `bloodline` (Stewardship, dynasty, golems) and `hematurgy` (Intrigue, lifedrain, dark rites).
    - **Blood Knight Exclusive (2 tracks)**: `vanguard` (Martial, commanding armies, battlefield protection) and `slaughter` (Prowess, single combat fatal duels, lethal harvesting).
  - Retired `resilience` from Blood Knight; moved `negate_health_penalty` and age prowess lock into `ancient` at 50 and 100 XP.
  - Aligned `enlightenment` milestones to `learning_per_piety_level = 1` (at 50 and 100 XP).
  - Unified `bm_manifest_lifeforce_decision` for both traits to award +3 `enlightenment` XP via a `learning` skill check.
  - Updated elevation trial (`bm_elevate_to_blood_mage_decision`) to carry over `ancient`, `benediction`, and `enlightenment` 1:1, converting 50% of martial tracks (`vanguard` -> `bloodline`, `slaughter` -> `hematurgy`).
- **Architecture Documentation Sync**:
  - Registered `blood-mage-trait-benefits.md` in `docs-ai/architecture/AGENTS.md` and `docs-ai/architecture/README.md`.
  - Harmonized architecture documentation across `docs-ai/architecture/*.md` to reflect current 1.20 CK3 implementation.

## Key Decisions
- **4-Column Track Format**: Chose `Track | 10 XP | 50 XP | 100 XP` with 1 row per track to provide maximum comparison density across tiers.
- **Shared vs Exclusive Tracks**: Shared the universal mystical/foundation tracks (`ancient`, `benediction`, `enlightenment`) between both Mage and Knight, giving Blood Knight martial/prowess supremacy (`vanguard`, `slaughter`) and Blood Mage governance/subterfuge supremacy (`bloodline`, `hematurgy`).
- **Dedicated Spec vs In-Trait Duplication**: Placed cumulative calculation matrix into dedicated `blood-mage-trait-benefits.md` while keeping `blood-mage-traits.md` focused on concepts and milestones.
