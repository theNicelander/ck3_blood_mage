# v1.20.76: Replace Bloodline Gold per Piety with Monthly Income & Domain Limit

## Motivation

- Engine limitation on character modifiers: `monthly_income_per_piety_level` is a native CK3 modifier intended exclusively for province holdings, buildings, and domiciles (`province_modifier`). When scripted onto characters or traits, the engine fails silently, providing no gold to rulers per Level of Devotion.
- Robust economic and realm expansion: Replace the ineffective income modifier with direct flat `monthly_income` and scaling `domain_limit`, rewarding bloodline masters with substantial monthly treasury income and expanded demesne holding capacity.
- Prevent tier elevation power loss: The sequential tier rework intended that ascending to Blood Mage or Blood Archmage preserves all cumulative milestone benefits up to 50 XP across tracks (and 100 XP for Ancient). Previously, only 50 XP attribute milestones were present on the base traits, dropping accumulated scheme slots, success chances, opinion, army damage, income multipliers, and Ancient renown/tenets on ascension.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Monthly Income & Domain Limit on Bloodline Track (`common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt`)**:
  - Replaced all instances of `monthly_income_per_piety_level` across all three sequential tiers (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`, `lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_bm_archmage`) with `monthly_income` and `domain_limit`:
    - **Blood Acolyte (Tier I)**: `+0.5` monthly income and `+1` domain limit at 50 XP; `+0.5` monthly income and `+1` domain limit at 100 XP (cumulative `+1.0` monthly income, `+2` domain limit).
    - **Blood Mage (Tier II)**: `+0.5` monthly income and `+1` domain limit absorbed into base trait floor; `+1.0` monthly income and `+1` domain limit at 50 XP; `+1.0` monthly income and `+1` domain limit at 100 XP (cumulative `+2.5` monthly income, `+3` domain limit).
    - **Blood Archmage (Tier III)**: `+1.5` monthly income and `+2` domain limit absorbed into base trait floor; `+1.5` monthly income and `+1` domain limit at 50 XP; `+1.5` monthly income and `+1` domain limit at 100 XP (cumulative `+4.5` monthly income, `+4` domain limit).
- **Restore Full Cumulative Milestone Base Trait Floors (`common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt`)**:
  - **Blood Mage (`lifestyle_blood_mage`)**: Base trait absorbs all Tier I benefits up to 50 XP (and 100 XP Ancient): 2.0 health, 30 life expectancy, +1.0 renown, +0.2 legitimacy, +30% devotion impact, +2 personal tenet slots, +1 max devotion level, +1 to all skills & prowess per piety level, +4 opinion, +4 personal scheme success, +1 max personal scheme, +3 knights, +6% army damage, +0.5 monthly income, +1 domain limit, +8% income, -6% army maint, +0.1 capital dev growth, +6 hostile scheme success, -8 enemy scheme success, +1 max hostile scheme, +0.50 piety, +12% piety mult, and permanent age lock.
  - **Blood Archmage (`lifestyle_bm_archmage`)**: Base trait absorbs all Tier I + Tier II benefits up to 50 XP (and 100 XP Ancient): 5.0 health, 100 life expectancy, +3.0 renown, +0.6 legitimacy, +90% devotion impact, +4 personal tenet slots, +2 max devotion level, +2 to all skills & prowess per piety level, +12 opinion, +12 personal scheme success, +2 max personal schemes, +6 knights, +16% army damage, +1.5 monthly income, +2 domain limit, +20% income, -16% army maint, +0.3 capital dev growth, +16 hostile scheme success, -24 enemy scheme success, +2 max hostile schemes, +1.50 piety, +32% piety mult, and permanent age lock.
