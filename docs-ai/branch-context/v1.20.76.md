# v1.20.76: Replace Bloodline Gold per Piety with Men-at-Arms Maintenance by Dread

## Motivation

- Engine limitation on character modifiers: `monthly_income_per_piety_level` is a native CK3 modifier intended exclusively for province holdings, buildings, and domiciles (`province_modifier`). When scripted onto characters or traits, the engine fails silently, providing no gold to rulers per Level of Devotion.
- Dynamic dread scaling for military upkeep: Replace the ineffective income modifier with native dread-scaling Men-at-Arms maintenance (`men_at_arms_maintenance_per_dread_mult`), rewarding tyrannical and terrifying blood mages with heavily reduced regiment upkeep as their dread mounts.
- Prevent tier elevation power loss: The sequential tier rework intended that ascending to Blood Mage or Blood Archmage preserves all cumulative milestone benefits up to 50 XP across tracks (and 100 XP for Ancient). Previously, only 50 XP attribute milestones were present on the base traits, dropping accumulated scheme slots, success chances, opinion, army damage, income multipliers, and Ancient renown/tenets on ascension.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Men-at-Arms Maintenance by Dread on Bloodline Track (`common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt`)**:
  - Replaced all instances of `monthly_income_per_piety_level` across all three sequential tiers (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`, `lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_bm_archmage`) with `men_at_arms_maintenance_per_dread_mult`:
    - **Blood Acolyte (Tier I)**: `-0.001` (-0.1% per dread $\rightarrow$ -10% at 100 dread) at 50 XP, `-0.001` at 100 XP (cumulative -20% MAA upkeep at 100 dread).
    - **Blood Mage (Tier II)**: `-0.001` absorbed into base trait floor, `-0.002` (-0.2% per dread) at 50 XP, `-0.002` at 100 XP (cumulative -40% from track, combined -50% MAA upkeep at 100 dread).
    - **Blood Archmage (Tier III)**: `-0.003` absorbed into base trait floor, `-0.003` (-0.3% per dread) at 50 XP, `-0.003` at 100 XP (cumulative -60% from track, combined -90% MAA upkeep at 100 dread).
- **Restore Full Cumulative Milestone Base Trait Floors (`common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt`)**:
  - **Blood Mage (`lifestyle_blood_mage`)**: Base trait absorbs all Tier I benefits up to 50 XP (and 100 XP Ancient): 2.0 health, 30 life expectancy, +1.0 renown, +0.2 legitimacy, +30% devotion impact, +2 personal tenet slots, +1 max devotion level, +1 to all skills & prowess per piety level, +4 opinion, +4 personal scheme success, +1 max personal scheme, +3 knights, +6% army damage, -0.1% MAA maint/dread, +8% income, -6% army maint, +0.1 capital dev growth, +6 hostile scheme success, -8 enemy scheme success, +1 max hostile scheme, +0.50 piety, +12% piety mult, and permanent age lock.
  - **Blood Archmage (`lifestyle_bm_archmage`)**: Base trait absorbs all Tier I + Tier II benefits up to 50 XP (and 100 XP Ancient): 5.0 health, 100 life expectancy, +3.0 renown, +0.6 legitimacy, +90% devotion impact, +4 personal tenet slots, +2 max devotion level, +2 to all skills & prowess per piety level, +12 opinion, +12 personal scheme success, +2 max personal schemes, +6 knights, +16% army damage, -0.3% MAA maint/dread, +20% income, -16% army maint, +0.3 capital dev growth, +16 hostile scheme success, -24 enemy scheme success, +2 max hostile schemes, +1.50 piety, +32% piety mult, and permanent age lock.
