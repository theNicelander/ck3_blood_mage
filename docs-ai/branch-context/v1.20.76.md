# v1.20.76: Replace Bloodline Gold per Piety with Men-at-Arms Maintenance by Dread

## Motivation

- Engine limitation on character modifiers: `monthly_income_per_piety_level` is a native CK3 modifier intended exclusively for province holdings, buildings, and domiciles (`province_modifier`). When scripted onto characters or traits, the engine fails silently, providing no gold to rulers per Level of Devotion.
- Dynamic dread scaling for military upkeep: Replace the ineffective income modifier with native dread-scaling Men-at-Arms maintenance (`men_at_arms_maintenance_per_dread_mult`), rewarding tyrannical and terrifying blood mages with heavily reduced regiment upkeep as their dread mounts.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Men-at-Arms Maintenance by Dread on Bloodline Track (`common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt`)**:
  - Replaced all instances of `monthly_income_per_piety_level` across all three sequential tiers (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`, `lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_bm_archmage`) with `men_at_arms_maintenance_per_dread_mult`:
    - **Blood Acolyte (Tier I)**: `-0.001` (-0.1% per dread $\rightarrow$ -10% at 100 dread) at 50 XP, `-0.001` at 100 XP (cumulative -20% MAA upkeep at 100 dread).
    - **Blood Mage (Tier II)**: `-0.001` absorbed into base trait floor, `-0.002` (-0.2% per dread) at 50 XP, `-0.002` at 100 XP (cumulative -40% from track, combined -50% MAA upkeep at 100 dread).
    - **Blood Archmage (Tier III)**: `-0.003` absorbed into base trait floor, `-0.003` (-0.3% per dread) at 50 XP, `-0.003` at 100 XP (cumulative -60% from track, combined -90% MAA upkeep at 100 dread).

### Other changes
