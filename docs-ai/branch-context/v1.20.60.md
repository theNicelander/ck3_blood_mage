# v1.20.60: XP rebalance, Channel lifestyle progression, and prisoner/cull actions

## Motivation

- Instruction additions were too verbose, wasting tokens.
- Condense Lifeforce granted Ancient XP instead of Enlightenment XP, distorting progression.
- Ancient XP sources were too broad and inconsistent across events and decisions.
- Channel <Skill> modifiers lacked lifestyle progression, unlike Blood Power.
- Managing eligible prisoners and fallen champions required manual character searching.

## Changes

- Terse squash-merge rule in `manage-pr`: `gh pr merge --squash` without `--subject` preserves `(#<PR>)`.
- Mandated `<branch>: <outcome title>` PR title format.
- Added token-saving and minimal-instructions principle to `AGENTS.md`.
- `bm_attune_lifeforce_decision`: AI now takes decision with 100% chance when un-attuned (`bm_has_any_attunement_trigger = no`); removed health penalty on initial attunement; lowered `ai_check_interval` to 3 months; explicit `ai_potential`.
- Condense Lifeforce (`bm_minor_condense` and `bm_major_condense`) now awards Enlightenment XP instead of Ancient XP.
- Restricted Ancient XP strictly to yearly pulses, attunement ticks, blood rune inscriptions, childbirth milestones (2 XP per child, +3 extra for firstborn), anointed heir transfer, and Wise One generation.
- Migrated miscellaneous Ancient XP in Seek Power events, interpersonal events, and Beauty trait manifestations to Enlightenment XP.
- Added +10 monthly (+30 quarterly) Lifestyle XP to Channel <Skill> modifiers (Diplomacy, Martial, Stewardship, Intrigue, Learning, and Prowess->Martial), plus +5 monthly (+15 quarterly) to active focus for Channel Experience Gain, via `quarterly_playable_pulse`.
- Added Current Situation important action notifications for Prisoner Lifedrain (`bm_action_can_lifedrain_prisoner`) and Cull Fallen Champion (`bm_action_can_cull_champion`), surfacing available dungeon prisoners and fallen champions with direct clicks to their interaction windows.
- Restricted `bm_cull_champion_interaction` to characters with prowess >= 20 across all valid targets (prisoners and fallen followers).
