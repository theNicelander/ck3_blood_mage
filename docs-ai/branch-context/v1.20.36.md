# v1.20.36: Align Trait, Faith, Interaction, and Situation Icons

## Summary & Motivation
Align all traits, character interactions, faiths, trait level tracks, and story cycle panel references with the newly iterated icons introduced in commit `bd071f4` (`bm_blodtru.dds`, `bm_blood_knight.dds`, `bm_blood_mage.dds`, `bm_interactions.dds`). Maintain copy-alias DDS files for backward compatibility so legacy saves and un-migrated paths never encounter missing textures.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Faith & Religion Icons (`gfx/interface/icons/faith/`)**:
  - Propagated `bm_blodtru.dds` across the Blóðtrú religion family: overwritten `blodtru_faith.dds`, `blodtru_faith_reformed.dds`, `blodtru_religion.dds`, `ketsudo_faith.dds`, `ketsudo_faith_reformed.dds`, `cult_of_blood.dds`, `cult_of_blood_reformed.dds`, `cult_of_blood_saturated.dds`, and created `bm_blodtru.dds`.
  - Also automatically refreshes `@bm_blodtru_icon!` and `@bm_ketsudo_icon!` texticons defined in `gui/bm_game_rule_texticons.gui` and `gui/shared/bm_texticons_religion.gui`.
- **Blood Knight Trait (`lifestyle_blood_knight`)**:
  - Pointed trait icon definition in `common/traits/bm_blood_knight_trait.txt` to `bm_blood_knight.dds` (was `blood_mage_3.dds`).
  - Updated Situation story cycle branch in `common/story_cycles/bm_blood_mage_story.txt` to `gfx/interface/icons/traits/bm_blood_knight.dds`.
  - Maintained `gfx/interface/icons/traits/blood_mage_3.dds` as an identical copy of `bm_blood_knight.dds`.
- **Blood Mage Trait (`lifestyle_blood_mage`)**:
  - Pointed trait icon definition in `common/traits/bm_blood_mage_trait.txt` to `bm_blood_mage.dds` (was `blood_mage.dds`).
  - Updated Situation story cycle default in `common/story_cycles/bm_blood_mage_story.txt` to `gfx/interface/icons/traits/bm_blood_mage.dds`.
  - Maintained `gfx/interface/icons/traits/blood_mage.dds` as an identical copy of `bm_blood_mage.dds`.
- **Character Interactions, Blood Empowerment & Ancient Track**:
  - Standardized on `bm_interactions.dds`.
  - Pointed `lifestyle_blood_empowerment` in `common/traits/bm_blood_empowerment_trait.txt` to `bm_interactions.dds` (was `blood_mage_no_background.dds`).
  - Maintained `gfx/interface/icons/traits/blood_mage_no_background.dds` as an identical copy of `bm_interactions.dds`.
  - Verified all 14 interaction DDS files in `gfx/interface/icons/character_interactions/` and `gfx/interface/icons/trait_level_tracks/ancient.dds` match `bm_interactions.dds`.
- **Blood Shrine Domicile Buildings**:
  - Enabled construction potential (`can_construct_potential`) and AI weighting (`ai_value`) for Blood Knights (`lifestyle_blood_knight`) alongside Blood Mages across all five tiers (`bm_domicile_blood_shrine_01..05`).
- **Blood Runes Accessibility**:
  - Minor Blood Rune in Cast Minor Blood Magic (`bm_cast_blood_magic_minor.001`) and Major Blood Rune in Cast Major Blood Magic (`bm_cast_blood_magic_major.001`) are available to both Blood Mages and Blood Knights without trait requirements on options, relying entirely on the parent decisions.
  - Updated yearly rune pulse `blood_mage_yearly_events.003` to trigger on rune modifiers (`minor_blood_rune_modifier`, `major_blood_rune_modifier`, `superior_blood_rune_modifier`) rather than requiring `lifestyle_blood_mage`, granting passive rolls to all rune holders.
- **Runtime Fixes & Basegame Modularity**:
  - Scoped `has_trait` check in `common/story_cycles/bm_blood_mage_story.txt` icon trigger to `story_owner = { ... }`, resolving 178 `Wrong scope for trigger: story` engine errors.
  - Removed duplicate holy site definitions overriding vanilla entries (`luoyang`, `hangzhou`, `quanzhou`, `dunhuang`, `tenerife`), resolving all `database_conflicts.log` overrides.
  - Promoted vanilla-eminent holy sites `tenerife` and `dunhuang` to `eminent_holy_sites` in `blodtru_faith` and `xuedao_faith`.
