# v1.20.47: Rename Blood Empowerment to Blood Power

## Summary & Motivation
Renames the secondary lifestyle trait from **Blood Empowerment** (`lifestyle_blood_empowerment`) to **Blood Power** (`lifestyle_blood_power`), and re-labels the Cast Major Blood Magic Grimoire action to **Channel Blood Power** (`bm_cast_blood_magic_major.001.power`). Replaces long, corporate-sounding terminology with concise, direct language that fits naturally alongside **Blood Mage** and **Blood Knight**.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Trait Definition & Rename (`lifestyle_blood_power`)**:
  - Renamed `common/traits/bm_blood_empowerment_trait.txt` -> `common/traits/bm_blood_power_trait.txt`.
  - Top-level trait identifier changed from `lifestyle_blood_empowerment` to `lifestyle_blood_power` across all 5 tracks (`dynasty`, `mastery`, `presence`, `prosperity`, `shadows`).
- **Major Grimoire Integration & Events (`events/bm_blood_power_event.txt`)**:
  - Renamed `events/bm_blood_empowerment_event.txt` -> `events/bm_blood_power_event.txt`.
  - Namespace updated to `bm_blood_power_event` with root event `bm_blood_power_event.001`.
  - Major Grimoire option updated to `bm_cast_blood_magic_major.001.power` ("Channel Blood Power") with tooltip `bm_cast_blood_magic_major.001.power_tt`.
- **Scripted Effects & Triggers (`common/scripted_effects/`, `common/scripted_triggers/`)**:
  - `bm_blood_mage_lifecycle_effects.txt`: Updated `bm_ensure_blood_mage_story_effect`, `bm_become_blood_mage_effect`, `bm_apply_blood_mage_prevalence_retention_effect`, `bm_become_blood_knight_effect`, and `bm_apply_blood_knight_prevalence_retention_effect` to check and grant `lifestyle_blood_power`.
  - `bm_trait_track_xp_gain_effects.txt`: Renamed XP effects to `bm_add_blood_power_xp_effect` and `add_blood_power_xp`, while maintaining backwards compatibility aliases `bm_add_blood_empowerment_xp_effect`, `add_blood_empowerment_xp`, and `add_crimson_empowerment_xp`.
  - `bm_trait_triggers.txt`: Renamed trigger to `bm_has_blood_power_xp_trigger`, preserving `bm_has_blood_empowerment_xp_trigger` as alias.
  - `bm_geyser_duel_events.txt`: Updated historical Egill Skallagrímsson character templates to use `lifestyle_blood_power`.
  - `bm_debug_decisions.txt`: Updated debug decision to `debug_add_blood_power_exp`.
- **Localization Updates (`localization/english/`)**:
  - `bm_traits_l_english.yml`: Updated `trait_lifestyle_blood_power` to "Blood Power" and maintained legacy trait key as fallback alias.
  - `bm_cast_blood_magic_self_l_english.yml`: Updated Education XP requirements to dynamically query `lifestyle_blood_power`, and updated Grimoire and event keys to "Channel Blood Power".
  - `bm_debug_l_english.yml`: Updated debug action localization to "BLOOD MAGE: Add Experience - Blood Power".
- **Documentation & Subsystems**:
  - Renamed `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-blood-empowerment.md` -> `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-blood-power.md`.
  - Updated all architecture references across `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `blood-mage-traits.md`, `blood-mage-track-xp.md`, `blood-mage-magic-tiers.md`, `blood-mage-prevalence-and-lifecycle.md`, `blood-mage-decisions.md`, `blood-mage-events-overview.md`, `blood-mage-lifeforce.md`, `blood-mage-blood-knight.md`, `description.txt`, `RELEASE_NOTES.md`, and skills/rules.

## Key Decisions
- **Blood Power over Blood Empowerment**: "Blood Power" is concise (2 syllables), direct, and sits cleanly alongside "Blood Mage" and "Blood Knight", eliminating confusion with "Empower Bloodline" and "Empower Blood Knight".
- **Channel Blood Power in Grimoire**: Prefixing the Grimoire action button with "Channel" provides an active verb matching "Condense Lifeforce" and "Inscribe Blood Runes".
- **Script ID Renaming with Fallback Aliases**: Renamed internal IDs to `lifestyle_blood_power` for long-term codebase hygiene while preserving scripted XP aliases and localization aliases so nothing errors or breaks silently.
