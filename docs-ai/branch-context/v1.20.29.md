# v1.20.29

## Summary & Motivation

Branch `v1.20.29` expands **Blood Knight** gameplay by granting Blood Knights (`lifestyle_blood_knight`) access to minor blood magic and power-seeking while establishing strict trait safeguards and Blood Mage (`lifestyle_blood_mage`) precedence:
- **Trait Acquisition Safeguards**: Fixes CK3 engine exploit where calling `add_trait_xp` on a track trait when the character does not possess the trait can bestow the trait at rank 0. All XP gain effects are wrapped with strict `has_trait` guards.
- **Minor Blood Magic for Knights**: Blood Knights can now open the minor blood magic grimoire, manifest lifeforce, seek power in the wilderness, cure minor illnesses, and attune minor lifeforce.
- **Blood Mage Precedence**: `lifestyle_blood_mage` is strictly the superior trait. Blood Knights receive a curated subset of minor abilities. Characters holding both traits defer to Blood Mage decisions and full ritual options.
- **Strict Prohibition on Draining**: Blood Knights cannot lifedrain or trait-drain characters.
- **Martial Lifeforce Acquisition**: Blood Knights earn Minor Lifeforce naturally through martial feats: lethal duel victories and battlefield slaughter.

## Key Decisions

- **Trait Acquisition Firewall**: Wrapping `add_trait_xp` calls within `has_trait` checks guarantees that Blood Knights channeling minor lifeforce or receiving shared effects never accidentally gain `lifestyle_blood_mage` without undergoing official initiation rites (`bm_become_blood_mage_effect`).
- **Blood Mage Precedence**: `lifestyle_blood_mage` is strictly the superior trait. Blood Knights receive a curated subset of minor abilities (minor blood magic grimoire, minor healing, manifesting lifeforce, and annual wilderness power-seeking) while higher-tier rites (lifedraining, trait theft, major/superior rituals) remain strictly reserved for Blood Mages. Characters holding both traits always defer to Blood Mage decisions and full ritual options.
- **Martial Power Fantasy**: Blood Knights acquire lifeforce primarily through combat and martial trials rather than occult rituals or draining prisoners.
