# v1.20-11

## Summary & Motivation

Branch `v1.20-11` implements the East Asian branch of the Blóðtrú religion—**Ketsudō / Xuedao (`ketsudo_faith`)**—into the mod for CK3 1.20+, expanding blood magic spirituality across Japan, China, Korea, and frontier regions. It establishes a 52-node sacred geography network, cultural heritage conversion routing, consolidates blood magic initiation into the "Ritual of Blood" anchored at Reykjavik and Tsushima, and relaxes scholarly/theological prerequisites.

## Core Concepts & High-Level Changes

- **Ketsudō Faith (`ketsudo_faith`) & Mainline Rite (`ketsudo_faith`)**:
  - Root-level faith under `blodtru_religion` (`rf_blodtru`) sharing adaptive tolerance, ancestor worship, and ritual celebration tenets.
  - Linked 1:1 to a scripted mainline rite in `common/religion/rite_types/bm_rite_types.txt` to prevent dynamic unlocalized rite generation.
- **East Asian Sacred Geography (52 Holy Sites)**:
  - 12 Eminent Holy Sites: Primary spiritual centers and imperial capitals granting faith-wide stat scaling per piety rank (`tsushima`, `kyoto`, `edo`, `mount_fuji`, `changan`, `luoyang`, `beijing`, `nanjing`, `kaifeng`, `taishan`, `kaesong`, `hanseong`).
  - 40 Regular Holy Sites: Sacred volcanic peaks, Wǔyuè mountains, Buddhist sanctuaries, and regional capitals across Japan, China, Korea, and frontier realms granting cyclical health, life expectancy, and epidemic resistance modifiers to county holders.
- **Cultural Heritage Conversion Routing**:
  - `bm_convert_to_blood_cult_by_heritage_effect` dynamically routes characters of East Asian cultural pillars (`heritage_japonic`, `heritage_chinese`, `heritage_korean`, `heritage_mongolic`, `heritage_tibetan`, `heritage_tungusic`, `heritage_viet`, `heritage_qiangic`, `heritage_tai`) to `ketsudo_faith` when taking `become_blood_cultist_decision`.
- **Ritual of Blood (`bm_enhance_blood_ritual_decision`)**:
  - Renamed from "Ritual of Crimson Initiation" to "Ritual of Blood".
  - Requires physical presence at the sacred initiation sanctuaries of either Reykjavik (`c_vestisland`) or Tsushima (`c_tsushima`).
  - Relaxed prerequisites so that `theologian` or `lifestyle_scholar` (Religious Scholar) with 100+ XP each qualify individually for the learning/spiritual trigger condition.
  - Removed redundant separate "Challenge the Blood Mage of Reykjavik" and "Challenge the Blood Mage of Tsushima" decisions and event files.

## Key Decisions

- **Unified Initiation Ritual over Fragmented Duels**:
  - Rather than maintaining fragmented decision and event chains for separate regional challenges, outsider initiation is consolidated into the core internal struggle ritual (`bm_enhance_blood_ritual_decision`), geographically anchored to either Reykjavik (West) or Tsushima (East).
- **Relaxed Theological Prerequisites**:
  - Unlocked `theologian` and `lifestyle_scholar` (100+ XP) as independent qualifying conditions rather than requiring both simultaneously.
- **Dedicated Holy Site Keys for East Asian Sacred Peaks**:
  - Defined custom holy site entries in `bm_holy_sites.txt` rather than relying solely on vanilla religion keys, ensuring blood magic stat bonuses and vitality modifiers are applied consistently across all 52 nodes.
- **Heritage-Driven Faith Assignment**:
  - Rather than forcing all blood cultists globally into European `blodtru_faith`, `become_blood_cultist_decision` dynamically assigns `ketsudo_faith` based on cultural heritage, preserving narrative coherence across Asia.
