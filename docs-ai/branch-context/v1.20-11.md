# v1.20-11

## Summary & Motivation

Branch `v1.20-11` implements the East Asian branches of the Blóðtrú religion—**Ketsudō (`ketsudo_faith`)** for Japan and Korea, and **Xuédào (`xuedao_faith`)** for the Chinese Central Plains and Sinitic sphere—into the mod for CK3 1.20+. It establishes dedicated sacred geography networks anchored in sacred peaks and ancient capitals, cultural heritage conversion routing, consolidates blood magic initiation into the "Ritual of Blood" anchored at Reykjavik and Tsushima, and relaxes scholarly/theological prerequisites.

## Core Concepts & High-Level Changes

- **Ketsudō Faith (`ketsudo_faith`) & Xuédào Faith (`xuedao_faith`)**:
  - Root-level faiths under `blodtru_religion` (`rf_blodtru`) sharing adaptive tolerance, ancestor worship, and ritual celebration tenets.
  - Linked 1:1 to scripted mainline rites in `bm_rite_types.txt` and `bm_xuedao_rite.txt` to prevent dynamic unlocalized rite generation.
- **Dedicated Sacred Geographies**:
  - `ketsudo_faith`: Anchored in Japan with an 8-Eminent mountain/seat configuration (`tsushima`, `kyoto`, `kamakura`, `edo`, `mount_fuji`, `mount_hiei`, `mount_aso`, `mount_osore`) scaling per piety level, and 11 regular regional holy sites (`mount_yotei`, `hakodate`, `matsuyama`, `dazaifu`, `kagoshima`, `naha`, `kotohira`, `hiraizumi`, `izumo`, `nagoya`, `niigata`) granting health, longevity, and epidemic resistance to county holders.
  - `xuedao_faith`: Anchored in China with 8 Eminent Holy Sites (`tsushima`, `changan`, `kaifeng`, `hangzhou`, `luoyang`, `beijing`, `taishan`, `huashan`) and 17 Regular Holy Sites (`songshan`, `hengshan_north`, `hengshan_south`, `mount_wutai`, `mount_emei`, `mount_jiuhua`, `huangshan`, `putuoshan`, `nanjing`, `chengdu`, `guangzhou`, `quanzhou`, `fuzhou`, `kunming`, `dunhuang`, `taipei`, `lhasa`).
- **Shared Initiation Duel Sanctuary**:
  - Both East Asian faiths share `tsushima` (`c_tsushima`, `b_yora`) as an Eminent holy site and initiation sanctuary, serving as the Eastern counterpart to Reykjavik. Outsiders may travel to Tsushima to challenge the hermit blood master via `bm_tsushima_blood_mage_duel_decision`.
- **Cultural Heritage Conversion Routing**:
  - `bm_convert_to_blood_cult_by_heritage_effect` routes Chinese-sphere cultural heritages (`heritage_chinese`, `heritage_qiangic`, `heritage_tai`, `heritage_viet`, `heritage_tibetan`) to `xuedao_faith`, Japonic/Korean/Mongolic/Tungusic heritages to `ketsudo_faith`, and other rulers to `blodtru_faith`.
- **Ritual of Blood & Initiation Duels**:
  - Outsider initiation is available either through the internal crucible `bm_enhance_blood_ritual_decision` anchored at Reykjavik or Tsushima, or through the direct duel challenge `bm_tsushima_blood_mage_duel_decision` on Tsushima against the hermit blood master.
  - Relaxed prerequisites on Ritual of Blood so that `theologian` or `lifestyle_scholar` (100+ XP) each qualify individually.

## Key Decisions

- **Decoupling Japan and China into Dedicated Faith Branches**:
  - Rather than combining all East Asian cultures into a single omnibus faith, `ketsudo_faith` represents the Japanese spiritual sphere with volcanic peaks and imperial/administrative seats, while `xuedao_faith` represents the Central Plains Sinitic sphere, sharing `tsushima` as a blood magic initiation hub.
- **Dedicated Holy Site File Organization (`bm_china_holy_sites.txt`)**:
  - All 23 Chinese holy sites are isolated into `bm_china_holy_sites.txt` using vanilla 1.20 province titles and verified county/barony targets (e.g., `c_jiangning` for Nanjing, `c_guangzhou_1` for Canton, `c_fuzhou_1` for Fujian, `c_tanzhou` for Mount Heng South, `c_mingzhou_1` for Mount Putuo, `c_hoanya` for Taipei, `c_shazhou` for Dunhuang).
- **Japanese Sacred Geography**:
  - `ketsudo_faith` features 8 Eminent sites strictly using `faith_character_modifier` scaling per piety level and 11 regular sites strictly using `county_holder_character_modifier` providing health, life expectancy, fertility, and epidemic resistance.
- **Relaxed Theological Prerequisites**:
  - Unlocked `theologian` and `lifestyle_scholar` (100+ XP) as independent qualifying conditions rather than requiring both simultaneously.
