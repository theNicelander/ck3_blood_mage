# v1.20-11

## Summary & Motivation

Branch `v1.20-11` implements the East Asian branches of the Blóðtrú religion—**Ketsudō (`ketsudo_faith`)** for Japan and Korea, and **Xuédào (`xuedao_faith`)** for the Chinese Central Plains and Sinitic sphere—into the mod for CK3 1.20+. It establishes dedicated sacred geography networks anchored in sacred peaks and ancient capitals, cultural heritage conversion routing, consolidates blood magic initiation into the "Ritual of Blood" anchored at Reykjavik and Tsushima, and relaxes scholarly/theological prerequisites.

## Core Concepts & High-Level Changes

- **Ketsudō Faith (`ketsudo_faith`) & Xuédào Faith (`xuedao_faith`)**:
  - Root-level faiths under `blodtru_religion` (`rf_blodtru`) sharing adaptive tolerance, ancestor worship, and ritual celebration tenets.
  - Linked 1:1 to scripted mainline rites in `bm_rite_types.txt` and `bm_xuedao_rite.txt` to prevent dynamic unlocalized rite generation.
- **Dedicated Sacred Geographies**:
  - `ketsudo_faith`: Anchored across Japan and Korea with a 6-Eminent configuration (`yamashiro`, `kamakura`, `mount_fuji`, `mount_aso`, `mount_osore`, `gyeongju`) scaling per piety level, and 21 regular regional holy sites (`tsushima`, `edo`, `hakodate`, `mount_yotei`, `mount_hiei`, `mount_koya`, `ise`, `naniwa`, `shinano`, `matsuyama`, `dazaifu`, `kagoshima`, `naha`, `kotohira`, `hiraizumi`, `izumo`, `nagoya`, `niigata`, `mount_baekdu`, `gaegyeong`, `hanyang`) granting health, longevity, and epidemic resistance to county holders.
  - `xuedao_faith`: Anchored across China and Korea with 8 Eminent Holy Sites (`changan`, `luoyang`, `beijing`, `hangzhou`, `chengdu`, `guangzhou`, `taishan`, `gyeongju`) and 24 Regular Holy Sites (`kaifeng`, `huashan`, `songshan`, `hengshan_north`, `hengshan_south`, `mount_wutai`, `mount_emei`, `mount_jiuhua`, `mount_wudang`, `huangshan`, `putuoshan`, `nanjing`, `jingzhou`, `wuchang`, `nanchang`, `quanzhou`, `fuzhou`, `kunming`, `dunhuang`, `taipei`, `lhasa`, `mount_baekdu`, `gaegyeong`, `hanyang`).
- **Shared Korean Sacred Centers**:
  - Both East Asian faiths share `gyeongju` as an Eminent holy site and `mount_baekdu`, `gaegyeong`, and `hanyang` as Regular holy sites, creating an integral spiritual bridge between the mainland and archipelagos.
- **Interactive Blood Faith Adoption**:
  - Faith conversion is consolidated into a single decision (`become_blood_cultist_decision`) triggering an event (`bm_faith_conversion.0001`) with four options: Blóðtrú (`blodtru_faith`), Ketsudō (`ketsudo_faith`), Xuédào (`xuedao_faith`), and a back out option (preserving cooldown and resources). AI weighting automatically routes regional heritages to their thematic branch.
- **Ritual of Blood Initiation**:
  - Outsider initiation is consolidated into the internal crucible `bm_enhance_blood_ritual_decision` anchored at Reykjavik (`c_vestisland`) and Tsushima (`c_tsushima`).
  - Relaxed prerequisites on Ritual of Blood so that `theologian` or `lifestyle_scholar` (100+ XP) each qualify individually.

## Key Decisions

- **Decoupling Japan and China into Dedicated Faith Branches**:
  - Rather than combining all East Asian cultures into a single omnibus faith, `ketsudo_faith` represents the Japanese spiritual sphere, while `xuedao_faith` represents the Sinitic sphere, bridged via shared Korean sacred centers and the Tsushima initiation sanctuary.
- **Fourfold Regional Holy Site File Organization (`bm_europe_holy_sites.txt`, `bm_china_holy_sites.txt`, `bm_japan_holy_sites.txt`, `bm_korea_holy_sites.txt`)**:
  - Holy sites are cleanly partitioned across four regional files for maintainability and modularity:
    - Europe (`bm_europe_holy_sites.txt`): 54 sites across Iceland, Atlantic isles, and Europe.
    - China (`bm_china_holy_sites.txt`): 26 sites across the Central Plains, sacred peaks, and maritime hubs.
    - Japan (`bm_japan_holy_sites.txt`): 23 sites across Japanese calderas, seats, and regional centers.
    - Korea (`bm_korea_holy_sites.txt`): 4 sites (`gyeongju`, `mount_baekdu`, `gaegyeong`, `hanyang`) shared across East Asian faiths.
- **Japanese & Korean Sacred Geography**:
  - `ketsudo_faith` elevates `yamashiro` and `kamakura` to Eminent status, fills inland Alps and pilgrimage routes (`mount_koya`, `ise`, `naniwa`, `shinano`), and shares the Korean spiritual network (`gyeongju`, `mount_baekdu`, `gaegyeong`, `hanyang`).
- **Relaxed Theological Prerequisites**:
  - Unlocked `theologian` and `lifestyle_scholar` (100+ XP) as independent qualifying conditions rather than requiring both simultaneously.
