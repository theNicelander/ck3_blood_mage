# v1.20.40: Blood Runes & Galdrastafir Architecture

## Summary & Motivation
Transforms the blood rune system into a focused magical stave-carving tradition based on Icelandic Galdrastafir. Characters carve single-slot, sequential runes (Minor -> Major -> Superior) into flesh, pairing base vitality/lifeforce trackers with 1 of 5 distinct staves. Blood Knights can self-carve up to Major staves; Blood Mages reach Superior staves with game-changing capstone mechanics. Mages and Knights can also inscribe runes onto courtiers, family, and retainers to build a runic retinue while advancing the Benediction discipline.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **3-Tier Sequential Progression & Base Trackers**:
  - `minor_blood_rune_modifier`: 20% yearly Minor Lifeforce roll.
  - `major_blood_rune_modifier`: 20% Minor, 10% Major, 5% dual Lifeforce roll.
  - `superior_blood_rune_modifier`: 10% Minor, 20% Major, 10% dual Lifeforce roll.
- **5 Icelandic Galdrastafir**:
  - 15 distinct modifiers (`bm_rune_<stave>_minor/major/superior`) offering linear scaling across all 3 tiers.
  - 5 Superior Capstones active exclusively when holding `bm_rune_<stave>_superior`:
    1. **Vegvísir (The Wayfinder)**: `character_travel_speed_mult = 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.40`, `character_travel_safety = 10 / 20 / 30`, `movement_speed = 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.15`. Capstone: Halves `seek_power_decision` cooldowns (1 yr landed, 6 months wanderer/knight) and guarantees no negative hazard outcomes.
    2. **Ægishjálmur (Helm of Awe)**: `prowess = 3 / 6 / 9`, `advantage = 2 / 4 / 6`, `knight_effectiveness_mult = 0.10 / 0.20 / 0.30`. Capstone: On combat start / battle clash (`bm_duel_on_actions.txt`), applies `bm_aegishjalmur_terror_modifier` (`prowess = -5`, `health = -0.5`, 1 year) to opposing commander and knights.
    3. **Feingur (Awakened Seed)**: `fertility = 0.10 / 0.20 / 0.30`, `years_of_fertility = 5 / 10 / 20`, `positive_random_genetic_chance = 0.10 / 0.20 / 0.30`, `health = 0.20 / 0.40 / 0.60`, `epidemic_resistance = 5 / 10 / 20`. Capstone: Doubles childbirth XP (`+4` base, `+8` if inherited; `bloodline` for Mages, `vanguard` for Knights). Unlocks interaction `bm_feingur_bless_dynasty_interaction` applying permanent modifier `bm_feingur_blessed_blood_modifier`.
    4. **Nábrókarstafur (Flayed Tithe)**: `domain_tax_mult = 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.20`, `men_at_arms_maintenance = -0.05 / -0.10 / -0.15`, `general_opinion = -5 / -10 / -15`. Capstone: +20 Gold on Point of Interest visited during travel (`bm_travel_on_actions.txt`). Winning battles (`bm_on_combat_end_winner`) awards Gold equal to 10% of enemy fatal casualties (clamped 25 to 250 Gold).
    5. **Hulinhjálmur (Unseen Shroud)**: `owned_hostile_scheme_success_chance_add = 10 / 20 / 30`, `enemy_hostile_scheme_success_chance_add = -10 / -20 / -30`, `monthly_lifestyle_xp_gain_mult = 0.10 / 0.20 / 0.30`. Capstone: Guarantees 100% success on yearly attunement XP rolls (`bm_yearly_events.txt`). Executing a successful Murder scheme reaps 1 Major Lifeforce (`bm_scheme_on_actions.txt`).
- **Self-Inscription via Grimoire**:
  - Minor Rune: In Cast Minor Magic (`bm_cast_blood_magic_minor.001`), costs 0 Piety + 1 Minor Lifeforce, requires Devotion Rank 2 & 25 Track XP, awards +4 `ancient` XP.
  - Major Rune: In Cast Major Magic (`bm_cast_blood_magic_major.001`), costs 200 Piety + 1 Major Lifeforce, requires Devotion Rank 3 & 50 Track XP, awards +5 `ancient` XP.
  - Superior Rune: In Cast Superior Magic (`bm_cast_blood_magic_superior.001`), costs 400 Piety + 1 Superior Lifeforce, requires Blood Mage, Devotion Rank 4 & 100 Track XP, awards +10 `ancient` XP.
- **Inscribe Blood Rune on Other (`bm_inscribe_rune_other_interaction`)**:
  - Available to both Blood Mages and Blood Knights targeting courtiers, children, kin, spouses, knights, golems, or liege.
  - Uses identical Piety/Lifeforce costs and caster gates as self-inscription, but awards `benediction` XP (+4 / +5 / +10) instead of `ancient` XP.
- **Flay Blood Rune Agony Rite (`bm_blood_runes.002`)**:
  - Agony swap rite burning `ancient` XP (-5 / -10 / -15), Piety (250 / 500 / 1000), Lifeforce, and inflicting wounds/exhaustion to strip active stave and re-inscribe at current tier.
- **Story Panel Integration**:
  - Custom loc `BM_BLOOD_MAGE_STAVE_DISPLAY` displays the active stave's historical name and tier in the Blood Mage story panel.

## Key Decisions & Trade-Offs
- **5 Focused Staves**: Distills the rune system down to 5 archetypes (traveler, warrior, dynasty patriarch/matriarch, miser/plunderer, schemer) to ensure sharp thematic identity and zero bloat.
- **Sequential Upgrades**: Staves cannot be jumped (must progress Minor -> Major -> Superior). Swapping requires the flaying rite.
- **Zero Vanilla Overwrites**: Modular on-action hooks, scripted effects, and values preserve 100% compatibility.
