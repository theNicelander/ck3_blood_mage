# v1.20.40: Blood Runes & Galdrastafir Architecture

## Summary & Motivation
Transforms the blood rune system into a full-fledged magical stave-carving tradition based on Icelandic Galdrastafir. Characters carve single-slot, sequential runes (Minor -> Major -> Superior) into flesh, pairing base vitality/lifeforce trackers with 1 of 10 distinct staves. Blood Knights can self-carve up to Major staves; Blood Mages reach Superior staves with game-changing capstone mechanics. Mages and Knights can also inscribe runes onto courtiers, family, and retainers to build a runic retinue while advancing the Benediction discipline.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **3-Tier Sequential Progression & Base Trackers**:
  - `minor_blood_rune_modifier`: 20% yearly Minor Lifeforce roll.
  - `major_blood_rune_modifier`: 20% Minor, 10% Major, 5% dual Lifeforce roll.
  - `superior_blood_rune_modifier`: 10% Minor, 20% Major, 10% dual Lifeforce roll.
- **10 Icelandic Galdrastafir**:
  - 30 distinct modifiers (`bm_rune_<stave>_minor/major/superior`) offering linear scaling across all 3 tiers.
  - 10 Superior Capstones active exclusively when holding `bm_rune_<stave>_superior`:
    1. **Vegvísir (Storm-Walker)**: Halves `seek_power_decision` cooldown and eliminates negative hazard outcomes.
    2. **Hólastafur (Earth-Opener)**: Grants +10 gold upon visiting any Point of Interest during travel (`on_travel_plan_movement`).
    3. **Feingur (Awakened Seed)**: Raises single-parent trait inheritance from 25% to 50% on child birth; doubles childbirth bloodline XP to +4/+8.
    4. **Ægishjálmur (Terror of the Slain)**: Unlocks single-combat and battlefield Lifeforce harvesting for Blood Mages.
    5. **Gapaldur & Ginfaxi (Unbroken Heel)**: Adds +15 duel victory weight and consumes 1 Minor Lifeforce to downgrade fatal blows to `wounded_2`.
    6. **Nábrókarstafur (Flayed Tithe)**: Yields +25 Gold per victim executed via lifedrain (single interaction or mass lifedrain).
    7. **Hulinhjálmur & Lásabrjótur (Unseen Siphon)**: Erases discovery and opinion penalties on failed curse/courtier lifedrain; slashes trait drain cooldown from 60 to 15 days.
    8. **Brýnslustafur (Honed Mind)**: Raises attunement yearly bonus XP proc from 50% to 100% guaranteed.
    9. **Draumstafir (Lucid Pulse)**: Bearer loses -25 Stress whenever a yearly rune Lifeforce pulse triggers.
    10. **Drepsóttarstafur (Plague-Ward)**: Halves Piety costs of all `bm_heal_ailment` treatments.
- **Self-Inscription via Grimoire**:
  - Minor Rune: In Cast Minor Magic (`bm_cast_blood_magic_minor.001`), costs 0 Piety + 1 Minor Lifeforce, requires Devotion Rank 2 & 25 Track XP, awards +4 `ancient` XP.
  - Major Rune: In Cast Major Magic (`bm_cast_blood_magic_major.001`), costs 200 Piety + 1 Major Lifeforce, requires Devotion Rank 3 & 50 Track XP, awards +5 `ancient` XP.
  - Superior Rune: In Cast Superior Magic (`bm_cast_blood_magic_superior.001`), costs 400 Piety + 1 Superior Lifeforce, requires Blood Mage, Devotion Rank 4 & 100 Track XP, awards +10 `ancient` XP.
- **Inscribe Blood Rune on Other (`bm_inscribe_rune_other_interaction`)**:
  - Available to both Blood Mages and Blood Knights targeting courtiers, children, kin, spouses, knights, golems, or liege.
  - Uses identical Piety/Lifeforce costs and caster gates as self-inscription, but awards `benediction` XP (+4 / +5 / +10) instead of `ancient` XP.
- **Flay Blood Rune Agony Rite (`bm_blood_runes.002`)**:
  - Agony swap rite burning `ancient` XP (-5 / -10 / -15), Piety (250 / 500 / 1000), Lifeforce, and inflicting wounds/exhaustion to strip active stave and re-inscribe at current tier.
- **Story Panel Integration**:
  - Custom loc `BM_BLOOD_MAGE_STAVE_DISPLAY` displays the active stave's historical name and tier in the Blood Mage story panel.

## Key Decisions & Trade-Offs
- **Sequential Upgrades**: Staves cannot be jumped (must progress Minor -> Major -> Superior). Swapping requires the flaying rite.
- **Zero Vanilla Overwrites**: Modular on-action hooks, scripted effects, and values preserve 100% compatibility.
- **Interaction Cooldown Dynamic Workaround**: Dynamic cooldown script values aren't supported in CK3 `cooldown = { days = ... }`. Hulinhjálmur's cooldown reduction uses a 15-day baseline with a 60-day validation flag for non-superior casters.
