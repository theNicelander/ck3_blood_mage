# v1.21.4: Overhaul Blood Temple into a 4-tier duchy district with 6 sub-buildings
PR: #199

## Motivation
- In CK3 1.21, duchy capitals use multi-slot districts rather than standalone buildings; the minimal 1.21 wrapper around Blood Temples left `bm_blood_temple_district` with 0 sub-building slots and a 7-tier all-in-one primary building.
- Moving specialized bonuses onto dedicated Blood Mage sub-buildings matches vanilla 1.21's 4-tier duchy district structure and lets players specialize each duchy (4 slots across 6 options).

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Blood Temple Primary (`bm_blood_temple_01..04`)**
  - Rescaled from 7 tiers to 4 tiers (*Blood Altar → Blood Shrine → Blood Temple → Blood Acropolis*; removed `05..07`), costing `200 / 400 / 700 / 1100` gold over `1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3` years.
  - Grants `1 → 2 → 3 → 4` `building_slots_for_host_district`, core monthly Piety (`+0.5` to `+2.0`, `+5%` to `+20%`), duchy prosperity (`+0.05` to `+0.20`), control baseline (`+2` to `+8`), levy size (`+5%` to `+20%`), and `+2` fort level / `+5` defender advantage at Tier IV.
  - Tiers I–II remain open to all governments; Tiers III–IV require a feudalized holding and Devotion 2+ / 3+.
- **6 Specialized Blood Mage Sub-Buildings (`bm_duchy_district_buildings.txt`)**
  - Registered in `bm_blood_temple_district`, each with 4 tiers (`250 / 450 / 750 / 1100` gold; `365 / 545 / 730 / 910` days; `district_level_requirement = 2..4` on Tiers II–IV):
  - **Blood Sanctuary** (`bm_blood_sanctuary_01..04`): Health (`+0.5` to `+2.0`), Life Expectancy (`+4` to `+16`), Duchy Epidemic Resistance (`+5` to `+20`).
  - **Bloodline Vault** (`bm_bloodline_vault_01..04`): Positive Inactive Inheritance, Random Genetic, and Genetic Trait Strengthening Chance (`+4%` to `+16%` each), plus Inbreeding Chance (`-5%` to `-20%`).
  - **Blood Academy** (`bm_blood_academy_01..04`): Learning (`+1` to `+4`), Learning per Piety Level (`+1` at T3, `+2` at T4), General Lifestyle XP (`+4%` to `+16%` and `+2 / +4 / +7 / +10` flat monthly), Cultural Fascination (`+5%` to `+20%`).
  - **Hall of Blood Ancestors** (`bm_blood_ancestors_hall_01..04`): Dynasty Prestige (`+0.25` to `+1.0`, `+5%` to `+20%`), Legitimacy Gain (`+5%` to `+20%`).
  - **Blood Basin** (`bm_blood_basin_01..04`): Duchy Prosperity Growth (`+0.15` to `+0.75`, `+5%` to `+25%`), Control Baseline (`+3` to `+15`), Control Growth (`+0.15` to `+0.75`).
  - **Ritual Vaults** (`bm_ritual_vaults_01..04`): Monthly Piety (`+0.5` to `+2.0`, `+5%` to `+25%`), Piety per Knight (`+0.04` to `+0.18`), Dread Baseline (`+5` to `+20`).

### Other changes
- Added verified 1.21 modifiers (`monthly_learning_lifestyle_xp_gain_mult`, `epidemic_resistance`, `building_slots_for_host_district`) to modifier allowlists.
- Synced `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-blood-runes.md` and `RELEASE_NOTES.md`.
