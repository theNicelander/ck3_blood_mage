# v1.20.59: Duchy Blood Temples & Tribal Availability

## Summary & Motivation
Restore Blood Temples as modular Duchy Capital buildings (`common/buildings/bm_duchy_buildings.txt`, `type = duchy_capital`) rather than county regular buildings. Regular buildings in CK3 are hard-locked to `common/holdings/00_holdings.txt` and cannot be added mod-side without overwriting vanilla base game files (violating zero-overwrite modularity and clashing with overhaul/building mods like UBA).
Enable tribal rulers holding their duchy capital to construct Blood Temples up to Tier IV (`Blood Temple`), while reserving Tiers V–VII (`Great Blood Temple`, `Blood Pantheon`, `Blood Acropolis`) for feudalized realms with non-tribal governments.
Enforce the core mod principle of "Nothing hidden, but often unavailable" by moving trait and faith checks from `can_construct_potential` to `can_construct_showing_failures_only`, ensuring the Blood Temple line is always visible in the duchy capital slot and grayed out with clear failure tooltips when requirements are not yet met.
Fix dynamic trigger localization error `NOT_bm_can_drain_traits_from_target_trigger_tt` in `event_localization/bm_trait_drain_l_english.yml` identified during error log audit.
Rebalance Blood Berserkers Men-at-Arms (`bm_blood_berserkers`) recruitment and upkeep costs alongside damage and pursuit tuning to better align with elite heavy infantry benchmarks.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Duchy Capital Building Architecture (`common/buildings/bm_duchy_buildings.txt`, `localization/english/bm_buildings_l_english.yml`)**:
  - Moved from `common/buildings/bm_buildings.txt` to `common/buildings/bm_duchy_buildings.txt` with `type = duchy_capital` and `flag = duchy_building`.
  - Empty `can_construct_potential` so the building line is always visible to all rulers in their duchy capital slot.
  - Placed trait (`lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_blood_knight`) and faith (`blodtru_religion`) checks inside `can_construct_showing_failures_only` with `bm_requires_blood_mage_or_blodtru_tt` custom tooltip, showing the building grayed out instead of hidden.
  - Enabled status controlled by `is_enabled = { county.duchy.holder = county.holder }` and `show_disabled = yes`.
  - Retains all v1.20.58 bonuses: Piety (`+0.5` to `+4.0`), Piety Mult (`+5%` to `+40%`), Dynasty Prestige (`+0.1` to `+1.0`), Health (`+0.2` to `+1.4`), Life Expectancy (`+2` to `+14`), Development Growth (`+0.1` to `+0.8`), Development Factor (`+5%` to `+40%`), Control Growth (`+0.1` to `+0.8`), and capstones (Genetic inheritance boons on Tiers V–VII, prowess and fort/advantage bonuses on Tier VII).
- **Tribal Ruler & Holding Progression**:
  - Tiers I–IV (`Blood Altar`, `Blood Shrine`, `Blood Monument`, `Blood Temple`) are open to both tribal and non-tribal rulers. Tier IV serves as the Tribal Capstone.
  - Tiers V–VII (`Great Blood Temple`, `Blood Pantheon`, `Blood Acropolis`) check `custom_description = { text = bm_requires_feudalized_realm_tt building_requirement_tribal = no holding_is_feudalized = yes }`, showing grayed out for tribal rulers until they feudalize their realm.
- **Engine Error Log Fix (`localization/english/event_localization/bm_trait_drain_l_english.yml`)**:
  - Added missing negation trigger key `NOT_bm_can_drain_traits_from_target_trigger_tt:0 "[recipient.GetShortUINameNoTooltip] does #bold not#! have any drainable genetic traits"` resolving the dynamic trigger loc warning reported in `error.log`.
- **Blood Berserkers Men-at-Arms Balance (`common/men_at_arms_types/bm_men_at_arms_types.txt`)**:
  - Recruitment cost increased from `150` to `250` Piety.
  - Monthly maintenance adjusted to `0.50` Piety unraised (from `0.40`) and `1.50` Piety raised (from `1.35`).
  - Base combat stats tuned: Damage decreased from `68` to `60`, Pursuit adjusted from `35` to `20` (Toughness remains `20`, Siege remains `0.10`).

## Key Decisions
- **Duchy Capital Building over County Regular Building**: County regular buildings (`type = regular`) require entries in vanilla `common/holdings/00_holdings.txt`, which cannot be extended modularly without overwriting vanilla holdings and conflicting with total conversions or building mods. Duchy capital buildings (`type = duchy_capital`) are fully modular, dynamically queried by the engine, and present in all duchy capitals (including tribal capitals).
- **Failures-Only Preview over Potential Hiding**: In CK3, conditions in `can_construct_potential` hide buildings completely if unmet. Moving prerequisites to `can_construct_showing_failures_only` honors the mod's rule of "Nothing hidden, but often unavailable", allowing prospective blood mages to see the temple's existence, costs, and requirements.
- **Tier IV Tribal Capstone**: Tribal holdings and buildings in CK3 historically feature shorter upgrade lines. Allowing tribal rulers to reach Tier IV gives them substantial Piety, Health, and Development power while incentivizing realm feudalization to achieve the genetic and defensive wonders of Tiers V–VII.
