# v1.20.50.e: 3-Way Bloodline Blessing, Anoint Heir Succession Awakening & Blood Tithe

## Summary & Motivation
Expands blood magic dynastic governance and lineage mechanics:
1. Upgrades `bm_bless_bloodline_interaction` to offer a 3-way exclusive selection (`bless_sovereign`, `bless_iron`, `bless_crucible`) tailoring stat and lifestyle bonuses to the recipient's role in the realm.
2. Introduces `bm_anoint_heir_interaction` to consecrate an heir or child with Major Lifeforce, granting a permanent stat modifier and connecting directly to `bm_transfer_anointed_heir_legacy_effect` on ruler death. Upon death, the anointed heir awakens their predecessor's blood class trait (if not already initiated), inherits one stack of each held Lifeforce tier, and absorbs 25% of all active trait track XP across all tracks.
3. Introduces `bm_demand_blood_tithe_interaction`, empowering House Heads to levy lifeforce from adult house members.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **3-Way Bless Bloodline (`bm_bless_bloodline_interaction`):**
  - Requires 35 Piety + 1 Minor Lifeforce (`bloodline_magic_cast_effect_minor`), awards `+2` `bloodline` / `vanguard` XP and 25 positive opinion.
  - 3 exclusive send options (`starts_enabled` dynamically picks based on heir/ruler/knight status):
    - `bless_sovereign`: Grants `bm_blessed_blood_sovereign_modifier` (+2 Diplomacy, +2 Stewardship, +10 Vassal Opinion, -20% Short Reign Duration, +0.25 Health) for 10 years.
    - `bless_iron`: Grants `bm_blessed_blood_iron_modifier` (+2 Martial, +4 Prowess, +15% Knight Effectiveness, +0.50 Health) for 10 years.
    - `bless_crucible`: Grants `bm_blessed_blood_crucible_modifier` (+25% Fertility, +15% Positive Genetic Chance, -20% Stress Gain, +0.50 Health) for 10 years.
- **Anoint Heir in Blood (`bm_anoint_heir_interaction`):**
  - Cast on primary heir or child for 250 Piety + 1 Major Lifeforce (`blood_magic_used_major`).
  - Clears `bm_anointed_heir_modifier` from any previously anointed child/heir.
  - Grants permanent `bm_anointed_heir_modifier` (+0.50 Health, +15 Dynasty Opinion, +15% Monthly Lifestyle XP, -25% Short Reign Duration), +30 Opinion, and +5 `bloodline` / `vanguard` XP.
- **Succession Legacy Transfer (`bm_transfer_anointed_heir_legacy_effect`):**
  - Hooked into `on_death` / `on_character_death`.
  - Class trait awakening: If the heir lacks both `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight`, awakens them into the deceased ruler's class.
  - Lifeforce inheritance: Inherits 1 stack of each Lifeforce tier held by the deceased ruler (Minor, Major, Superior) with automatic stack recount.
  - XP inheritance: Inherits 25% of the deceased ruler's XP across all active tracks (`ancient`, `enlightenment`, `benediction`, `bloodline`/`vanguard`, `hematurgy`/`slaughter`, and all 5 `lifestyle_blood_power` tracks).
- **Demand Blood Tithe (`bm_demand_blood_tithe_interaction`):**
  - House Head Blood Mage or Blood Knight can demand lifeforce from adult living house members (50 Piety, 2-year cooldown).
  - Rewards caster with 1 Major Lifeforce (if target has Blood Mage/Knight) or 1 Minor Lifeforce (mundane kin), plus +2 `bloodline`/`vanguard` XP.
  - Recipient gets `recently_lifedrained_modifier` and 1-year `lifeforce_modifier_negative_minor`, plus -25 Opinion unless following a Blood Cult faith or `loyal`.

## Key Decisions
- **Exclusive send options over separate interactions:** Consolidated into `bm_bless_bloodline_interaction` using `send_options_exclusive = yes` to keep the character interaction menu concise and clean.
- **Permanent modifier with sibling cleanup for Anointed Heir:** Allowed anointing any child or primary heir, but strictly enforcing a single anointed heir per ruler by stripping the modifier from siblings when a new heir is anointed.
- **Inheritance of 25% track XP via dedicated script values:** Centralized calculation in `bm_heir_succession_values.txt` evaluating caller scope against `scope:dying_ruler` with integer rounding.
