# v1.20.69: Sequential 3-Tier Blood Magic Hierarchy

## Motivation

- Replace parallel/flat trait structure with a sequential 3-tier progression hierarchy (Blood Acolyte -> Blood Mage -> Blood Archmage) mapped directly to Minor, Major, and Superior Lifeforce fuels.
- Standardize all 3 tiers around the identical 6 core attribute tracks (Ancient, Bloodlust, Benediction, Bloodline, Hematurgy, Enlightenment).
- Enable blank-slate track resetting on ascension without power loss by baking permanent base stat floors and extracting unspent discipline XP into bonus Lifeforce stacks.

## Changes

### Key changes

- Sequential 3-Tier Hierarchy:
  - Tier I: Blood Acolyte (`lifestyle_bm_acolyte`) fueled by Minor Lifeforce, granting Minor Magic access and foundational Major rites.
  - Tier II: Blood Mage (`lifestyle_blood_mage`) fueled by Major Lifeforce, unlocking 4★ education, Rank 2–3 congenital traits, prisoner trait theft, and Tier II covenants. Base floor absorbs Tier I vitals (2.0 health, 33 life expectancy, 33 fertility, 33 plague resistance), 50 XP milestones (+1 skill per piety level for Diplomacy, Martial, Stewardship, Intrigue, Learning), and permanent age lock (`no_prowess_loss_from_age = yes`).
  - Tier III: Blood Archmage (`lifestyle_bm_archmage`) fueled by Superior Lifeforce, unlocking 5★ education, 2nd education, Rank 4–5 traits, Blood Golems, Galdrastafir, and Tier III covenants. Base floor absorbs Tier II vitals (5.0 health, 100 life expectancy, 50 fertility, 50 plague resistance), 50 XP milestones (+2 skills per piety level for Diplomacy, Martial, Stewardship, Intrigue, Learning), and permanent age lock.
  - Aligned the ancient track to the bottom across all three tiers so it is the 6th and final track (`bloodline`, `hematurgy`, `benediction`, `enlightenment`, `bloodlust`, `ancient`), and ordered base trait stats systematically by Ancient, Diplomacy, Martial, Stewardship, Intrigue, Learning.
- Blank-Slate Reset & Lifeforce Extraction:
  - Advancing tier swaps trait and resets all 6 tracks to 0 XP.
  - Unspent XP across the 5 non-ancient tracks converts at a rate of 20 XP = +1 Minor Lifeforce (Acolyte -> Mage) or +1 Major Lifeforce (Mage -> Archmage) via `bm_elevate_tier_effect`.
- Blood Distillation Rite (`bm_sanguine_distillation_decision`):
  - Converts non-ancient track XP into Ancient XP: Acolyte 10:5 ratio (10 track XP + 1 Minor LF + 50 Piety -> +5 Ancient XP); Mage 15:5 ratio (15 track XP + 1 Minor LF + 100 Piety -> +5 Ancient XP).
  - Ancient attunement option removed from `bm_attune_lifeforce_decision`, establishing distillation and rites as the primary active paths to 100 Ancient XP.
- Blood Mirror Ascension Event (`bm_ascension_duel.0001` - `0003`):
  - Gated at 100 Ancient XP, required Devotion rank (2+ for Mage, 4+ for Archmage), and Blood Rune rank (Minor for Mage, Major for Archmage).
  - Spawns exact clone of caster (`bm_crimson_reflection_template`) mirroring prowess for a non-lethal battlefield duel (`FATALITY = practice`, `LOCALE = battlefield`).
  - Victor elevates to the next tier and despawns reflection; defeat inflicts 1-year casting exhaustion, caps injury at `wounded_2`, deducts -10 Ancient XP (resetting to 90 XP), and prevents death.
- Martial & Legacy Unlocks:
  - Knighthood/commander gender exemption (`ignores_gender_army_rules = yes`) unlocks at Tier II+ or bloodlust >= 20 XP.
  - Fatal duel kills grant +5 Bloodlust XP and 1 Major Lifeforce (with 25% Superior chance vs high prowess / blood mages).
  - Blood Berserkers require Tier II+ or bloodlust >= 50 XP; Blood-Bound Behemoths require Archmage or (Tier II+ with bloodlust >= 80 XP).
  - Cull Champion interaction active at bloodlust >= 20 or hematurgy >= 20.
  - Heir succession and birth prevalence dynamically awaken or inherit Acolyte or Mage.

### Other changes

- Added trait icons: Bronze (`bm_blood_bronze_burnished.dds`), Silver (`bm_blood_mage_silver.dds`), and Archmage (`bm_blood_archmage.dds`, restored from the deprecated golden blood knight master asset).
- Standardized flavour text terminology: replaced all instances of "crimson" and "sanguine" with "blood" across decisions, events, traits, and modifiers, and codified the rule in the localization guide.
- Updated architecture documentation (`blood-mage-traits.md`, `blood-mage-magic-tiers.md`).
- Added full sibling localization keys for all 3 traits and their 6 tracks.
