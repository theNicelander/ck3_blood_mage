# Branch Context: v1.21.12

## Motivation

Add built-in, zero-submod trait draining compatibility for major CK3 total conversions (**A Game of Thrones**, **LotR: Realms in Exile**, and **Elder Kings 2**) so players can harvest signature overhaul traits and bloodlines without maintaining separate compatibility submods.

## Changes

- **Overhaul Detection & Trait Drain Router:**
  - `common/scripted_triggers/bm_triggers.txt`: Added `bm_is_agot_loaded_trigger`, `bm_is_lotr_loaded_trigger` (includes Day-1 `tedjin_reactionaries_triggered`), and `bm_is_ek2_loaded_trigger` (includes Day-1 `morrowind_high_councilor`), reused in `is_playing_overhaul_mod`.
  - `common/scripted_triggers/bm_trait_triggers.txt`: Split vanilla congenital checks into `bm_can_drain_vanilla_traits_from_target_trigger` and added `bm_compat_can_drain_traits_from_target_trigger` into `bm_can_drain_traits_from_target_trigger` (powers both `bm_blood_drain_interaction` and `bm_action_can_trait_drain_prisoner`).
  - `common/scripted_effects/bm_drain_trait_effects.txt`, `common/character_interactions/bm_blood_drain.txt`, `events/bm_trait_drain_events.txt`: Added `bm_compat_open_trait_drain_event_effect` router and hub option (`bm_trait_drain.001.compat_hub`); targets with only overhaul traits jump directly to the overhaul harvest event.
- **Isolated Overhaul Modules:**
  - **AGOT** (`bm_compat_agot_triggers.txt`, `bm_compat_agot_trait_drain_events.txt`, `bm_compat_agot_l_english.yml`): Drain `dragon_dreams`, `greensight`, and **Dragonblood** heritage (adds caster to AGOT's `historical_dragonseed` global list).
  - **LotR: Realms in Exile** (`bm_compat_lotr_triggers.txt`, `bm_compat_lotr_trait_drain_events.txt`, `bm_compat_lotr_l_english.yml`): Drain `blood_of_numenor` (tiers 1-10), `skinchanger`, `sorcerer` (tiers 1-3), and `twotreeslight`.
  - **Elder Kings 2** (`bm_compat_ek2_triggers.txt`, `bm_compat_ek2_trait_drain_events.txt`, `bm_compat_ek2_l_english.yml`): Drain `arcana_good` (tiers 1-3, purges `arcana_bad`), `magickal`, `lifespan` (tiers 1-4), `dragonborn`, `vampire_character`, `lycan_character`, and `necromancer` (guarded against EK2 opposite transformations).
- **Docs:** Updated `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-interactions.md`, `.agents/skills/debug-ck3-logs/SKILL.md`, and `RELEASE_NOTES.md`.
