# v1.21.12: Built-in AGOT, LotR, and Elder Kings 2 Trait Draining
PR: #207

## Motivation
- Overhaul players using **A Game of Thrones**, **LotR: Realms in Exile**, or **Elder Kings 2** could only drain vanilla congenital traits (`intellect_good`, `physique_good`, `beauty_good`, `fecund`, `giant`, `pure_blooded`).
  - Maintaining separate per-overhaul compatibility submods adds friction for both players and maintainers.
  - Isolating overhaul trait/trigger references into runtime-guarded `bm_compat_<overhaul>_*` modules keeps core files clean while enabling native trait harvesting out of the box.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Overhaul Detection & Trait Drain Router** (`bm_triggers.txt`, `bm_trait_triggers.txt`, `bm_blood_drain.txt`, `bm_trait_drain_events.txt`): Unified overhaul detection and trait-drain routing across vanilla and total conversions.
  - Added Day-1 global-variable detection triggers: `bm_is_agot_loaded_trigger` (`AGOT_is_loaded`), `bm_is_lotr_loaded_trigger` (`tedjin_reactionaries_triggered` / `sauron_destroyed`), and `bm_is_ek2_loaded_trigger` (`morrowind_high_councilor` / `almsivi_*_power`), reused in `is_playing_overhaul_mod`.
  - Wired `bm_compat_can_drain_traits_from_target_trigger` into `bm_can_drain_traits_from_target_trigger`, automatically enabling both **Drain Positive Traits** (`bm_blood_drain_interaction`) and the **Can Drain Prisoner Trait** Current Situation alert (`bm_action_can_trait_drain_prisoner`).
  - Prisoners with only overhaul traits jump directly to the overhaul harvest event; prisoners with both vanilla and overhaul traits gain a `"Harvest their otherworldly blood..."` hub option in `bm_trait_drain.001` with a `"Back"` return path.
- **AGOT Trait & Dragonblood Draining** (`bm_compat_agot_trait_drain.0001`): Isolated Westerosi blood and sight harvest (1 Major Lifeforce, Learning duel, 60d caster / 180d victim CD, sacrifices prisoner on success).
  - **Dragon Dreams** (`dragon_dreams`): 250 Piety, `-50` duel penalty, `+5` Cunning XP.
  - **Greensight** (`greensight`): 250 Piety, `-50` duel penalty, `+5` Cunning XP.
  - **Infuse Dragonblood**: 500 Piety, Devotion 2+, `-60` duel penalty, `+5` Cunning & `+5` Ancient XP; checks `agot_has_dragonblood_heritage` and adds caster to AGOT's `historical_dragonseed` global list for full, inheritable Dragonblood heritage.
- **LotR: Realms in Exile Trait Draining** (`bm_compat_lotr_trait_drain.0001`): Isolated Middle-earth blood and sorcery harvest.
  - **Blood of Númenor** (`blood_of_numenor`, tiers 1-10): `+1` rank per drain; scales 100 / 200 / 350 Piety and `-30` / `-60` / `-100` duel penalty at ranks 1-3 / 4-6 / 7+; `+3` Cunning & `+2` Ancient XP.
  - **Skinchanger** (`skinchanger`): 250 Piety, `-50` duel penalty, `+5` Cunning XP.
  - **Sorcerer** (`sorcerer`, tiers 1-3): `+1` rank per drain; 150 / 250 / 400 Piety, `-40` / `-70` duel penalty, `+4` Cunning & `+2` Wisdom XP.
  - **Light of the Two Trees** (`twotreeslight`): 750 Piety, Devotion 3+, `-100` duel penalty, `+5` Cunning & `+5` Ancient XP.
- **Elder Kings 2 Trait Draining** (`bm_compat_ek2_trait_drain.0001`): Isolated Tamrielic arcane, lifespan, and transformation harvest.
  - **Arcana** (`arcana_good`, tiers 1-3): 100 / 175 / 250 Piety, purges `arcana_bad` step-by-step before advancing `arcana_good`, `+3` Cunning XP.
  - **Magickal** (`magickal`): 150 Piety, `-30` duel penalty, blocked if `arcana_bad`, `+4` Cunning XP.
  - **Lifespan** (`lifespan`, tiers 1-4): `+1` rank per drain; 200 / 350 / 500 Piety (Devotion 2+ at rank 3+), `-40` / `-70` duel penalty, blocked if already immortal (`lifespan_6`, `lifespan_magic_6`, `vampire_character`, `lich_character`), `+4` Cunning & `+3` Ancient XP.
  - **Dragonborn** (`dragonborn`): 750 Piety, Devotion 3+, `-100` duel penalty, `+5` Cunning & `+5` Ancient XP.
  - **Vampirism** (`vampire_character`, 400 Piety, Devotion 2+, `-60`), **Lycanthropy** (`lycan_character`, 350 Piety, Devotion 2+, `-50`), **Necromancy** (`necromancer`, 200 Piety, `-40`), **Lichdom** (`lich_character`, 600 Piety, Devotion 3+, `-80`), **Briarheart** (`briarheart`, 400 Piety, Devotion 2+, `-60`), and **Hagraven** (`hagraven`, 450 Piety, Devotion 2+, `-60`): Guarded against EK2 opposite transformations.
  - **Tsaesci Blood** (`tsaesci`, tiers 1-4): 200 / 350 / 500 Piety (Devotion 2+ at rank 3+), `-40` / `-70` duel penalty, `+4` Cunning & `+3` Ancient XP.
  - **Hist Blessings** (`bm_compat_ek2_trait_drain.0002`): Paginated sub-menu for `argonian_behemoth`, `ghost_scale`, `amber_blessed`, and `sun_blessed` (tiers 1-3, 150 / 250 / 350 Piety, `+4` Cunning XP).
- **Vanilla Trait & Blood Memory Drain Gaps** (`bm_trait_drain.001`, `bm_memory_drain.0002`/`.0004`):
  - Added **Strong** (`strong`, removes `weak`) and **Shrewd** (`shrewd`, removes `dull`) to vanilla Trait Drain.
  - Added 4-track **Hastiluder** (`tourney_participant`: `foot`/`bow`/`horse`/`wit`) and **Varangian** (`varangian`) to vanilla Blood Memory Drain.
- **Overhaul & Workshop Blood Memory Drain** (`bm_compat_memory_triggers.txt`, `bm_compat_memory_drain_events.txt`):
  - Added `"Siphon Otherworldly & Modded Lore"` hub routing into isolated per-mod pickers for **AGOT** (`lifestyle_mariner`, `fire_obsessed`), **LotR** (12 lifestyle/commander traits including 4-track `lifestyle_artifact_crafting`), **EK2** (`battlemage`, `plunderer`, `crimeboss`), **Commander+ 1.21** (29 single-track commander traits), **Sub Rosa** (9 intrigue traits), and **Councillor's Experience** (5 councillor lifestyle traits).
