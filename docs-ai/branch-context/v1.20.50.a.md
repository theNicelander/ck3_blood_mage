# v1.20.50.a: Shared Target Trigger, Blood Knight Mentorship, Exsanguination & Vice Purging Rites

## Summary & Motivation
Establishes the foundational target evaluation trigger `bm_is_valid_positive_magic_target_trigger` and stress coping trait trigger `bm_has_stress_coping_trait_trigger` for the `v1.20.50` character interaction overhaul. Expands initiation and lifeforce granting interactions to support bidirectional Blood Knight mentorship, fixes stave stealth defense when suffering discovery penalties in hostile siphons, and introduces two new self and interpersonal rites: **Bleed the Own Vein** (self-exsanguination to condense Lifeforce) and **Purge Stress Vices** (burning away coping traits).

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Shared Positive Target & Coping Triggers (`common/scripted_triggers/bm_triggers.txt`)**:
  - `bm_is_valid_positive_magic_target_trigger`: Standardizes positive magic eligibility across courtiers, direct children, close/extended family, dynasty kin, spouses/consorts, lovers/soulmates, friends/best friends, knights, councillors, wards/guardians, golem house kin, and lieges.
  - `bm_has_stress_coping_trait_trigger`: Evaluates all 11 CK3 coping traits (`drunkard`, `hashishiyah`, `rakish`, `reclusive`, `irritable`, `flagellant`, `profligate`, `improvident`, `contrite`, `comfort_eater`, `inappetetic`).
- **Blood Rune Inscription Target Harmonization (`common/character_interactions/bm_inscribe_rune_other.txt`)**:
  - Replaced hardcoded relationship checks with `bm_is_valid_positive_magic_target_trigger`.
  - Wrapped `on_accept` outcome effects inside `send_interface_toast` with `bm_recount_lifeforce_stacks_effect = yes`.
- **Blood Knight Mentorship & Lifeforce Granting Parity (`common/character_interactions/bm_grant_lifeforce.txt`, `common/character_interactions/bm_become_a_blood_mage.txt`)**:
  - `grant_lifeforce_interaction` and `grant_lifeforce_interaction_reversed`: Now accessible to both Blood Mages and Blood Knights, targeting valid positive targets or prisoners, awarding shared `enlightenment` and `benediction` XP, with full toast wrapping and stack recount.
  - `ask_for_blood_magic_interaction` and `bm_get_blood_magic_from_prisoner_interaction`: Can now target either Blood Mages or Blood Knights, bestowing the matching class trait (`bm_become_blood_mage_effect` or `bm_become_blood_knight_effect`).
- **Stealth Siphon Stave Fix (`common/character_interactions/bm_curse_blood.txt`, `common/character_interactions/bm_drain_lifeforce.txt`)**:
  - Guarded `lifedrained_me` opinion penalties with `bm_has_superior_stave_trigger = { STAVE = hulinhjalmur }` so casters wielding Superior **Hulinhjálmur** remain completely concealed even upon failure.
- **Modifier Cleanup (`common/modifiers/bm_modifiers.txt`)**:
  - Removed deprecated `resilience_attuned` modifier.
- **New Self & Coping Rites (`common/character_interactions/bm_self_rites.txt`)**:
  - `bm_bleed_own_vein_interaction` (**Bleed the Own Vein**): Self-interaction on 1-year cooldown. Offers two modes:
    - *Minor Exsanguination:* Inflicts `wounded_1` (or upgrades to `wounded_2`) for 1 Minor Lifeforce and +2 Hematurgy (Mage) / +2 Slaughter (Knight) XP.
    - *Major Exsanguination:* Inflicts `wounded_2`, `lifedrained_modifier`, and +25 Stress for 1 Major Lifeforce and +4 Hematurgy (Mage) / +4 Slaughter (Knight) XP.
  - `bm_purge_coping_traits_interaction` (**Purge Stress Vices**): Usable on self or any valid positive target. Costs 250 Piety + 1 Major Lifeforce. Removes all negative coping vices, reduces Stress by -50, grants +25 opinion if cast on an ally, and awards +4 Enlightenment XP (self) or +4 Benediction XP (ally).

## Key Decisions
- **Shared Trigger Standard**: Centralized relationship checks into `bm_is_valid_positive_magic_target_trigger` to eliminate inconsistencies across character interactions and support the subsequent parallel streams.
- **Symmetric Mentorship**: Allowed uninitiated characters to learn from or steal blood magic from Blood Knights, granting `lifestyle_blood_knight` directly and eliminating the prior requirement of becoming a Blood Mage first.
- **Exsanguination as High-Risk Emergency Fuel**: Bleed Own Vein provides a self-sufficient Lifeforce generator without requiring victims or piety, balanced by severe wounds, temporary lifedrain, and stress.
