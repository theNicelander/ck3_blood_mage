# v1.20.44: Blood Runes Custom DDS Icons

## Summary & Motivation
Adds authentic, procedurally rendered 120x120 DDS icons for all 5 Galdrastafir staves (Vegvísir, Ægishjálmur, Feingur, Nábrókarstafur, Hulinhjálmur), replacing generic placeholder trait track icons in the Carve Galdrastafir decision widget and generic modifier icons on active stave modifiers.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Procedural Custom DDS Icons (`gfx/interface/icons/modifiers/bm_stave_<key>.dds`)**:
  - `bm_stave_vegvisir.dds`: Wayfinder eight-arm compass with distinctive terminal glyphs.
  - `bm_stave_aegishjalmur.dds`: Helm of Awe with 8 radiating tridents, concentric rings and crossbars.
  - `bm_stave_feingur.dds`: Awakened Seed with central seed diamond, sprouting shoots, branching roots, and lateral fertility nodules.
  - `bm_stave_nabrokarstafur.dds`: Flayed Tithe stave faithfully rendered to historical source (barbed tines, ring terminals, central eye arc, hourglass diamond, crossbar spikes, fanned triple-ring base).
  - `bm_stave_hulinhjalmur.dds`: Unseen Shroud with concentric rings, chamfered octagon, inner diamond, cross axis with ovals, 4 cardinal cone spikes, and 4 corner 8-spoke stars.
- **Modifier Definitions (`common/modifiers/bm_blood_runes_modifiers.txt`)**:
  - Pointed each stave modifier's `icon` property to its respective `bm_stave_<key>`.
- **Decision Option List Widget (`common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_carve_galdrastafir_decision.txt`)**:
  - Updated all 5 stave entries in the widget to display their custom 120x120 `.dds` icon instead of fallback trait track icons.

## Key Decisions
- **Mod-native Custom Icons over Vanilla Placeholders**: While vanilla has generic icon categories, none feature authentic Norse/Icelandic Galdrastafir sigils. Creating standalone 120x120 ARGB DDS icons ensures sharp visual presentation in both decision option lists and character modifier trays.
- **Reference Accuracy**: `nabrokarstafur` and `hulinhjalmur` match historical grimoire stave structures provided by the user.
