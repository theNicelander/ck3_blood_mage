# v1.21.3: Rebalance Icelandic traditions, add Drain Blood Memory, and step-down wound healing
PR: #198

## Motivation
- Icelandic traditions (`bm_tradition_althingi`, `bm_tradition_verbudir`) over-stacked bonuses, bypassed `Coastal Warriors` vs. `Seafaring`, and lacked replacement guards and matching ethos tooltips.
- Blood Mages lacked non-lethal siphoning of lifestyle/commander/martial fame traits, and `Cure Illness` could not step down `wounded_2` or `wounded_3`.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Drain Blood Memory (`bm_blood_drain`, `bm_memory_drain.*`)**
  - Non-lethal `drain_memory` on prisoners without `lifedrained_modifier`: siphons 9 lifestyle, 16 commander, or 4 martial fame traits, or `+20` XP on tracked traits `< 100` XP.
  - Costs Major Lifeforce, `150 + 50 × held` piety, `25 + 15 × held` stress (`60`d caster cooldown); Learning duel scaled by Blood Mage XP minus `12 × held`.
  - Success: `+3` Cunning XP, `lifedrained_modifier`, `-50` opinion (bypassed by Hulinhjálmur). Failure: major negative Lifeforce, `180`d victim cooldown. AI skips at `stress >= 150` or on spouses/consorts/children.
- **Step-down wound healing (`bm_cure_illness_interactions`, `bm_heal_ailment.0001`)**
  - Added `wounded_2` → `wounded_1` (Minor Lifeforce + minor piety) and `wounded_3` → `wounded_2` (Tier 2+, Major Lifeforce + major piety).
- **Icelandic traditions (`bm_tradition_althingi`, `bm_tradition_verbudir`)**
  - **Alþingi**: fascination `+15%`, removed flat county `prosperity_growth` (kept `+0.05` capital, `+20%` factor), below-baseline control `+0.20` (`+5` baseline), removed coastal check, added `tradition_things` guard.
  - **Verbúðir**: removed Seafaring port slot/MAA control and `port_district_stationed_maa_bonus` (kept tribal ports, early upgrades, `+1` trade goods), `low_sea_danger_reduction`, fish `+25%` production / `+3%` sell price, `tradition_maritime_mercantilism`/`tradition_fishermen` guards.

### Other changes
- Fixed incompatible-ethos cost tooltip keys on both traditions.
