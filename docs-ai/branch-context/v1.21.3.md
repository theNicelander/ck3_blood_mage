# v1.21.3: Rebalance Icelandic custom traditions
PR: #198

## Motivation
- Icelandic's two custom traditions (`bm_tradition_althingi` and `bm_tradition_verbudir`) stacked multiple vanilla traditions' worth of bonuses and bypassed vanilla's `Coastal Warriors` vs. `Seafaring` mutual-exclusion lock.
- Both traditions also had mismatched ethos penalty tooltips and missing replacement guards.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Alþingi Commonwealth (`bm_tradition_althingi`)**
  - Lowered `cultural_head_fascination_mult` to `+15%` and removed flat county `prosperity_growth` (kept `+0.05` capital prosperity growth and `+20%` `prosperity_growth_factor`).
  - Raised `monthly_control_change_when_below_baseline_add` from `+0.10` to `+0.20` alongside `+5` baseline control.
  - Removed the unintended coastal province requirement and added a `scope:replacing` guard for `tradition_things`.
- **Verbúðir (`bm_tradition_verbudir`)**
  - Removed Seafaring-exclusive port slot/MAA control parameters (`seafarers_port_building_slot`, `seafarers_port_maa_control`) and redundant `port_district_stationed_maa_bonus` (kept tribal port access, early port upgrades, and `+1` `max_trade_goods_on_route`).
  - Adjusted `coastal_sea_travel_danger` to `low_sea_danger_reduction` (matching vanilla `tradition_fishermen`) while keeping `sea_travel_danger = medium_sea_danger_reduction` and fish/port trade identity.
  - Added `scope:replacing` guards for `tradition_maritime_mercantilism` and `tradition_fishermen`.

### Other changes
- Fixed incompatible-ethos cost tooltip keys on both traditions to match their allowed ethoses.
