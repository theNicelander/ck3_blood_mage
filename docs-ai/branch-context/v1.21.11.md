# v1.21.11: Battlefield Lifeforce Harvest, Battle Fury XP & Duel XP Rebalance
PR: #206

## Motivation
- Battlefield Lifeforce harvesting and Battle Fury XP could still be farmed rapidly by chasing defeated armies across multiple back-to-back engagements, and duel Fury XP varied inconsistently across single combat sources (`+3` to `+5`).
  - Replaces step-based battle harvest with linear total-combatant chances gated by separate 90-day on-harvest cooldowns, adds a 60-day Battle Fury XP cooldown (victory-only), and standardizes duel and sparring Fury XP.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Battlefield Lifeforce Harvest (`bm_combat_values.txt`, `bm_battle_victory_lifeforce_harvest_effect`)**: Linear total-combatant scaling with separate 90-day on-harvest cooldowns
  - Scales from total combatants across both sides (`winning_side.side_max_army_size + enemy_side.side_max_army_size`) and removes the `num_enemies_killed` threshold bypass.
  - Rolls Minor, Major, and Superior Lifeforce independently so large clashes can yield multiple tiers when off cooldown:
    - Minor: `10%` per `1,000` total combatants (`500+` floor, capped at `100%`), triggers 90-day `bm_battle_lifeforce_minor_cooldown` on harvest.
    - Major: `1%` per `1,000` total combatants (`>= 5,000` total, capped at `100%`), triggers 90-day `bm_battle_lifeforce_major_cooldown` on harvest.
    - Superior: `1%` at `10,000` total combatants scaling linearly to `100%` at `110,000`, triggers 90-day `bm_battle_lifeforce_superior_cooldown` on harvest.
- **Battlefield Fury XP (`bm_combat_effects.txt`, `bm_combat_on_actions.txt`)**: 60-day cooldown, victory-only, simplified total-combatant formula
  - Battles with `< 500` total combatants and all battle defeats yield `0` XP (removed `on_combat_end_loser` / `bm_on_combat_end_loser` and loser reward effects).
  - Qualifying victories (`>= 500` total combatants) trigger a 60-day `bm_battle_fury_xp_cooldown` and grant `+1` base Fury XP, `+1` extra for commanders, and `+1` extra when total combatants exceed `10,000` (Commander: `+2` / `+3`; Knight: `+1` / `+2`).
- **Duel & Blood Sparring Ring Fury XP (`bm_duel_on_actions.txt`, `bm_prisoner_blood_duel_events.txt`, `bm_seek_power_events.txt`, `bm_ritual_spar_events.txt`)**: Standardized duel XP
  - Standard duels (lethal single combat kill, Prisoner Blood Pit Duel, Blood Hunt single combat) now grant `+2` Fury XP on victory and `+1` Fury XP on defeat.
  - Blood Sparring Ring now grants `+1` Fury XP on victory and `0` Fury XP on defeat.
