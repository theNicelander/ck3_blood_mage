# v1.20.56: Blóðtrú Blood Knight Virtue, Devotion Level Reduction Purge from Major Magic, Education Devotion Requirements & Blood Rune Flavour Text Standardization

## Summary & Motivation
Align Blóðtrú faith virtues to embrace both `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight`, purge devotion level reduction (`add_piety_level = -1`) from Major blood magic (Enhance Education: 1★ to 4★) so that piety level loss is strictly reserved for Superior magic (Master Education: 5-Star Ascension), enforce Devotion Level 4 requirement for Embrace New Education (`bm_superior_new_education` / `bm_education_new.001`) with clear grayed-out requirement preview, and standardize Blood Rune flavour text across all modifiers, decisions, interactions, story tooltips, and toasts to consistently describe passively harvesting Lifeforce from the world around you.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Blóðtrú Virtue Standardization (`common/religion/religion_types/bm_religion.txt`)**:
  - Added `lifestyle_blood_knight` as an explicit virtue alongside `lifestyle_blood_mage`, replacing `lifestyle_mystic` and `pilgrim`.
- **Devotion Level Loss Strictly Reserved for Superior Magic**:
  - `common/scripted_effects/bm_education_duel_effect.txt`: Removed `add_piety_level = -1` on success and critical failure from `education_duel_effect`. Advancing education from 1★ to 4★ consumes Major Lifeforce and scaled Piety with tiered Devotion requirements (1★: Devotion 1+, 2★: Devotion 2+, 3★: Devotion 3+), but never deducts a Devotion Level.
  - Retained `add_piety_level = -1` exclusively in `education_master_duel_effect` (Master Education: 5-Star Ascension, Superior Lifeforce ritual).
- **Embrace New Education Devotion Level 4 Requirement**:
  - `common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_cast_blood_magic.txt`: Reaffirmed Devotion Level 4 requirement (`piety_level >= 4`) on `bm_superior_new_education`.
  - `events/bm_education_new.txt`: Added `piety_level >= 4` requirement to each discipline option with `show_as_unavailable = { NOT = { has_trait = education_<trait> } }` so characters who lack Devotion Level 4 see the exact unmet requirement grayed out.
  - `localization/english/bm_cast_blood_magic_self_l_english.yml`: Fixed devotion rank label on `bm_new_education_devotion_req` to Holy Person (#V 4#!), and updated `bm_major_improve_education_tooltip` to reflect that Major enhancement does not cost a Devotion Level.
- **Blood Rune Flavour Text & Tooltip Standardization**:
  - `localization/english/bm_blood_rune_l_english.yml`: Standardized `minor_blood_rune_modifier_desc`, `major_blood_rune_modifier_desc`, `superior_blood_rune_modifier_desc`, `blood_runes_effects_list` (`.minor`, `.major`, `.superior`), `bm_blood_rune.001.desc`, `bm_blood_rune_yearly_event_popup.title`, and `bm_blood_rune_decision` (`_desc`, `_tooltip`) to consistently describe passively harvesting Lifeforce from the world around you.
  - `localization/english/bm_cast_blood_magic_self_l_english.yml`: Fixed erroneous "passive stat enhancements" and vague "occult bonuses" in `bm_minor_blood_rune_tooltip`, `bm_major_blood_rune_tooltip`, and `bm_superior_blood_rune_tooltip` to accurately state that runes passively harvest Minor/Major Lifeforce from the world around you.
  - `localization/english/bm_blood_runes_l_english.yml`: Updated `bm_inscribe_rune_other_interaction_desc` to state that targets will passively harvest Lifeforce from the world around them.
  - `localization/english/bm_blood_mage_story_l_english.yml`: Updated `bm_blood_mage_blood_rune_tt` in the Story panel to state that living markings allow you to passively harvest Lifeforce from the world around you each year.
- **Architecture Documentation**:
  - `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-duels-and-education.md`: Updated master duels table and rules reflecting that Devotion Level loss is strictly reserved for Superior Master Education.

## Key Decisions
- **Piety Level Loss Reserved for Superior Tier**: Major blood magic (Enhance Education 1★–4★) does not burn a Devotion Level; only apex Superior rituals (Master Education 4★->5★) require sacrificing a Level of Devotion.
- **Consistent Event Option Preview**: Added `show_as_unavailable` in `bm_education_new.001` so unreached Devotion requirements are cleanly displayed without revealing already possessed disciplines.
- **Unified Blood Rune Mechanics & Flavour Language**: Replaced legacy descriptions promising stat buffs with concise, consistent flavour text establishing that Blood Runes passively harvest Lifeforce from the world around you.
