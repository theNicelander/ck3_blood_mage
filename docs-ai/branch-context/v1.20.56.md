# v1.20.56: Blóðtrú Blood Knight Virtue, Devotion Level Reduction Purge from Major Magic, Education Devotion Requirements, Blood Rune Flavour Text Standardization, Blood Hunt Inverted Cooldowns, Multi-Skill Combinations & Duel Probability Rebalance

## Summary & Motivation
Align Blóðtrú faith virtues to embrace both `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight`, purge devotion level reduction (`add_piety_level = -1`) from Major blood magic (Enhance Education: 1★ to 4★) so that piety level loss is strictly reserved for Superior magic (Master Education: 5-Star Ascension), enforce Devotion Level 4 requirement for Embrace New Education (`bm_superior_new_education` / `bm_education_new.001`) with clear grayed-out requirement preview, standardize Blood Rune flavour text across all modifiers, decisions, interactions, story tooltips, and toasts to consistently describe passively harvesting Lifeforce from the world around you, invert Blood Hunt cooldowns (1 year landed, 3 years wandering; halved by Superior Vegvísir) to reflect landed Lifeforce scarcity, unify Blood Hunt encounters and options identically across Blood Mages and Blood Knights, make dispatcher encounters scale purely by Prowess, and recalibrate event duel challenges with multi-skill combinations to a fair 20 skill benchmark (~60% success at 20 skill, ~70% at 25, ~80% at 30, ~95% at 40+).

## Key Decisions
- **Piety Level Loss Reserved for Superior Tier**: Major blood magic (Enhance Education 1★–4★) does not burn a Devotion Level; only apex Superior rituals (Master Education 4★->5★) require sacrificing a Level of Devotion.
- **Consistent Event Option Preview**: Added `show_as_unavailable` in `bm_education_new.001` so unreached Devotion requirements are cleanly displayed without revealing already possessed disciplines.
- **Unified Blood Rune Mechanics & Flavour Language**: Replaced legacy descriptions promising stat buffs with concise, consistent flavour text establishing that Blood Runes passively harvest Lifeforce from the world around you.
- **Inverted Cooldowns for Landed Rulers**: Landed characters have scarcer piety and slower natural lifeforce generation, justifying a shorter 1-year cooldown on Blood Hunts, whereas wandering adventurers have ample piety and now take a 3-year cooldown.
- **Unified Road Encounter with Champion**: Both Blood Mages and Blood Knights face the traveler accompanied by an escort knight champion, opening martial duels, brawls, lifedraining, and recruitment to both classes.
- **Multi-Skill Challenges with Fair Benchmarks**: Standardized wilderness encounter challenges to a 20 skill benchmark with thematic combined skills, eliminating punishing 24–30 target values and establishing a reliable progression curve (~60% success at 20 skill, ~70% at 25, ~80% at 30, ~95% at 40+).
