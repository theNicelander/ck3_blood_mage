# v1.20.87: Anchor specialized Blood reservoirs 1:1 to Major Lifeforce and double their bonuses

## Motivation

- Channeling 1 Major Lifeforce into 3 specialized stacks while reclaiming 1 stack into 1 Minor Lifeforce turned Transmute Lifeforce into an unintended Major-to-Minor splitter and diluted the value of Major Lifeforce.
- Reclaiming stacks with Track XP allowed repeatable Channel/Reclaim loops to farm lifestyle track XP for Piety.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **1:1 Major Lifeforce Transmutation (`bm_transmute_lifeforce_decision`, `events/bm_transmute_lifeforce_events.txt`)**:
  - Removed Minor Lifeforce transmutations (`bm_transmute_lifeforce.0001.minor`, `bm_transmute_lifeforce.0003`) and removed `lifeforce_modifier_minor` from `bm_transmute_lifeforce_decision` validity.
  - Channeling (`bm_transmute_lifeforce.0002`) now converts **1 Major Lifeforce -> 1 stack** of Radiant, Furious, Fortunate, Cunning, Wise, or Ancient Blood for **50 Piety** (`+4` matching Track XP, or `+2` `ancient` XP for Ancient Blood).
  - Reclaiming (`bm_transmute_lifeforce.0004`) now converts **1 specialized stack -> 1 Major Lifeforce** for **50 Piety** with **no Track XP**.
- **2x Specialized Blood Reservoir Scaling (`common/modifiers/bm_lifeforce.txt`)**:
  - Doubled all per-stack bonuses on `lifeforce_modifier_radiant`, `lifeforce_modifier_furious`, `lifeforce_modifier_fortunate`, `lifeforce_modifier_cunning`, `lifeforce_modifier_wise`, and `lifeforce_modifier_ancient` (including `+0.2` monthly Piety and `+10` monthly Lifestyle XP per stack).
- **Blood Blessing Ancient Blood Cost (`common/character_interactions/bm_blood_blessing.txt`)**:
  - Updated the Piety cost for transferring `lifeforce_modifier_ancient` to a partner or kin from `25` to `lifedrain_piety_cost_major` (`50`).

### Other changes

- Synced `docs-ai/architecture/` (`blood-mage-lifeforce.md`, `blood-mage-decisions.md`, `blood-mage-events-overview.md`) and `RELEASE_NOTES.md`.
