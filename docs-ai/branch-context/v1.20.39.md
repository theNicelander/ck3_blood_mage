# v1.20.39: Blood Mage vs. Blood Knight Parallel Devotion Architecture

## Summary & Motivation
Elevate `lifestyle_blood_knight` from a stepping-stone into a permanent, Devotion-anchored endgame peer to `lifestyle_blood_mage` in CK3 1.20 (*Fire and Sword* vs *Shepherd of the Faithful*). Re-anchors combat scaling to Devotion levels, introduces apex Devotion expansion up to Level 8 (Godlike), unifies Superior Blood Magic access, cleanses track milestone clutter, and introduces symmetric initiation rites.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Permanent Peer Choice & Deprecation of Elevation**:
  - Removed `bm_elevate_to_blood_mage_decision`, `events/bm_blood_knight_elevation_events.txt`, and elevation conversion effects.
  - Blood Mage and Blood Knight are mutually exclusive permanent choices.
- **Apex Devotion Levels 6–8 (Divine, Demigod, Godlike)**:
  - Added `character_max_piety_level_add = 1` to the 100 XP capstones of all three shared tracks (`ancient`, `enlightenment`, `benediction`) on both `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight`.
- **Devotion Impact Multipliers (`piety_level_impact_mult`)**:
  - Added +10% per tier to blood runes in `common/modifiers/bm_blood_runes_modifiers.txt`: `0.10` (Minor), `0.20` (Major), `0.30` (Superior).
  - Added `piety_level_impact_mult = 0.05` (+5% each, stacking up to +25%) to the 100 XP capstones of all 5 `lifestyle_blood_empowerment` tracks (`dynasty`, `mastery`, `presence`, `prosperity`, `shadows`).
- **Devotion Rewiring for Blood Knight**:
  - Rewired `vanguard` and `slaughter` from prestige scaling to devotion scaling (`martial_per_piety_level`, `prowess_per_piety_level`).
  - Base traits: stripped stat penalties/bonuses, retaining clean `same_opinion = 10`.
  - Maintained `knight_limit = 1` per tier on `slaughter` and `vanguard`.
  - Skipped vitality baselines at 50 and 100 XP milestones across all tracks.
  - Added full age prowess lock (`no_prowess_loss_from_age = yes`) at 100 XP across all tracks on both Blood Mage and Blood Knight.
- **Symmetric Initiation Rites**:
  - Knights create Knights (`make_blood_knight_interaction`); Mages create Mages (`grant_blood_magic_interaction`).
  - Symmetric costs: Major Lifeforce + 100 Piety (+4 Vanguard/Bloodline XP) OR Minor Lifeforce + 250 Piety (+2 Vanguard/Bloodline XP).
  - Geyser duel (`bm_geyser_duel.0001`): both Learning (Mage) and Prowess (Knight) options require `piety_level >= 2` and cost 1 Devotion level (`add_piety_level = -1`).
- **Superior Blood Magic Parity**:
  - Unlocked `bm_cast_blood_magic_superior_decision`, `bm_manifest_superior_lifeforce_decision`, `blood_golem_creation_decision`, and deadly ailment healing for Blood Knights.
  - Duels in rites and manifestation use `skill = learning` for both classes.
  - Slaying foes with `prowess >= 35` or blood traits in single combat yields a 25% chance of Superior Lifeforce for Blood Knights.
- **Progress Tracking Parity**:
  - Updated `bm_blood_mage_total_trait_xp_value` to support Blood Knights across all 5 tracks.

## Key Decisions
- **No Overwrite & Modular CK3 1.20 Architecture**: Pure script and modular definitions without overriding vanilla files.
- **Zero Godmode / Balanced Scaling**: Apex Devotion tiers require mastering multiple capstones to 100 XP.
