# v1.20.28

## Summary & Motivation

Branch `v1.20.28` establishes the **Superior Blood Magic** tier by introducing the hub decision `bm_cast_blood_magic_superior_decision` and restructuring high-tier rituals around **Superior Lifeforce** (`lifeforce_modifier_superior`):
- Enforces the design principle of *earned power over godmode*: reality-altering and engine-limit-breaking magical feats demand distilled Superior Lifeforce rather than routine Major reserves.
- Separates education progression: routine upgrades (tiers 1★–4★) remain in Major Blood Magic, while elevating to legendary 5★ mastery and unlocking secondary education trees (New Education) require Superior Blood Magic.
- Bifurcates congenital manifestation: standard mortal perfection (ranks 1–3: Quick/Intelligent/Genius, Comely/Pretty/Beautiful, Hale/Robust/Herculean) stays in Major Blood Magic, while ascending beyond mortal limits into superhuman perfection (ranks 4–5) demands Superior Blood Magic.
- Relocates the Superior Blood Rune (`superior_blood_rune_modifier`) and integrates Blood Golem creation into the Superior Magic grimoire.

## Core Concepts & Changes

- **Cast Superior Blood Magic Hub Decision**:
  - `common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_cast_blood_magic_superior.txt`: `bm_cast_blood_magic_superior_decision` added under `bm_decision_group`. Requires `lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifeforce_modifier_superior`, and `piety_level >= 2`. Triggers selection event `bm_cast_blood_magic_superior.001`.
- **Superior Blood Magic Grimoire Event**:
  - `events/bm_cast_blood_magic_superior_events.txt`: `bm_cast_blood_magic_superior.001` offers:
    - *Master Education (5★ Ascension)*: Requires tier 4 education, scaled piety, and Superior Lifeforce. Triggers `bm_education_master.001`.
    - *Embrace New Education*: Unlocks secondary education trees. Requires 1,000 piety, at least one 5★ education, and Superior Lifeforce. Triggers `bm_education_new.001`.
    - *Inscribe Superior Blood Rune*: Consumes Superior Lifeforce and 400 piety to replace Major Blood Rune with Superior Blood Rune.
    - *Manifest Transcendent Perfection (Ranks 4–5)*: Triggers `bm_channel_manifest_traits.002` to elevate traits from rank 3 to 4 or 4 to 5.
    - *Forge Blood Golem*: Direct access to golem shaping (`blood_golem.001`), synced with `bm_blood_golem_cooldown`.
- **Education Enhancements Restructuring**:
  - `events/bm_cast_blood_magic_major_events.txt` & `events/bm_education_enhance.txt`: `bm_cast_blood_magic_major.001` now restricts `improve_education` to upgrading tiers 1★–3★ (capping at 4★).
  - `events/bm_education_master_events.txt`: Added namespace `bm_education_master` and `bm_education_master.001` handling 5★ duels.
  - `common/scripted_effects/bm_education_duel_effect.txt`: Added `education_master_duel_effect` with Learning/discipline duel, +10 enlightenment XP, and superior exhaustion backlash.
- **Congenital Manifestation Bifurcation**:
  - `events/bm_cast_blood_magic_major_events.txt` & `events/bm_channel_manifest_traits_events.txt`: `bm_channel_manifest_traits.001` capped at rank 3 (Genius / Herculean / Beautiful).
  - `events/bm_channel_manifest_traits_events.txt`: Added `bm_channel_manifest_traits.002` for ranks 4 and 5.
  - `common/scripted_effects/bm_manifest_traits_effects.txt`: Added `bm_manifest_superior_intellect_duel_effect`, `bm_manifest_superior_physique_duel_effect`, and `bm_manifest_superior_beauty_duel_effect` consuming Superior Lifeforce and applying superior exhaustion on failure.
- **Blood Golem Synchronization**:
  - `common/decisions/cast_magic/bm_create_blood_golem.txt`: Standalone decision synced with shared flag `bm_blood_golem_cooldown` (3 years).
- **Localization**:
  - `localization/english/bm_cast_blood_magic_self_l_english.yml` & `bm_channel_manifest_traits_l_english.yml` updated with all decision, event, toast, and tooltip keys.
- **Situation Tab Blood Rune Display**:
  - Added `BM_BLOOD_MAGE_BLOOD_RUNE_DISPLAY` to `common/customizable_localization/bm_blood_mage_story_custom_loc.txt`, dynamically querying active Blood Rune modifier (`superior_blood_rune_modifier`, `major_blood_rune_modifier`, `minor_blood_rune_modifier`, or fallback none).
  - Integrated into `BM_BLOOD_MAGE_INFORMATION_STRING` in `localization/english/bm_blood_mage_story_l_english.yml` alongside disciplines, Lifeforce stacks, and discipline attunement.

## Key Decisions

- **Preserving Pacing Across Trait Ranks**: Capping Major Magic at rank 3 congenital traits and rank 4 educations ensures players can cure defects and attain historical mortal excellence without gating basic progression behind Superior Lifeforce, while reserving the mythical superhuman heights strictly for the apex tier.
- **Shared Cooldowns**: Blood Golem creation from the grimoire and standalone decision share `bm_blood_golem_cooldown` to prevent circumventing limits.
- **Superior Backlash**: Failing a superior ritual inflicts `temporary_negative_lifeforce_effect_superior` rather than minor/major backlash to reflect the volatile danger of uncontained apex magic.
