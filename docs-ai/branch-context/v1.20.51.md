# v1.20.51: Empower Dynasty Bloodline Overhaul & Blood Knight Harmonization

## Summary & Motivation
Align Empower Dynasty Bloodline house covenant ranks with the core Lifeforce hierarchy (Minor, Major, Superior), display live house covenant tiers directly in the decision selection widget and hover tooltips, streamline covenant names to the concise `Covenant of <Concept>` format, and restore missing modifier icons in the House View.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Covenant Rank Alignment**:
  - Shifted rank hierarchy from Awakened $\rightarrow$ Ascendant $\rightarrow$ Sovereign to **Minor (Tier I)** $\rightarrow$ **Major (Tier II)** $\rightarrow$ **Superior (Tier III)**.
  - Matches the exact Lifeforce costs (Minor, Major, Superior) and eliminates terminology disconnect.
- **Dynamic UI Level Tags**:
  - Implemented customizable localization (`common/customizable_localization/bm_covenant_custom_loc.txt`) evaluated on `GetPlayer`.
  - Selection buttons in `bm_empower_bloodline_decision` now display live suffix tags:
    - `Covenant of Majesty [None]` (when unranked)
    - `Covenant of Majesty [Minor]` (when Tier I active)
    - `Covenant of Majesty [Major]` (when Tier II active)
  - Hover tooltips now prominently feature a dynamic header banner:
    - `Current House Rank: None | Next Upgrade: Minor (Tier I)`
    - `Current House Rank: Minor (Tier I) | Next Upgrade: Major (Tier II)`
    - `Current House Rank: Major (Tier II) | Next Upgrade: Superior (Tier III)`
- **Streamlined Covenant Names**:
  - Standardized all 7 covenants to `Covenant of <Noun>`:
    - Majesty (Diplomacy)
    - Warband (Martial)
    - Prosperity (Stewardship)
    - Shadow (Intrigue)
    - Insight (Learning)
    - Legacy (Bloodline/Health)
    - Mastery (Lifestyle/Languages)
- **Family & Kin Initiation for Blood Knights & Blood Mages (`common/character_interactions/bm_blood_knight_interactions.txt`, `common/character_interactions/bm_grant_blood_magic.txt`, `localization/english/bm_interactions_l_english.yml`)**:
  - Expanded `make_blood_knight_interaction`:
    - Loosened target requirement from sworn knights and courtiers (`is_knight_of = scope:actor`) to include spouses, consorts, children (any age), close family, extended family, dynasty kin, courtiers, and sworn knights.
    - Dropped `recipient_must_be_knight_tt` requirement, allowing non-knights (spouses, minor children, female kin) to be initiated as Blood Knights directly.
    - Added +5 Vanguard XP reward for Major initiation when recipient is a child or dynasty kin, mirroring Blood Mage's +5 Bloodline XP for kin.
    - Added AI priority weights: spouse (+100), children (+75), sworn knights (+50), high prowess (+30), councillors (+25), and dynasty kin (+25).
    - Generalized option descriptions in localization (`make_blood_knight_interaction.knight_minor_desc` & `.knight_major_desc`) to cleanly refer to `[recipient.GetShortUINameNoTooltip]`.
  - Harmonized `grant_blood_magic_interaction`:
    - Scoped `is_shown` to spouses, consorts, children, close family, extended family, dynasty kin, courtiers, sworn knights, and close relations (lovers, friends, councillors, liege).
  - Expanded `empower_blood_knight_interaction` & `empower_blood_mage_interaction`:
    - Removed courtier-only limitation from `empower_blood_knight_interaction`, allowing Blood Knights to empower spouses, children, family, dynasty kin, and sworn knights even if landed or outside the court.
    - Added consort, close family, and extended family checks to `empower_blood_mage_interaction`.

## Key Decisions
- **Suffix Button Tags**: Used `[None]`, `[Minor]`, `[Major]` suffix tags on decision buttons to ensure instant visibility at selection time while fitting within `decision_view_widget_option_list_generic.gui` layout constraints.
- **Dedicated Modifier DDS Assets**: Added separate modifier DDS icons for each covenant in `gfx/interface/icons/modifiers/` rather than generic skill shields to maintain visual continuity between the decision widget and the house view.
- **Family Initiation Parity**: Allowed Blood Knights to awaken their kin and spouses directly without requiring combat knight appointment, while providing full mechanical symmetry (+5 XP for kin, identical AI prioritisation, and cross-realm family empowerment) with Blood Mages.
