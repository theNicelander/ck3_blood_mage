# v1.21.10: Trait & Memory Drain, Blood Temple Costs, Burn Lifeforce, Sparring & Battle Fury Balance
PR: #205

## Motivation
- Tier 1 Acolytes were gated out of higher-rank and special congenital traits in The Essence Harvest, while Drain Blood Memory charged `150+50N` Piety even for trait XP drains.
  - Unlocks congenital trait draining for all Blood Mages and lowers Memory Drain costs (`100+50N` new traits, flat `100` Piety for trait XP).
- Custom Blood Temple buildings used flat gold costs (`200`-`1100`) higher than vanilla duchy sub-buildings, and Burn Lifeforce was duplicated across Cast Blood Magic widgets behind Devotion gates.
  - Aligns Blood Temple costs with vanilla `royal_forest_01`-`04` and consolidates Burn Lifeforce inside `Transmute Lifeforce` with Stress + exhaustion brakes.
- Friendly Blood Sparring Ring bouts could generate unwanted grudges/rivalries, and battle Fury XP / Lifeforce harvest scaled off combined army size (`num_total_troops`), maxing Fury too quickly from small skirmishes.
  - Auto-restores pre-spar relations on bout completion and switches battle rewards to enemy army size with a skirmish floor and `50+` Fury diminishing returns.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Trait Draining (`bm_trait_drain.001`, `bm_blood_drain_interaction`, `bm_action_can_trait_drain_prisoner`)**: Available to all Blood Mage tiers including Acolytes
  - Removed Tier 2+ gate on `fecund`, `giant`, and `pure_blooded` options in `bm_trait_drain.001` ("The Essence Harvest").
  - Simplified `bm_can_drain_leveled_trait_from_target_trigger` to allow any Blood Mage below rank 5 to drain leveled traits (`intellect_good`, `physique_good`, `beauty_good`) regardless of target rank or caster tier.
  - Removed redundant `bm_can_drain_traits_from_target_tier_valid_trigger` from `bm_blood_drain_interaction`, `bm_action_can_trait_drain_prisoner`, and its localization keys.
- **Drain Blood Memory (`bm_memory_drain_values.txt`, `bm_memory_drain_events.txt`, `bm_blood_drain_interaction`)**: Reduced Piety scaling
  - Lowered new-trait drain cost from `150+50N` to `100+50N` Piety.
  - Added flat `100` Piety cost (`bm_memory_drain_xp_piety_cost`) when draining trait XP for already-owned traits, with `bm_memory_drain_min_piety_cost` gating interaction affordability.
- **Blood Temple Building Costs (`bm_blood_temple_01`-`04`, 6 district sub-building lines)**: Matched to vanilla `royal_forest_01`-`04`
  - Replaced flat gold costs (`200/400/700/1100` primary, `250/450/750/1100` sub-buildings) with `cost_gold = expensive_building_tier_3_cost` / `4_cost` / `4_cost` / `5_cost` (`175.5` / `247.5` / `247.5` / `360` base gold).
  - Applies to `bm_blood_temple_01`-`04`, `bm_blood_sanctuary_01`-`04`, `bm_bloodline_vault_01`-`04`, `bm_blood_academy_01`-`04`, `bm_blood_ancestors_hall_01`-`04`, `bm_blood_basin_01`-`04`, and `bm_ritual_vaults_01`-`04`.
- **Burn Lifeforce moved to Transmute Lifeforce (`bm_transmute_lifeforce_decision`, `bm_transmute_lifeforce.0005`)**: Consolidated under Transmute with no cooldown and Stress cost
  - Removed `bm_minor/major/superior_burn_lifeforce` options and 180-day cooldown flags from `Cast Minor/Major/Superior Blood Magic`.
  - Added **Burn Lifeforce** option to `bm_transmute_lifeforce.0001` leading to `bm_transmute_lifeforce.0005` (Minor: `+50` spendable Piety / `+15` Stress; Major: `+150` spendable Piety / `+35` Stress; Superior: `+300` spendable Piety / `+60` Stress; standard tier exhaustion; `add_piety_no_experience`, no Devotion or track XP; no cooldown; `ai_chance = 0`).
  - Expanded `bm_transmute_lifeforce_decision` validity to include Minor and Superior Lifeforce, with an AI guard so AI only opens the decision when holding Major Lifeforce or a specialized reservoir.
- **Blood Sparring Ring (`bm_ritual_spar_events.txt`, `bm_duel_effects.txt`)**: Prevented accidental post-sparring grudges and rivalries
  - Added `bm_spar_snapshot_relations_effect`, `bm_spar_restore_relations_effect`, and `bm_spar_clear_relation_flags_effect` to snapshot pre-bout relations and strip any new grudges/rivalries spawned by the single combat engine.
- **Battle Fury XP & Lifeforce Harvest (`bm_combat_effects.txt`)**: Gated by enemy army size with skirmish floor and `50+` Fury diminishing returns
  - Switched `bm_combat_winner_commander_reward_effect`, `bm_combat_winner_knight_reward_effect`, and `bm_battle_victory_lifeforce_harvest_effect` from `combat.num_total_troops` to `enemy_side.side_max_army_size`, with a `< 500` enemy troop floor (`0` XP and no Lifeforce harvest).
  - Rebalanced commander (`+1`/`+2`/`+3` below 50 Fury; `0`/`+1`/`+2` at 50+ Fury) and knight (`+1`/`+1`/`+2` below 50 Fury; `0`/`+1`/`+1` at 50+ Fury) XP across `500` / `2,500` / `7,500` enemy troop tiers.
  - Required both sides `>= 1,000` troops (`>= 2,500` at 50+ Fury) for defeat XP (`+1` Fury, `+1` Wisdom).
