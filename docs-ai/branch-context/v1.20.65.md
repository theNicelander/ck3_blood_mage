# v1.20.65: Icelandic Culture Polish & Blood Knight Combatant Exemption

## Motivation

- Under vanilla faith and cultural gender laws, characters initiated as Blood Knights (such as women under patriarchal faiths) could not serve as knights or commanders, directly conflicting with their identity as martial blood champions.
- The initial name list for `bm_icelandic` contained residual vanilla Old Norse/Norwegian nominative forms rather than authentic Icelandic orthography with epenthetic `-ur` and accented vowels.
- Unrequested vanilla baseline names diluted the flavor and thematic identity of the curated Icelandic saga, mythological, and clan roster.

## Changes

### Key changes

- Grant vanilla character modifier `ignores_gender_army_rules` ("Worthy Commander") automatically upon becoming a Blood Knight (`bm_become_blood_knight_effect`, `bm_ensure_blood_mage_story_effect`), qualifying Blood Knights to serve as knights and commanders across all cultures and faiths without vanilla overrides.
  - Automatically remove `ignores_gender_army_rules` if `lifestyle_blood_knight` is lost (unless the character is a vanilla `shieldmaiden`).
  - Update `make_blood_knight_interaction` descriptions and send option tooltips to clarify that initiates can serve as knights and commanders regardless of gender norms.
- Change `bm_icelandic` ethos from `ethos_bellicose` to `ethos_communal`.
- Replace `tradition_things` (Ting-Meet) in `bm_icelandic` with custom cultural tradition `bm_tradition_althingi` (Alþingi Commonwealth).
- Implement `bm_tradition_althingi` (`common/culture/traditions/bm_althingi.txt`):
  - Category: `realm`; layers: `0 = diplo`, `1 = western`, `4 = council.dds`.
  - Succession & duels: Unlocks Scandinavian Elective (`scandinavian_elective_enabled = yes`) and Hólmganga (`has_access_to_trials_by_combat = yes`, `trial_by_combat_enabled = yes`).
  - Tradeport harbor integration: Hooks natively into Tradeport buildings 1–8 via `trade_ports_give_control_growth = yes` (monthly control growth) and `trade_ports_give_levies = yes` (levy size), plus `trade_ports_give_fascination = yes` (+2% Cultural Fascination progress per level) and consolidated tooltip bullet `bm_althingi_trade_ports = yes`.
  - Realm ruler & county modifiers: Capital development growth (+0.10/mo), Cultural Head fascination (+25%), culture county development growth (+0.05/mo).
  - Coastal province modifiers: +25% coastal development growth factor, -10% holding/building gold cost, and -10% holding/building build speed across coastal baronies.
  - Sibling English localization in `bm_althingi_tradition_l_english.yml`.
- Replace `tradition_fishermen` in `bm_icelandic` with `tradition_fp3_beacon_of_learning` (Beacon of Learning, requires `legacy_of_persia` DLC flag, with `tradition_philosopher_culture` fallback).
- Standardize all masculine and feminine names in `bm_name_list_icelandic` to authentic Icelandic forms with epenthetic `-ur` endings (e.g., `Gunnhildur`, `Einar`, `Grímur`, `Þorbrandur`, `Kveldúlfur`, `Skallagrímur`, `Ingólfur`, `Hörður`, `Haukur`, `Sigurður`).
- Purge all unrequested baseline Old Norse names, retaining strictly the curated 124 masculine and 73 feminine names alongside 31 historical Sturlungaöld, mythological, saga, and Tolkien-adapted clan dynasties.
- Provide comprehensive UTF-8 BOM localization mappings in `bm_icelandic_l_english.yml` for all name tokens and orthographic variants with proper Icelandic diacritics (á, é, í, ó, ú, ý, þ, æ, ö, ð).

### Other changes

- Synchronize `blood-mage-culture.md` and `blood-mage-blood-knight.md` architecture specifications with the changes.
