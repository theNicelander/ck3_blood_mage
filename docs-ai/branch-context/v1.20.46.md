## Summary & Motivation
Synthesizes all changes across 45+ version branches since the June 2025 baseline release (v1.15, git hash `0a94aa6372f037c9ea3729c2d7e93f729aea9d40`) into a public-facing, living release notes document (`RELEASE_NOTES.md`) structured specifically for players and Steam Workshop release announcements. ## Key Decisions
- **Grimoire Consolidation over Standalone Decision**: Housing Blood Empowerment in the Cast Major Blood Magic Grimoire preserves menu consolidation alongside Enhance Education, Major Blood Runes, Bloodline, and Manifest Traits, avoiding unnecessary clutter in the global Decisions tab.
- **Deferred Cost & Zero-Cost Backout**: Costs (150 Piety + Major Lifeforce) are charged strictly upon discipline selection within `bm_blood_empowerment_event.001`, matching the UX pattern of other Grimoire multi-choice sub-events (`bm_education_enhancement.001`).
- **Net Diff Presentation**: Intermediate development steps (e.g., Crimson Warrior interaction → Crimson Knight trait → stepping stone to Blood Mage) are collapsed forward into their net final state (Blood Knight as a permanent Devotion-anchored martial peer to Blood Mage).
- **Public-Facing vs. Developer Documentation**: Architecture documents in `docs-ai/architecture/` remain the source of high-density technical specifications (variable names, script values, engine mechanics), while `RELEASE_NOTES.md` serves players and Steam Workshop subscribers.
