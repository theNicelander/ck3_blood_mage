# v1.20.31 - Bloodline Expansion, Track XP Realignment & Trait Stat Parity

## Summary & Motivation
Resolves pacing bottlenecks and role dissonance across `lifestyle_blood_mage` and `lifestyle_blood_knight`. Expands the previously starved Bloodline track with active family milestones, kin blessings, and genetic manifestations. Realigns Blood Runes and Manifest Lifeforce to the Ancient school, makes bestowing Blood Magic a dynastic elevation, restricts battlefield lifeforce harvesting strictly to Blood Knights, and standardizes identical health and lifespan scaling per 10 XP tier across both traits.

## Core Concepts & Changes
- **Bloodline Progression Expansion**:
  - `grant_blood_magic_interaction`: Realigned from Benediction to **Bloodline** track XP (+4 base; +5 if granted to child or dynasty kin).
  - Congenital Trait Manifestation (`bm_manifest_traits`): Moved from Enlightenment to **Bloodline** track XP (+4 XP for ranks 1–3; +6 Bloodline XP & +2 Enlightenment XP for ranks 4–5).
  - Childbirth Milestones (`on_birth_child` -> `bm_on_birth_bloodline_xp`): Welcoming a child as a Blood Mage parent awards **+2 Bloodline XP** (+4 total if the child inherits Blood Magic).
  - New Targeted Interaction (`bm_bless_bloodline_interaction`): Spend Minor Lifeforce + 35 Piety on a child or dynasty kin to grant a 10-year blessing buff (+1 all stats, +10% fertility, +0.25 health) and **+2 Bloodline XP**.
  - Minor Rite: Commune with Bloodline (`bm_cast_blood_magic_minor.001`): Added minor spell costing Minor Lifeforce + 50 Piety for 5-year dynastic resonance buff and **+2 Bloodline XP**.
  - Purge Impurities (`bm_cast_blood_magic_minor.001`): Cleansing bad congenital traits (`ugly`, `dull`, `weak`) now awards **+2 Bloodline XP**.
  - Yearly Dynasty Modifiers Pulse (`blood_mage_yearly_events.002`): Boosted roll chance from 5% to **10% per active house modifier** (up to 70% cap).
- **Ancient Track Realignment**:
  - Blood Runes: Minor (+4 XP), Major (+5 XP), and Superior (+10 XP) runes realigned from Benediction to **Ancient** track XP.
  - Manifest Lifeforce (`bm_manifest_lifeforce_decision`): Realigned from Enlightenment to **Ancient** track XP (+3 XP) for Blood Mages.
  - Manifest Superior Lifeforce (`bm_manifest_superior_lifeforce_decision`): Realigned to **Ancient** track XP (+8 crit / +5 success / +2 fail).
- **Blood Knight Identity & Martial Realignment**:
  - Stat Parity: Standardized baseline health and lifespan bonuses across all tiers in `lifestyle_blood_knight` to identically match `lifestyle_blood_mage`: `+0.1` Health, `+2` Life Expectancy, `+1` Year Fertility, and `+1` Epidemic Resistance per 10 XP tier.
  - Manifest Lifeforce (`bm_manifest_lifeforce_decision`): Blood Knights now test **Prowess** (instead of Learning), earning **+3 Resilience XP** and generating Minor Lifeforce.
  - Battlefield Lifeforce Exclusivity: Removed battlefield duel kill lifeforce for Blood Mages; combat lifeforce harvesting is now exclusive to Blood Knights (33% battle win roll, single combat fatal kills).
  - Martial Healing (`heal_disease_minor`): Self-healing grants **+2 Resilience XP**; healing fellow knights, comrades, or liege grants **+1 Vanguard XP & +1 Benediction XP**.
- **Documentation & Verification**:
  - Updated all matching architecture documents in `docs-ai/architecture/`.
  - All 148 checked files verified via `python3 scripts/check_repo.py --fix`.
