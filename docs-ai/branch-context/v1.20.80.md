# v1.20.80: Reformed Mainline Blood Faiths & Old Unreformed Variants

## Motivation

- Core Blood Mage faiths (`blodtru_faith`, `ketsudo_faith`, `xuedao_faith`) started as unreformed pagan faiths by default, imposing a `-20` Feudal/Clan vassal opinion penalty, blocking Holy Wars, and leaving Feudal European/Asian blood rulers vulnerable to rapid proselytization by organized world religions.
- Players wishing to play tribal raiders (such as 867 Norse Vikings) who rely on pagan homeland defensive attrition and manual reformation progression lacked an explicit unreformed option once mainline faiths are organized.

## Changes

### Key changes

- **Reformed Mainline Blood Faiths**:
  - `blodtru_faith`, `ketsudo_faith`, and `xuedao_faith` are now Organized/Reformed by default at game start, removing `unreformed_faith_doctrine` across faith and rite definitions.
  - Standardized default faith icons to reformed iconography (`blodtru_faith_reformed` and `ketsudo_faith_reformed`).
  - Standardized all faith and rite map colors to pitch black (`color = { 0.0 0.0 0.0 }`).
  - Feudal and Clan blood rulers immediately gain Holy War casus belli and full conversion resistance without requiring prior holy site conquest or piety grinding.
- **Old Unreformed Variant Faiths**:
  - Introduced `old_blodtru_faith` ("Old Blóðtrú"), `old_ketsudo_faith` ("Old Ketsudō"), and `old_xuedao_faith` ("Old Xuédào") under `blodtru_religion`.
  - Configured each Old variant with `unreformed_faith_doctrine`, 1:1 mainline rites in `bm_rite_types.txt`, pitch black map color (`color = { 0.0 0.0 0.0 }`), base unreformed icons, and shared regional eminent holy sites.
  - Available for selection in Ruler Designer / Character Creation and console.

- **Conversion Decision Sub-Event (`bm_faith_conversion.0001` / `.0002`)**:
  - The decision to become a blood cultist defaults to offering the reformed cultural blood faiths.
  - Added a dedicated path in `bm_faith_conversion.0001` leading to sub-event `bm_faith_conversion.0002` allowing characters to consciously embrace Old Blóðtrú, Old Ketsudō, or Old Xuédào.

- **Dedicated Transmute Lifeforce Decision (`bm_transmute_lifeforce_decision`)**:
  - Consolidated all 13 Lifeforce-to-Lifeforce conversions into a dedicated inline option-list widget decision (`decision_view_widget_option_list_generic` with `show_from_start = yes`).
  - Houses all reservoir channelings (`bm_minor_channel_mojo`, `bm_minor_channel_fury`, `bm_minor_channel_focus`, `bm_major_channel_mojo`, `bm_major_channel_fury`, `bm_major_channel_focus`), reservoir reclaims (`bm_minor_reclaim_mojo`, `bm_minor_reclaim_fury`, `bm_minor_reclaim_focus`), condensation rites (`bm_minor_condense`, `bm_major_condense`), and splitting rites (`bm_major_split`, `bm_superior_split`).
  - Integrated into `common/story_cycles/bm_blood_mage_story.txt` for direct display and access in the Blood Mage Situation tab.
  - Decluttered `bm_cast_blood_magic_minor_decision`, `bm_cast_blood_magic_major_decision`, and `bm_cast_blood_magic_superior_decision`, restoring them to pure spellcasting grimoires (channeling XP, trait purges, education advancements, golem creation, and blood runes).

- Synchronized `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-decisions.md`, `blood-mage-lifeforce.md`, `blood-mage-magic-tiers.md`, and `blood-mage-story.md` with the Transmute Lifeforce decision architecture.
