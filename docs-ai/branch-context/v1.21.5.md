# v1.21.5: Consolidate Blood Magic character interactions & remove Anoint Heir

## Motivation
- Reduce right-click menu clutter in the Blood Magic interaction category by folding standalone curse and duplicate blessing interactions into their unified modal counterparts.
- Remove Anoint Heir completely from the mod.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Blood Drain (`bm_blood_drain_interaction`)**: Added **Cast Blood Curse** (`drain_curse`) as an exclusive radio option (50 Piety, Minor Lifeforce, 10+ Cunning XP, 1-year `bm_curse_blood_cooldown`), preserving its Intrigue + Learning duel and AI scoring/vetoes, and removed standalone `bm_curse_blood_interaction`. Reworked **Demand Blood Tithe** (`drain_tithe`) to guarantee success without a duel (wounds target + `-30` opinion instead of `recently_lifedrained_modifier`/`lifeforce_modifier_negative_minor`) and expanded **Minor Life drain** (`drain_minor`, `20/40/40`) and **Empowered Minor Life drain** (`drain_minor_empowered`, `40/40/20`) non-prisoner duels to 3 outcomes (Secret Success with no opinion penalty, Success with `-30` opinion + wounded, Fail with `-30` opinion + `+20` Stress).
- **Cast Blood Blessing (`bm_cast_blood_blessing_interaction`)**: Unified **Blood Blessing** (`Empower Blood Mage`, `Grant Minor/Major/Superior Lifeforce`, `Grant Ancient Blood`, `Inscribe Blood Rune` for non-self targets) into `bm_cast_blood_blessing_interaction` alongside the 6 tiered permanent Blood Blessings, scoped the 1-year `bm_blessing_cooldown` to the 6 tiered blessings only, and removed duplicate `bm_blood_blessing_interaction`.
- **Grant Blood Magic (`grant_blood_magic_interaction` / `ask_for_blood_magic_interaction`)**: Initiating someone as a Blood Mage now also grants a permanent `+30` `made_me_a_blood_mage` opinion bonus.
- **Removed Anoint Heir**: Deleted `blessing_anoint_heir`, `bm_anointed_heir_modifier`, `bm_transfer_anointed_heir_legacy_effect`, `bm_on_character_death_dynasty`, and all related script values, icons, and localization.

### Other changes
- Updated `bm_adopt_lowborn_interaction` icon to use the Blood Mage interaction icon (`bm_adopt_lowborn_interaction.dds`) and updated `bm_blood_distillation_interaction` icon reference to `bm_cast_blood_blessing_interaction`.
- Updated `AGENTS.md` to require branch re-check before commit/push, and synced `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-interactions.md`, `docs-ai/architecture/blood-mage-dynasty.md`, and `RELEASE_NOTES.md`.
