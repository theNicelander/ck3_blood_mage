# v1.21.5: Consolidate Blood Magic character interactions & remove Anoint Heir
PR: #200

## Motivation
- Reduce right-click menu clutter in the Blood Magic interaction category by folding standalone curse and duplicate blessing interactions into their unified modal counterparts.
- Remove Anoint Heir completely from the mod.

## Changes
### Key changes
- **Blood Drain (`bm_blood_drain_interaction`)**: Consolidated curse and expanded drain options.
  - **Cast Blood Curse (`drain_curse`)**: Moved in as an exclusive radio option (50 Piety, Minor Lifeforce, 10+ Cunning XP, 1-year `bm_curse_blood_cooldown`), preserving its Intrigue + Learning duel and AI scoring/vetoes; removed standalone `bm_curse_blood_interaction`.
  - **Major Life drain (Overdrained) (`drain_major_overdrained`)**: Added player-only emergency prisoner harvest (bottom option, 0 Piety) that executes the target for Major Lifeforce + `+3` Cunning XP at the cost of a stackable 2-year `bm_overdrained_modifier` (`-0.3` Health, `-1.0` Monthly Piety, `-5%` Monthly Piety), `+20` Stress, and non-escalating `wounded_1` if uninjured.
  - **Demand Blood Tithe (`drain_tithe`)**: Reworked to guarantee success without a duel (wounds target + `-30` opinion instead of `recently_lifedrained_modifier`/`lifeforce_modifier_negative_minor`).
  - **Minor Life drain (`drain_minor`, `20/40/40`) & Empowered (`drain_minor_empowered`, `40/40/20`)**: Expanded non-prisoner duels to 3 outcomes: Secret Success (no opinion penalty), Success (`-30` opinion + wounded), and Fail (`-30` opinion + `+20` Stress).
- **Cast Blood Blessing (`bm_cast_blood_blessing_interaction`)**: Unified all non-self blessing interactions.
  - Folded `Empower Blood Mage`, `Grant Minor/Major/Superior Lifeforce`, `Grant Ancient Blood`, and `Inscribe Blood Rune` (non-self) alongside the 6 tiered permanent Blood Blessings.
  - Scoped the 1-year `bm_blessing_cooldown` to the 6 tiered blessings only and removed duplicate `bm_blood_blessing_interaction`.
- **Grant Blood Magic (`grant_blood_magic_interaction` / `ask_for_blood_magic_interaction`)**:
  - Initiating someone as a Blood Mage now grants only the permanent `+30` `made_me_a_blood_mage` opinion bonus (removed the decaying `+50` `magic_opinion_positive`).
- **Removed Anoint Heir**:
  - Deleted `blessing_anoint_heir`, `bm_anointed_heir_modifier`, `bm_transfer_anointed_heir_legacy_effect`, `bm_on_character_death_dynasty`, and all related script values, icons, and localization.
- **Character Interaction XP Equalisation**: Sealed the `ancient` XP leak and balanced track distribution.
  - **Ancient Blood Blessing (`blessing_ancient_*`)**: Now grants `+2/+4/+6` Wisdom XP so Blood Distillation remains the sole interaction granting Ancient XP.
  - **Track reassignments**:
    - **Drain Blood Memory**: `+4` Wisdom.
    - **Bleed Own Veins**: `+2/+4` Fury.
    - **Cull Champion**: `+4` Fury only.
    - **Contest of the Red Art**: Split `+3/+3` (crit) and `+2/+2` (win) Fury + Wisdom (`+1` Wisdom on defeat).
    - **Adopt into Dynasty**: `+4` Fortune.
  - **Standardized payouts**:
    - **Grant Lifeforce**: `+2/+4/+6` Radiance (`+4` Fortune for Ancient Blood).
    - **Inscribe Blood Rune (Other)**: `+2/+4/+6` Radiance.
    - **Minor Life drain (Empowered)**: `+2` Cunning.
    - **Demand Blood Tithe**: `+3` Fortune.
    - **Bestow Blood Traits**: `+4` Fortune.
    - **Influence Personality**: `+5` Wisdom on win.
