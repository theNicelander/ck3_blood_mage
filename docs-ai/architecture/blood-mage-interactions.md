## Key Mechanics & AI Logic

- **Current Situation Alerts (`bm_action_can_heal`, `bm_action_can_lifedrain_prisoner`, `bm_action_can_blood_duel_prisoner`, `bm_action_can_attune_lifeforce`, `bm_action_can_trait_drain_prisoner`, `bm_action_can_ascend_tier`, `bm_action_can_carve_galdrastafir`):** Defined in `common/important_actions/bm_important_actions.txt`. All Blood Mage alerts use `@bm_ancient_discipline_icon!`.
  - `bm_action_can_ascend_tier`: Surfaces whenever `can_execute_decision = bm_elevate_blood_mage_tier_decision`, opening the Ascend Blood Magic Tier decision view.
  - `bm_action_can_carve_galdrastafir`: Surfaces for Archmages holding `superior_blood_rune_modifier` with no stave carved (`bm_has_any_stave_trigger = no`), opening the Carve Galdrastafir Stave decision view.
  - `bm_action_can_heal`: Surfaces whenever an eligible priority target (self, spouse, children, close family, lovers, friends, liege, councillors, court position holders, or knights) suffers from an ailment curable by the player. Formatted dynamically like vanilla declare war: marked green (`Status_Suggestion_Good` with `#P @health_icon! Can Heal#!`) when the player has the corresponding Lifeforce tier, and gray (`Status_Suggestion` with `#low @health_icon! Needs Lifeforce#!` under `unimportant`) when lacking Lifeforce. Clicking directly launches `bm_heal_ailment`.
  - `bm_action_can_lifedrain_prisoner`: Surfaces whenever the player has eligible dungeon prisoners and sufficient Piety to lifedrain them (Major Lifedrain, 50 Piety; Minor Lifedrain, 25 Piety, respects 3-month cooldown).
  - `bm_action_can_blood_duel_prisoner`: Surfaces whenever the player has 50 Piety, can fight single combat, and holds an eligible `20+` Prowess prisoner, clicking directly into `bm_blood_drain_interaction`.
  - `bm_action_can_trait_drain_prisoner`: Surfaces for any player Blood Mage (including Tier 1 Acolytes) whenever any prisoner has a drainable positive congenital trait (`bm_can_drain_traits_from_target_trigger`), marked `unimportant` (gray) when the player cannot currently drain that target (missing Major Lifeforce or active caster/victim cooldowns). Clicking opens `bm_blood_drain_interaction`.
  - **All-Tier Trait Draining:** All Blood Mages (including Tier 1 Acolytes) can drain positive congenital traits up to rank 5 (`bm_can_drain_leveled_trait_from_target_trigger`) and special/physical traits (`fecund`, `giant`, `pure_blooded`, `strong`, `shrewd`) in `bm_trait_drain.001` ("The Essence Harvest").
  - **Built-in Overhaul & Modded Trait/Memory Draining (`bm_compat_*`):** Isolated per-overhaul modules gated by Day-1 `on_game_start` global variables (`bm_is_agot_loaded_trigger`, `bm_is_lotr_loaded_trigger`, `bm_is_ek2_loaded_trigger`) plug into `bm_can_drain_traits_from_target_trigger`, `bm_compat_open_trait_drain_event_effect`, `bm_can_drain_memory_from_target_trigger`, and `bm_compat_open_memory_drain_event_effect`:
    - **AGOT:** Trait Drain (`dragon_dreams`, `greensight`, **Dragonblood** via `historical_dragonseed`); Blood Memory (`lifestyle_mariner`, `fire_obsessed`).
    - **LotR: Realms in Exile:** Trait Drain (`blood_of_numenor` 1-10, `skinchanger`, `sorcerer` 1-3, `twotreeslight`); Blood Memory (`lifestyle_mariner`, `lifestyle_archivist`, `lifestyle_miner`, `lifestyle_artifact_crafting`, `artificer`, `enchanter`, `excavator`, `cavern_commander`, `cavalry_leader`, `banner_orc`, `whip_master`, `explosive_engineer`).
    - **Elder Kings 2:** Trait Drain (`arcana_good` 1-3, `magickal`, `lifespan` 1-4, `dragonborn`, `vampire_character`, `lycan_character`, `necromancer`, `lich_character`, `briarheart`, `hagraven`, `tsaesci` 1-4, and Hist Blessings `argonian_behemoth`/`ghost_scale`/`amber_blessed`/`sun_blessed` 1-3 in `.0002`); Blood Memory (`battlemage`, `plunderer`, `crimeboss`).
    - **Vanilla & Workshop Blood Memory:** Added vanilla `tourney_participant` (4-track Hastiluder) and `varangian`, plus an isolated Blood Memory module (`bm_compat_memory_drain`) for **Commander+ 1.21** (29 commander traits).
- **AI Hostile Magic Vetoes:** `factor = 0` blocks AI mages from using hostile magic (`lifedrain_prisoner`, `lifedrain_courtier`, `trait_drain_prisoner`, `curse_blood`, `bm_get_blood_magic_from_prisoner`) against spouses/consorts, children, primary heirs, close family, councillors, knights, employed court positions, and Blood Golems under any circumstances (with hard interaction validity blocks against spouses, children, knights, councillors, court positions, and golems). Placed at the bottom of `ai_will_do` to guarantee additive modifiers cannot resurrect score. Furthermore, liege, dynasty members, lovers/soulmates/wards, and characters with positive opinion are vetoed unless they are a rival or nemesis. AI House Heads using Demand Blood Tithe (`drain_tithe`) can target disliked (opinion <= 0) adult house members, but strict protective vetoes on spouses, children, heirs, close family, councillors, knights, and court positions remain absolute.
- **AI Positive Magic Prioritisation & Rival Veto:** `factor = 0` strictly vetoes performing positive magic (`bm_heal_ailment`, `bm_cast_blood_blessing_interaction`, `grant_blood_magic_interaction`, `grant_lifeforce_interaction*`, `bm_purge_coping_traits_interaction`) on rivals and nemeses, placed at the bottom of evaluation blocks to prevent score resurrection. In `ai_willingness_to_do_positive_magic`, spouses/consorts grant `+200` and primary heirs grant `+150`.
- **Aggressive Spouse & Heir Initiation Priority:** AI Blood Mages (`grant_blood_magic_interaction`) aggressively prioritise initiating uninitiated spouses (`+1000`), consorts (`+500`), and primary heirs (`+800`) over general children (`+100`), sworn knights (`+35`), councillors (`+25`), or dynasty members (`+25`).
- **Modal Interaction Execution Safety (`send_options_exclusive = yes`):** CK3 AI evaluates combinations of `send_option` flags including the empty set `{}`. Every modal interaction (`grant_blood_magic`, `bm_blood_drain`, `bm_cast_blood_blessing`, `bm_empower_bloodline`, `bm_manifest_lifeforce`, `bm_bleed_own_vein`, `bm_channel_focus_interaction`) sets `can_invalidate_interaction = yes` on every option, scores each valid option with explicit additive weights in `ai_will_do`, and enforces an absolute `factor = 0` veto when no option flag is selected (`NOR = { scope:opt1 = yes scope:opt2 = yes ... } factor = 0`). This prevents the AI from firing interactions with empty payloads.
- **Story Panel Sync:** Granting warrior/champion status automatically updates the Blood Retinue in the Blood Magic panel.
- **Debug Logging Standard & Relationship Hierarchy:** Character interactions, decisions, and executions log to `logs/debug.log` using standardized prefixes with full metadata:
  - `BloodMageInteraction: <name> (<option>) | Actor: <name> (ID:<id>, <Player|AI>) -> Recipient: <name> (ID:<id>, <Player|AI>) | Rel: <relation> | Tag: <role_tag> | Opinion: <opinion>`
  - `BloodMageSelfCast: <name> (<option>) | Actor: <name> (ID:<id>, <Player|AI>)`
  - `BloodMageDecision: <name> (<item>) | Actor: <name> (ID:<id>, <Player|AI>)`
  - `BloodMageExecution: lifedrain | Executioner: <name> ... -> Victim: <name> ...`
  The relationship tag custom loc (`bm_debug_relation_tags`) evaluates roles with protected identities first: `Self` > `Spouse/Consort` > `Primary Heir` > `Child` > `Close Family` > specific councillors (`Chancellor`, `Marshal`, `Steward`, `Spymaster`, `Court Chaplain`, `Councillor`) > `Court Position` > `Golem` > `Knight` > `Lover/Soulmate` > `Friend` > `Rival/Nemesis` > `Liege` > `Prisoner` > `Dynasty` > `Vassal` > `Courtier` > `Other`. This guarantees no critical relationship is masked in audit logs.
