## Key Mechanics & AI Logic

- **Current Situation Healing Alert (`bm_action_can_heal`):** Defined in `common/important_actions/bm_important_actions.txt`. Surfaces a top-bar Current Situation notification whenever the player (Blood Mage or Blood Knight) has sufficient Piety and Lifeforce to heal an afflicted priority target (self, close family, spouse, lovers, friends, liege, councillors, or knights), clicking directly into `bm_heal_ailment`.
- **AI Hostile Magic Vetoes:** `factor = 0` blocks AI mages and knights from using hostile magic (`lifedrain_prisoner`, `lifedrain_courtier`, `trait_drain_prisoner`, `curse_blood`, `bm_get_blood_magic_from_prisoner`, `bm_cull_champion_interaction`) against spouses/consorts, children, close family, councillors, knights, employed court positions, and Blood Golems under any circumstances (with hard interaction validity blocks against spouses, children, knights, councillors, court positions, and golems). Placed at the bottom of `ai_will_do` to guarantee additive modifiers cannot resurrect score. Furthermore, liege, dynasty members, lovers/soulmates/wards, and characters with positive opinion are vetoed unless they are a rival or nemesis.
- **AI Positive Magic Prioritisation & Rival Veto:** `factor = 0` strictly vetoes performing positive magic (`bm_heal_ailment`, `bm_heal_old_age_trait`, `bless_bloodline`, `bm_anoint_heir_interaction`, `make_blood_knight`, `empower_blood_knight`, `grant_blood_magic`, `grant_lifeforce_reversed`, `bm_inscribe_rune_other_interaction`, `bm_purge_coping_traits_interaction`, `bm_anoint_knight_interaction`) on rivals and nemeses, placed at the bottom of evaluation blocks to prevent score resurrection. In `ai_willingness_to_do_positive_magic`, spouses/consorts grant `+200` and primary heirs grant `+150`.
- **Aggressive Spouse & Heir Initiation Priority:** AI Blood Mages (`grant_blood_magic_interaction`) and Blood Knights (`make_blood_knight_interaction`) aggressively prioritise initiating uninitiated spouses (`+1000`), consorts (`+500`), and primary heirs (`+800`) over general children (`+100`), sworn knights (`+50`), councillors (`+25`), or dynasty members (`+25`). Checked at an accelerated 3-month frequency (`ai_frequency = 3`) and expanded to include landed heirs via `ai_recipients = vassals`. Generic self-cast decisions (`bm_cast_blood_magic`) apply `-80` AI weight when an uninitiated spouse or primary heir is awaiting initiation, preserving Lifeforce for family transmission.
- **Class Parity in Initiation:** Knights create Knights via `make_blood_knight_interaction`; Mages create Mages via `grant_blood_magic_interaction`. Costs are symmetric: Major Lifeforce + 100 Piety OR Minor Lifeforce + 250 Piety.
- **Story Panel Sync:** Granting warrior/champion status automatically updates the Blood Retinue in the Blood Magic panel.
- **Debug Logging Standard:** Character interactions log to `logs/debug.log` using standardized prefixes:
  - `BloodMageInteraction: <name> | Actor: <name> (ID:<id>, <Player|AI>) -> Recipient: <name> (ID:<id>, <Player|AI>) | Rel: <relation> | Tag: <role_tag> | Opinion: <opinion>`
  - `BloodMageSelfCast: <name> | Actor: <name> (ID:<id>, <Player|AI>)`
  - `BloodMageExecution: lifedrain | Executioner: <name> ... -> Victim: <name> ...`
