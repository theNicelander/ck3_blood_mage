# CK3 1.21 New Modifiers, Traits & Mechanics - Blood Mage Integration Ideas

Catalog of new modifier definitions, traits, and economic/district hooks introduced in **CK3 1.21** (`937` new modifier definitions across `00_definitions.txt`, `00_gdp_definitions.txt`, `00_trade_definitions.txt`, `00_unit_definitions.txt`, `00_district_definitions.txt`, and `00_terrain_definitions.txt`) that fit Blood Mages thematically and mechanically.

---

## 1. Devotion-Scaled Temple & Tribal Holding Tax (`*_tax_per_piety_level`)

In CK3 1.20, `tax_per_piety_level` and `monthly_income_per_piety_level` were split and replaced in 1.21 by category-specific holding/county/domicile tax scaling modifiers:

| Modifier | Scope | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `religious_tax_per_piety_level` | County / Province (`duchy_capital_county_modifier`, `province_modifier`) | Scales religious tax in the holding/county by the holder's Level of Devotion (`piety_level`, up to Level 8 in Blood Mages). Used in vanilla 1.21 ecclesiastical domicile buildings, mandala shrines, and sacred county modifiers. |
| `tribal_tax_per_piety_level` | County / Province | Scales tribal tax by holder's Level of Devotion. |
| `generic_tax_per_piety_level` | County / Province | Scales base holding tax by holder's Level of Devotion. |
| `religious_tax_mult` / `monthly_religious_tax` | County / Province | Direct % multiplier or flat gold add to religious tax revenue. |

### Where to Add
- **Duchy Blood Temples (`bm_blood_temple_01..07` in `common/buildings/bm_duchy_buildings.txt`):**
  - Currently grant Piety, Piety/Knight, Health, Life Expectancy, Prosperity Growth, and Control Growth, but **0 Gold/Tax**.
  - Adding `religious_tax_per_piety_level = 0.02` → `0.10` (and `monthly_religious_tax` on Tiers V–VII) inside `duchy_capital_county_modifier` or `province_modifier` turns Blood Temples into lucrative sacrificial tithing centers that scale up to 8× with the mod's 8-tier Level of Devotion cap.
- **Domicile Blood Shrines (`bm_blood_shrine_01..05` in `common/domiciles/buildings/bm_domicile_buildings.txt`):**
  - Can use `province_modifier = { religious_tax_per_piety_level = 0.02 }` (matching vanilla `11_ecclesiastical_domicile_buildings.txt`) so landed estate owners extract devotion-scaled temple tithes where their estate rests.

## 2. Character-Scoped Capital County & Faith Tax Modifiers

CK3 1.21 introduced character-scoped modifiers that directly buff the ruler's capital county or faith-aligned domain taxes without needing county-scoped scripts:

| Modifier | Scope | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `character_capital_county_monthly_prosperity_growth_factor` | Character | `%` multiplier to monthly prosperity growth in the character's capital county (complements `_add`). |
| `character_capital_county_control_baseline_add` | Character | Raises the minimum/resting Control baseline in the capital county (used in vanilla 1.21 Authority perks & Republic laws). |
| `character_capital_popular_opinion_add` | Character | Flat Popular Opinion bonus in the capital county. |
| `character_capital_province_size_baseline_add` | Character | Raises the baseline Settlement Size of the capital province, unlocking more district capacity. |
| `domain_tax_same_religion_mult` / `domain_tax_different_religion_mult` | Character | Multiplies tax from domain holdings of the same or different religion. |
| `baron_piety_contribution_add` / `baron_piety_contribution_mult` | Character | Extracts monthly Piety directly from sub-realm barons (temple/city/castle baronies). |

### Where to Add
- **Fortune Track (`lifestyle_blood_mage` / `fortune`), `Fortunate Blood` (`lifeforce_modifier_fortunate`), or `Covenant of Fortune`:**
  - `character_capital_county_monthly_prosperity_growth_factor = 0.05` / `0.10` and `character_capital_province_size_baseline_add` let tall Blood Mage capitals (like Reykjavik or Tsushima) bloom into sprawling metropolises.
- **Cunning Track / Ægishjálmur Stave (`bm_rune_stave_aegishjalmur`):**
  - `character_capital_county_control_baseline_add = 10` ensures the Blood Mage's seat of power never falls into lawlessness even during wars or drains.
- **Blóðtrú Identity Doctrine (`bm_blodtru_identity_doctrine`) or Blood Temple Holder Modifiers:**
  - `baron_piety_contribution_mult = 0.10` / `0.25` rewards rulers who pack their domain counties with sub-baronies and temple holdings.

## 3. Sworn Knight Trade & Military Modifiers (`00_trade_definitions.txt` & `00_unit_definitions.txt`)

CK3 1.21 expanded knight scaling and Men-at-Arms battlefield sustain:

| Modifier | Scope | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `trade_competitiveness_per_knight_add` / `trade_competitiveness_per_knight_mult` | Character / Domicile / Building | Scales trade competitiveness by the ruler's number of sworn Knights (`+0.5%` to `+1%` per knight in vanilla 1.21). |
| `maa_reinforcement_rate` / `stationed_maa_reinforcement_rate` | Character / Province | Accelerates how rapidly Men-at-Arms (such as Blood Berserkers and Blood-Bound Behemoths) replenish casualties. |
| `maa_control_value_add` / `maa_control_value_mult` | Character / Province | Increases county control suppression power of stationed Men-at-Arms. |
| `mercenary_hire_limit` | Character | Increases maximum concurrent mercenary companies a ruler can contract. |
| `county_movement_speed` / `province_movement_speed` | County / Province | Boosts army movement speed across owned/sacred territory. |

### Where to Add
- **Furious Blood (`lifeforce_modifier_furious`) & Covenant of Fury (`dynasty_blood_fury_modifier_1..3`):**
  - `maa_reinforcement_rate = 0.05` per stack (`+10% / +20% / +30%` on Covenant of Fury): supernaturally rapid regeneration of fallen regiments via blood magic.
- **Nábrókarstafur Stave (`bm_rune_stave_nabrokarstafur`) & Verbúðir Tradition (`bm_tradition_verbudir`):**
  - `trade_competitiveness_per_knight_mult = 0.005` or `trade_competitiveness_mult = 0.10` pairs Blood Mages' high knight limits (`fury` track + Covenant of Fury) with 1.21's trade economy.
  - `max_trade_routes = 1` on Capstone **Nábrókarstafur** (the macabre Icelandic wealth stave), making it a true economic powerhouse in 1.21 alongside its flat gold generation.

## 4. Administrative & Celestial Governor Efficiency (`governor_efficiency_per_*`)

CK3 1.21 added dynamic attribute-to-governor-efficiency scaling modifiers on characters:

| Modifier | Scope | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `governor_efficiency_add` / `governor_efficiency_mult` | Character | Flat or `%` bonus to Administrative/Celestial Governor Efficiency (vanilla 4★/5★ educations now grant `+0.02` / `+0.04`). |
| `governor_efficiency_per_prowess_add` | Character | Scales Governor Efficiency with Prowess (`+0.005` per Prowess in vanilla). |
| `governor_efficiency_per_martial_add` / `_diplomacy_add` / `_stewardship_add` | Character | Scales Governor Efficiency with core attributes. |
| `governor_efficiency_per_prestige_level_add` | Character | Scales Governor Efficiency with Level of Fame. |
| `realm_governor_efficiency_per_legitimacy_level_add` | Character | Scales realm-wide Governor Efficiency across all vassal governors by the liege's Legitimacy level! |

### Where to Add
- **Blood Mage & Blood Archmage Base Traits (`lifestyle_blood_mage`, `lifestyle_bm_archmage`):**
  - Vanilla 1.21 added `governor_efficiency_add = 0.02` (4★) and `0.04` (5★) to all education traits. Adding `governor_efficiency_add = 0.05` (Mage) / `0.10` (Archmage) or `governor_efficiency_per_prowess_add = 0.002` makes Blood Mages and Blood Golems exceptional governors in Administrative (Byzantine) and Celestial (Xuédào / China) empires.
- **Covenant of Radiance Tier III (`dynasty_blood_radiance_modifier_3`):**
  - `realm_governor_efficiency_per_legitimacy_level_add = 0.02` rewards imperial Blood Mage dynasties ruling sprawling Administrative or Celestial realms.

## 5. New 1.21 Traits, Education & Commander Tracks

CK3 1.21 added several traits and track XP modifiers that connect directly to Blood Mage subsystems:

1. **5★ Republican Knowledge Education (`education_republican_knowledge_5`):**
   - Previously, `education_republican_knowledge` capped at Rank 4 (`education_republican_knowledge_4`). CK3 1.21 adds a native 5★ tier (`education_republican_knowledge_5`) granting `+10` Stewardship, `+10` Learning, `+10` Intrigue, `+0.04` Governor Efficiency, `+15%` Trade Expertise growth, and `+25%` Artisan track XP.
   - **Idea:** Allow **Cast Superior Blood Magic -> 5★ Master Education** or **Embrace Secondary Education** to upgrade `education_republican_knowledge_4` → `education_republican_knowledge_5` if the character already holds a metropolitan/republican education trait.
2. **Merchant Lifestyle Trait (`lifestyle_merchant` with `caravaneer`, `artisan`, `creditor` tracks):**
   - New vanilla 3-track lifestyle trait (`caravaneer` boosts travel speed & trade route length; `artisan` boosts `+5%` to `+10%` monthly lifestyle XP & domicile prestige; `creditor` reduces dread loss `-5%` and adds `+2.5` to `+5` scheme secrecy).
   - **Idea:** Allow **Trait Drain Prisoner** or a **Nábrókarstafur** ritual to siphon merchant acumen or grant `trait_track_artisan_xp_gain_mult` / `trait_track_creditor_xp_gain_mult`.
3. **19 Commander Trait Track XP Multipliers (`trait_track_<commander_trait>_xp_gain_mult`):**
   - In 1.21, all 19 commander traits (`reaver`, `holy_warrior`, `aggressive_attacker`, `unyielding_defender`, `reckless`, `military_engineer`, `logistician`, `organizer`, `winter_soldier`, etc.) now have 3-step XP progression tracks (33 / 66 / 100 XP) and dedicated XP gain multipliers (`trait_track_reaver_xp_gain_mult`, `trait_track_holy_warrior_xp_gain_mult`, etc.).
   - **Idea:**
     - **Furious Blood (`lifeforce_modifier_furious`)** or **Fury Track**: Add `trait_track_reaver_xp_gain_mult = 0.10`, `trait_track_aggressive_attacker_xp_gain_mult = 0.10`, and `trait_track_reckless_xp_gain_mult = 0.10`.
     - **Blood Sparring Ring / Battle Victories**: Award commander trait track XP alongside `fury` and `lifestyle_blademaster` XP when a Blood Mage commands battles.

## 6. Occult & Ritual Trade Goods (`00_gdp_definitions.txt`)

CK3 1.21's trade system introduces specific trade goods and categories with production, buy/sell price, and expertise modifiers:
- **Thematic Goods:** `trade_good_incense_*`, `trade_good_wine_*`, `trade_good_amber_*`, `trade_good_silver_*`, `trade_good_gold_*`, `trade_good_books_paper_*`, and `trade_category_medicaments_*` (apothecaries, herbs, healing tonics).
- **Where to Add:**
  - **Blood Temples (`bm_blood_temple_01..07`):** Just as vanilla 1.21 Eminent Holy Sites boost `trade_good_incense_sell_price_mult` and `trade_good_books_paper_sell_price_mult`, high-tier Blood Temples (`bm_blood_temple_05..07`) or Blood Temple Districts could boost local `trade_category_medicaments_production_mult` or `trade_good_incense_production_add`, turning Blood Temple capitals into centers of occult alchemy and apothecary trade.
  - **Blood Temple District (`bm_blood_temple_district`):** In 1.21, duchy capital districts can optionally list secondary `buildings = { ... }` (such as vanilla `charnel_grounds_01`, `burial_site_01`, `great_megalith_01`, `pilgrimage_office_01`, `almonry_general_01` from `endowment_district`), letting players construct macabre secondary buildings inside their Blood Temple District if desired.
